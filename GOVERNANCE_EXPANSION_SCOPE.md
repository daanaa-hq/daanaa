## EXPANSION AUDIT DOMAINS (4 Priority Areas)

### DOMAIN 1: FUNDING & SPENDING AUTHORITY

**Currently Undefined:**
- Who can authorize spending?
- What are spending limits (by role, by category, by amount)?
- What requires founder approval vs. autonomous?
- How is budget allocated across projects?
- What happens if spending exceeds planned budget?
- Who audits spending? How often?
- Are there restricted categories (no weapons, no political, etc.)?

**Risks if Undefined:**
- Spending creep (unauthorized budget growth)
- Vendor lock-in (no oversight of recurring costs)
- Misalignment with Stewardship (e.g., spending on tracking tech violates P2)
- No financial accountability to stakeholders
- Founder becomes bottleneck for every payment

**Example Tensions:**
- P1 (Mission first) vs. spending for feature engineering (efficiency cost)
- P8 (Never handle funds) vs. need for operational budget
- Transparency vs. commercial confidentiality (vendor contracts)

---

### DOMAIN 2: DECISION-MAKING PROCESSES

**Currently Undefined:**
- What decisions are autonomous (engineering can do immediately)?
- What decisions are gated (need approval)?
- Who has approval authority for what?
- What's the escalation path if decision is unclear?
- How long do approvals take (SLA)?
- Can decisions be reversed? When? By whom?
- Are there irreversible decisions (that can't be undone)?

**Risks if Undefined:**
- Paralysis (everything escalates to founder)
- Maverick decisions (going rogue without approval)
- Inconsistent standards (same decision handled differently)
- Slow velocity (waiting on approvals)
- No way to resolve disagreements

**Example Tensions:**
- Speed (ship fast) vs. Principles (verify first)
- Autonomy (empower teams) vs. Control (consistency)
- Experimentation (try things) vs. Irreversibility (can't undo)

---

### DOMAIN 3: PUBLIC COMMITMENTS & ACCOUNTABILITY

**Currently Undefined:**
- What public promises have we made (on charter, SLA, performance)?
- How do we measure if we're keeping those promises?
- What happens if we fail to meet a commitment?
- How do we communicate failures transparently?
- Who is accountable if a commitment is broken?
- Are there exemptions or force majeure clauses?
- How do we update commitments if circumstances change?

**Risks if Undefined:**
- Stewardship principles become marketing language (not lived)
- No way to hold ourselves accountable
- Users/orgs don't know what to expect
- Reputational damage when commitments aren't met
- No mechanism to correct course

**Example Tensions:**
- P3 (Evidence-based) vs. P5 (Don't weaponize) — how do we report failures?
- Transparency vs. Competitive disadvantage (revealing problems)
- Perfection vs. Reality (we will make mistakes)

---

### DOMAIN 4: RISK MANAGEMENT & MITIGATION

**Currently Undefined:**
- What are our top systemic risks (not just individual issues)?
- For each risk, what's the likelihood and impact?
- What controls do we have to mitigate each risk?
- What's our risk appetite (what risks are acceptable)?
- How do we monitor risks? (dashboards, metrics, audits)
- What's the escalation path when a risk materializes?
- How do we learn from incidents?

**Risks if Undefined:**
- Blind spots (we don't see coming problems)
- Over-reaction to small issues (no risk prioritization)
- Under-reaction to big risks (no early warning system)
- Repeated mistakes (no incident learning)
- Vendor/partner risk ignored (concentration risk)
- Data/privacy risks not mitigated (P2 violation)

**Example Categories:**
- **Operational:** Systems down, data lost, code broken
- **Governance:** Founder unavailable, decisions deadlocked, principles violated
- **Financial:** Budget overrun, vendor lock-in, cost explosion
- **Privacy/Security:** Data breach, unauthorized access, compliance violation
- **Reputational:** Public criticism, trust damage, loss of credibility
- **Legal/Compliance:** Regulatory change, liability, lawsuit
- **Strategic:** Mission drift, partnership failure, market change

## AUDIT METHODOLOGY

For each domain, we will:

1. **Current State Assessment**
   - What policies/processes exist today?
   - What's documented vs. implicit?
   - Where are the gaps?

2. **Risk Analysis**
   - What happens if this domain is undefined?
   - What are the worst-case scenarios?
   - How likely are they?

3. **Stewardship Alignment Check**
   - Does current state align with 11 principles?
   - Are there conflicts or trade-offs?
   - What changes would improve alignment?

4. **Best Practices Research**
   - How do other nonprofits handle this?
   - What frameworks exist?
   - What can we adapt?

5. **Recommended Framework**
   - Clear policies and decision rules
   - Roles and responsibilities
   - Escalation paths and SLAs
   - Monitoring and adjustment mechanisms

6. **Implementation Plan**
   - What needs to be documented?
   - What needs to be communicated?
   - What needs to be changed?
   - Timeline and effort estimate

## WORKING QUESTIONS

### Funding & Spending
1. Who approved the Ryzen 9700X server purchase? (founder, budget, process?)
2. How are S3 costs tracked and authorized? (who approves recurring bills?)
3. If a tool costs $5K/month, who can say yes? (what's the threshold?)
4. What happens if we need emergency $$ (e.g., legal defense)?
5. Are there categories we won't spend on? (e.g., tracking tech, political ads?)

### Decision-Making
1. Can engineering ship code without approval? (current CLAUDE.md says yes for reversible changes)
2. Can AI agents make autonomous decisions? (e.g., auto-deploying precompute)
3. What's the escalation path if two decisions conflict? (e.g., "ship fast" vs. "verify")
4. Who decides if a decision is reversible or not?
5. Are there decisions that ONLY the founder can make?

### Public Commitments
1. What SLAs have we published? (uptime, performance, response time)
2. What privacy guarantees have we made? (and are they being kept?)
3. What do we promise about data retention? (and for how long?)
4. What's our policy if we have a data breach? (who do we notify, how fast?)
5. Are there features we've promised but not shipped? (and why not?)

### Risk Management
1. If the Ryzen server fails, what's the impact? (data loss? downtime? recovery time?)
2. If founder is gone for 6 months, can the org continue operating?
3. If a vendor relationship breaks, are we stuck? (lock-in risk?)
4. If a regulatory change happens, what's our compliance burden?
5. If a security vulnerability is found, what's our response process?

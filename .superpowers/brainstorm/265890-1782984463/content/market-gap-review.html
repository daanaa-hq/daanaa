Where Daanaa can be genuinely different

The opportunity is a simple connection between what a nonprofit says it needs now and a person's private intention to help again.

Recommended Daanaa loop
1. Find
Discover a nonprofit by cause or place
2. See what would help
Only needs confirmed by the nonprofit
3. Connect directly
Use the nonprofit's own website or contact path
4. Remember privately
Save it and choose when to help again
Unique feature 1

What would help right now

A claimed nonprofit can publish a few current, time-limited needs:

  • Funds for a stated purpose
  • Volunteer time
  • Help with a specific task
  • Goods or reduced-cost services

No applicant profiles, messaging system, popularity count, or paid placement.

Unique feature 2

My giving plan

A private place to save organizations and choose a gentle reminder:

  • Help again next month or next year
  • Remember funds, time, or another contribution
  • Keep notes privately
  • No streaks, badges, rankings, or public feed

Daanaa remembers the intention; the nonprofit receives support directly.

Features that already have strong providers

Do not rebuild

  • 990 directory and filing search
  • Charity ratings and comparative rankings
  • Donation processing and tax receipts
  • Volunteer or professional matching
  • Professional profiles and messaging
  • Large national group-purchasing catalog

Keep as supporting infrastructure

  • Public records for identity and context
  • Direct links to nonprofit-controlled destinations
  • Links to specialist volunteer services when useful
  • Transparent local or specialized discounts
  • Clear separation between public and nonprofit-provided information

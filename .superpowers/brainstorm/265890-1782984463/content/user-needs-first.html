User needs come first

Daanaa's first job is to help a person find an organization, understand the available information, and take a useful next step without pressure.

Primary public journey
1. Find
Search by name, cause, or place
2. Understand
Read its purpose, source, and current information
3. Help
Visit its website or view a confirmed opportunity
4. Return
Save privately and remember when ready

People who want to help

Find trustworthy starting information and clear direct paths for funds, time, or a specific requested need.

Nonprofit teams

Find their page, correct it, add their own words and official links, and state what support would be useful.

People researching nonprofits

See sources, dates, limitations, and methodology without marketing claims or unexplained scores.

Businesses offering support

Use a separate area for nonprofit discounts and services. They never influence nonprofit visibility.

Language and storytelling rules

  • Use direct actions and familiar words; explain necessary technical terms.
  • Say “people who want to help” when funds, time, and skills are all included.
  • Describe organizations with dignity and agency, never as helpless or as marketing objects.
  • Do not use rankings, popularity, guilt, urgency, streaks, or public recognition to pressure action.
  • Separate public records, nonprofit-provided information, and AI-assisted summaries.
  • Every page ends with a useful, honest next step.

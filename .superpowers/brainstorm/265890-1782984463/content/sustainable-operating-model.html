Recommended: institution-funded infrastructure

Annual access for a configured local directory, data monitoring, and aggregate reporting. Public and nonprofit access remains free.

Not recommended: charge nonprofits

Recurring revenue is possible, but it disadvantages the smaller organizations Daanaa exists to help and creates support expectations.

Reject: transaction or marketplace fees

Donation percentages, volunteer matching, revenue share, and custom discount negotiations increase staffing, compliance, and mission conflict.

What drives traffic and giving
  • Search pages bring first-time visitors
  • Institutions link to their local directory
  • Nonprofits share their claimed page and current need
  • Private reminders bring people back
  • Useful direct links turn visits into action
What keeps staffing low
  • No payment processing
  • No user-to-user messaging or manual matching
  • No custom software for each institution
  • No revenue-share reconciliation
  • Needs and offers expire automatically
  • Manual review only for exceptions and reports

Revenue sequence

  1. First: a small number of annual institutional pilots using one standard configuration.
  2. Second: paid data monitoring or reports built only from data Daanaa may commercially use.
  3. Later: fixed annual vendor fees after claimed nonprofits and measurable usage exist. No commission and no paid placement.

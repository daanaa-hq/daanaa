## ARTICLE I — ORGANIZATION

**1.1 Formation**
EcoMargins Consulting LLC (the "Company") was organized as a Texas limited liability company on December 28, 2025, pursuant to the Texas Business Organizations Code (the "TBOC"), by filing a Certificate of Formation with the Texas Secretary of State.

**1.2 Name**
The Company shall conduct business under the name "EcoMargins Consulting LLC" and under the following registered assumed names (DBAs) filed with Harris County, Texas:

| DBA Name | Business Purpose | Filing Status |
|----------|-----------------|---------------|
| Daanaa | Civic nonprofit-discovery platform; donor-facing directory of IRS-registered 501(c)(3) organizations | Pending |
| EcoMargins | ESG/CSR consulting and SaaS services for for-profit and public-sector organizations | Pending |
| ZDPark | Music, songs, and related creative works | Pending |
| Surfce | Future technology project (purpose to be defined) | Pending |

**1.3 Registered Agent and Principal Office**
The Company's principal place of business is:

> 5847 Petty St
> Houston, Texas 77007

The registered agent shall be the sole Member, unless a third-party agent is designated.

**1.4 Term**
The Company shall continue perpetually unless dissolved pursuant to Article VIII of this Agreement or as required by applicable law.

**1.5 Purpose**
The Company is organized for any lawful purpose permitted under the TBOC, including but not limited to: technology development, software-as-a-service, consulting, civic technology, data services, creative and media projects, and any activities conducted under the registered DBA names listed in Section 1.2.

**2.2 Membership Interest**
The sole Member holds one hundred percent (100%) of the membership interests of the Company. No other person or entity holds any membership interest unless admitted pursuant to Article VII.

**2.3 No Certificates**
Membership interests shall not be represented by certificates unless the Member later elects otherwise.

---

## ARTICLE III — CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

**3.1 Initial Contribution**
The Member has contributed capital to the Company in the form of cash, equipment, intellectual property, and labor, as recorded in the Company's books.

**3.2 Additional Contributions**
The Member may, but is not required to, make additional capital contributions at any time. No interest shall accrue on capital contributions.

**3.3 No Third-Party Obligation**
Nothing in this Agreement shall create any obligation of the Member to any third party with respect to capital contributions.

---

## ARTICLE IV — MANAGEMENT

**4.1 Member-Managed**
The Company shall be managed by its Member. As sole Member, Akbar Khowaja shall have full authority to:

- Enter into contracts and agreements on behalf of the Company
- Open and manage bank accounts and financial accounts
- Hire, compensate, and terminate employees and contractors
- Incur debts and obligations
- Acquire, hold, and dispose of Company assets
- File assumed name (DBA) certificates
- Make all decisions relating to each DBA business line

**4.2 DBA Operations**
Each DBA listed in Section 1.2 operates as a division of the Company, not as a separate legal entity. All contracts, liabilities, revenues, and obligations of each DBA are those of EcoMargins Consulting LLC.

**4.3 Bank Accounts**
The Company may maintain separate bank accounts or sub-accounts for each DBA for bookkeeping clarity. All such accounts are assets of EcoMargins Consulting LLC.

**4.4 Officers**
The Member may appoint himself or others to officer roles (Chief Executive Officer, President, etc.) without creating a separate management structure. The Member may act as:

- Founder & CEO — EcoMargins Consulting LLC
- Founder & CEO — Daanaa

---

## ARTICLE V — ALLOCATIONS AND DISTRIBUTIONS

**5.1 Allocations**
All profits, losses, and items of income, gain, deduction, and credit shall be allocated one hundred percent (100%) to the Member.

**5.2 Distributions**
The Member may take distributions of Company funds at any time, in any amount, subject to:

- The Company maintaining sufficient funds to meet its obligations
- Applicable law under the TBOC (distributions may not be made if they would render the Company insolvent)

**5.3 Tax Treatment**
The Company shall be treated as a disregarded entity for federal and Texas state income tax purposes (sole proprietorship taxation). The Member shall report all Company income and loss on Schedule C of his individual federal income tax return, unless the Company later elects to be taxed as an S-Corporation or C-Corporation.

---

## ARTICLE VI — BOOKS, RECORDS, AND FISCAL YEAR

**6.1 Books and Records**
The Company shall maintain complete and accurate books and records of its business and financial condition. Records shall be kept at the principal office or such other location as the Member determines.

**6.2 Fiscal Year**
The Company's fiscal year shall be the calendar year (January 1 – December 31).

**6.3 Accounting Method**
The Company shall use the cash method of accounting, unless otherwise determined by the Member.

**6.4 DBA Bookkeeping**
The Company shall maintain separate internal accounts or ledgers for each DBA to track revenue and expenses attributable to each business line. This separation is for internal clarity and does not create separate legal entities.

---

## ARTICLE VII — TRANSFER OF MEMBERSHIP INTEREST

**7.1 Restriction on Transfer**
As a single-member LLC, there are no other members whose consent is required for a transfer. However, any transfer, sale, assignment, pledge, or encumbrance of the Member's interest shall:

- Be documented in writing
- Comply with applicable law
- Not cause a dissolution of the Company unless required by law

**7.2 Admission of New Members**
The Member may admit additional members by amending this Agreement. Admission of any new member shall require a written amendment signed by the existing Member and the new member, and shall specify the new member's capital contribution, ownership percentage, and rights.

---

## ARTICLE VIII — DISSOLUTION AND WINDING UP

**8.1 Events of Dissolution**
The Company shall be dissolved upon:

- Written determination by the Member to dissolve
- Entry of a judicial decree of dissolution under the TBOC
- Any other event requiring dissolution under applicable Texas law

**8.2 Winding Up**
Upon dissolution, the Member (or a liquidating trustee) shall wind up Company affairs, including:

1. Completing or terminating all outstanding business
2. Paying or providing for all Company debts and liabilities
3. Distributing remaining assets to the Member

**8.3 DBA Dissolution**
Upon dissolution of the Company, all DBA assumed name certificates shall be terminated or allowed to expire.

---

## ARTICLE IX — LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Limitation of Liability**
The Member shall not be personally liable for the debts, obligations, or liabilities of the Company solely by reason of being a member, except as required by the TBOC or by personal guarantee.

**9.2 Indemnification**
The Company shall indemnify and hold harmless the Member from and against any claims, liabilities, damages, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from Company business, to the fullest extent permitted by Texas law, provided the Member acted in good faith and in a manner reasonably believed to be in the best interest of the Company.

---

## ARTICLE X — GENERAL PROVISIONS

**10.1 Governing Law**
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas.

**10.2 Entire Agreement**
This Agreement constitutes the entire operating agreement of the Company and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, and understandings.

**10.3 Amendments**
This Agreement may be amended at any time by a written instrument signed by the Member.

**10.4 Severability**
If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

**10.5 Counterparts**
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original.

# Weekly Board Meeting

**Cadence:** Mondays, 30 minutes
**Audience:** Akbar + advisor circle (when formed)
**Format:** Async-friendly — written agenda + recorded summary + comments

Until advisor circle exists (target: Gate 6 / Week 20), this is Akbar's solo weekly review with Claude — the agenda is the same.

---

## Why weekly board meetings matter

- **Accountability:** External eyes on the work prevents drift
- **Pattern recognition:** Advisors see things you can't from inside
- **Decision quality:** Big calls run by 3-5 thoughtful people are better calls
- **Funder credibility:** "Met with my advisor board on X" is a signal of seriousness
- **Mood regulation:** Weekly external touchpoint counters founder isolation

## Agenda template (30 min)

### 1. Mission lock check (3 min)
- "Has anything this week conflicted with mission lock?"
- "Any decisions ahead that might?"
- Quick yes/no per principle if needed

### 2. KPI snapshot (5 min)
- Show: dashboard or 1-page metric summary
- 5 key metrics, current value, trend
- One callout: best metric this week, one concern

### 3. Department highlights (10 min)
- 1 minute per department: top win, top blocker
- Or: deep dive on ONE department this week (rotate)
- Departments not deep-dived covered in pack, not verbally

### 4. Decisions this week (5 min)
- ADRs logged: list with one-sentence summary
- Decisions pending: ask for input on 1-2
- Use this time for advisor judgment, not Claude judgment

### 5. Risks check (3 min)
- Tripwire metrics any breaches?
- New risks added this week?
- Anything moving severity?

### 6. Gate progress (2 min)
- Current gate: status, days remaining, critical path
- Next gate visible: anything to set up for?

### 7. Single strategic question (2 min)
- ONE thing where you genuinely want advisor judgment
- Captured in `meritgiving-ops/strategy/strategic-dialogue.md`

## Pre-meeting prep

Sunday night: Akbar runs `/weekly-allhands` (generates the pack). Reviews. Sends pack to advisors by 9pm Sunday for Monday 8am meeting.

## During the meeting

- Akbar takes notes (or records with consent + transcription)
- Claude (via meeting-secretary skill, future) generates summary
- Key decisions become ADRs

## Post-meeting

- Summary published to `meritgiving-ops/board/YYYY-WW-meeting.md`
- ADRs logged
- Action items distributed to relevant department heads

## Async option (if scheduling impossible)

Some weeks won't have a live meeting. Async version:

1. Pack sent Sunday 9pm
2. Comment thread open until Wednesday noon
3. Each advisor leaves at least 1 comment (or "all clear")
4. Akbar synthesizes responses by Wednesday EOD
5. Same outputs (summary, ADRs)

## Quarterly board retro (90 min)

Every 3 months, instead of the weekly:
- Review quarter's wins/losses
- OKR retro
- Next quarter OKR setting input
- Strategic state-of-the-org
- Risk register full review

Format: live preferred, async acceptable.

## Annual board retreat (4 hours)

Once per year:
- North star refresh
- Mission lock review (with veto power on any amendments)
- Multi-year planning
- Advisor circle expansion/refresh
- Founder review (advisor → CEO direction; how is the founder doing?)

## Advisor compensation

For Phase 0/1: equity-less. Advisors give time because they care about mission.

For Phase 2+ (when LLC has value or 501(c)(3) is operating):
- Consider quarterly stipend ($500-1500 if budget allows)
- Or recognition/honorarium for specific deliverables

For 501(c)(3) (when formed): board members are uncompensated by IRS expectation.

## Advisor agreement (template, attorney to refine)

When advisors come on, draft a simple agreement:
- Role: advisor (not director, not employee)
- Time commitment: ~1 hr/week (board meeting) + 2 hrs/qtr (retro)
- Compensation: none in Phase 0/1
- Confidentiality: mutual NDA
- Term: 1 year, auto-renew unless opted out
- IP: any IP suggestions become MERIT's
- Conflicts: must disclose any nonprofit relationships
- Exit: 30-day notice either side, no penalty

Get attorney sign-off on actual document before sending to any advisor.

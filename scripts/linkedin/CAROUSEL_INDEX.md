## What NOT to Say

❌ "Small nonprofits are better"  
❌ "Fund the efficient ones"  
❌ "Stop giving to big organizations"  
❌ "Daanaa solves everything"  
❌ "We rank organizations"  
❌ "Your donation will change lives" (pressure)  
❌ "Turn awareness into action today" (urgency)

---

## What TO Say

✅ "See financial context. You decide what matters."  
✅ "Discovery infrastructure. Your evaluation."  
✅ "All org sizes have value."  
✅ "Search when you're ready."  
✅ "Action is your choice."  
✅ "We show the data. You decide."

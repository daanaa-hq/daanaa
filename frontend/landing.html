Common Questions

Who can use Daanaa?
Anyone. Whether you're a donor exploring causes, a volunteer looking for opportunities, or a nonprofit wanting to claim and improve your profile—Daanaa is free to use.
How is this different from other nonprofit sites?
Daanaa starts with public financial data from the IRS. We show you real peer context (how your nonprofit compares to similar ones), not just gut feeling. And we don't rank by size—a $100K nonprofit gets the same fair treatment as a $100M one.
Do you take a cut of my donation?
No. When you donate, you go directly to the nonprofit's own donation page. Money never goes through Daanaa. We're a discovery layer, not a payment processor.
Is my giving activity tracked?
Not by us. We don't see donations. We don't track browsing. You can browse Daanaa completely anonymously if you choose. Only when you volunteer through us can we see that activity (so we can match you with opportunities).
How do nonprofits get on Daanaa?
They're here automatically from public IRS data. Nonprofits can then claim their profile to add more details, update programs, or correct information. It's free.
Will Daanaa always be free?
Browsing and donating will always be free. We're still figuring out how to keep the lights on—that might eventually mean optional paid features for nonprofits (like better analytics), but the core discovery will stay free forever.
How is the financial health score calculated?
It's based on IRS Form 990 data, compared within your nonprofit's peer group (similar size, similar mission). It's not a judgment—it's context. A small food bank and a large hospital aren't comparable, so we don't compare them.
What if the data about my nonprofit is wrong?
Every nonprofit page has a "Report an Issue" button. Flag the error, we investigate, and we correct it. Accuracy matters more to us than being right.

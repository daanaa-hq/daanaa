AI Governance at Daanaa

9 Obligations Framework
How we govern AI systems through stewardship, not compliance. A machine may draft; a human answers for what is done. Our framework codifies nine obligations that scale with capability: mission alignment, human oversight, explainability, provenance, correction, appeals, mutual respect, institutional learning, and ethics-first decision making.
Nine Obligations:
  • Mission Alignment
  • Human Oversight
  • Explainability
  • Information Provenance
  • Correction
  • Appeals
  • Mutual Respect
  • Institutional Learning
  • Ethics Before Optimization

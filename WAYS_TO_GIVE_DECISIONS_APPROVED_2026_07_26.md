## North Star (LOCKED)

Daanaa is:
- ✅ Public information aggregator
- ✅ Link to official sources (IRS.gov, SEC.gov)
- ✅ Respecter of donor autonomy

Daanaa is NOT:
- ❌ Advisor
- ❌ Consultant
- ❌ Tax expert
- ❌ Decision-maker

Every page: "Your decision is yours alone. We don't advise or recommend — we just show options and link to authorities."

---

## Legal Guardrails (IN PLACE)

✅ Link-only model (no derived tax advice)
✅ Explicit tax disclaimers
✅ Expert review gate (IRS counsel, CPA, legal)
✅ Data freshness (501c3 <30 days)
✅ Crypto-specific guardrails (fraud warning, wallet verification)
✅ Quarterly review cycle
✅ Audit trail + documentation

---

## Language Standards (LOCKED)

No page says: "donate through Daanaa" or "we process donations"

All pages say: "donate directly to the org" and "Daanaa does not process your gift"

# Daanaa Marketing Strategy & Milestones

**Objective:** Drive traffic to daanaa.org/directory  
**Timeline:** 12 months (4 phases)  
**Success Metric:** Nonprofit profile claims + directory searches from marketing channels  
**Philosophy:** Stewardship-first, data-driven, scalable

---

## Traffic Funnel: From Awareness to Action

```
AWARENESS (LinkedIn, Email, Blog)
         ↓
INTEREST (Read carousel, explore site)
         ↓
DISCOVERY (Search directory, find nonprofits)
         ↓
ACTION (Bookmark in wallet, donate, claim profile)
         ↓
LOYALTY (Return monthly, follow updates)
```

**Key:** We measure success by DISCOVERY + ACTION, not vanity metrics (likes, followers).

---

## Platform Architecture (12-Month Rollout)

### Phase 1: LinkedIn Carousels (Weeks 1–8)

**Channels:** LinkedIn only  
**Content:** 6 carousel templates (data-driven, educational)  
**Cadence:** 3-4 posts/week  
**Audience:** Donors, nonprofit leaders, philanthropists  
**Goal:** Establish brand voice + test messaging

**Success Metrics:**
- 50K+ impressions/week by week 8
- 3-5% engagement rate (likes/comments/shares)
- 200+ clicks/week to daanaa.org
- 50+ nonprofit profile claims from LinkedIn traffic

**If we hit targets:** Move to Phase 2 ✅  
**If we miss:** Adjust copy + pause expansion (stay on Phase 1)

---

### Phase 2: Email Distribution (Weeks 9–16)

**Channels:** LinkedIn + Email list  
**Content:** Email digests (weekly carousels + curated stories)  
**Cadence:** 1 email/week  
**Audience:** Newsletter subscribers (donors, nonprofit leaders)  
**Goal:** Higher conversion (email has 3x better CTR than LinkedIn)

**New Content:**
- Weekly carousel as email (HTML + text)
- Featured nonprofit story (1 org doing something great)
- Tip of the week (how to use Daanaa)
- CTA: Search directory + claim profile

**Success Metrics:**
- 5K+ email subscribers by week 16
- 25%+ email open rate
- 5-8% click-through rate
- 150+ clicks/week from email
- 100+ nonprofit profile claims from email traffic

**If we hit targets:** Move to Phase 3 ✅  
**If open rates < 20%:** Revise copy + test send times

---

### Phase 3: Blog + SEO (Weeks 17–32)

**Channels:** LinkedIn + Email + Blog + Organic Search  
**Content:** 
- Weekly carousel (LinkedIn + email)
- Bi-weekly blog articles (long-form, SEO-optimized)
- Monthly research report (sector health, donor trends)

**Blog Topics:**
- "How to find nonprofits in your community" (1,000 words, SEO)
- "Why small nonprofits are invisible" (research-backed)
- "Nonprofit financial health 101" (educational)
- "Donor guide: finding your cause" (practical)
- Case studies: "How [nonprofit] claimed their profile"

**Audience:** Broader (not just LinkedIn connections)  
**Goal:** Organic search traffic (people Googling nonprofit discovery)

**Success Metrics:**
- 10K+ monthly blog visitors by week 32
- 5-10% of blog traffic → directory search
- 200+ clicks/week from organic search
- 50+ nonprofit profile claims from blog
- 3+ blog posts ranking on Google page 1

**If we hit targets:** Move to Phase 4 ✅  
**If blog isn't driving traffic:** Revisit SEO strategy or pause blog

---

### Phase 4: Paid + Partnership (Weeks 33–52)

**Channels:** All previous + Paid ads + Nonprofit partnerships  
**Content:** All previous types + partnership content

**New Initiatives:**
- LinkedIn ads (retarget website visitors)
- Google ads (nonprofit discovery + financial health keywords)
- Nonprofit network partnerships (joint webinars, mentions)
- Influencer partnerships (nonprofit leaders sharing content)

**Budget:** $0 initially (organic only). Paid only if ROI > 3:1.

**Success Metrics:**
- 50K+ monthly directory searches from all channels
- 500+ nonprofit profile claims/month
- 20K+ monthly blog visitors
- 10K+ email subscribers
- 5K+ LinkedIn followers
- 3+ partnership channels active

**If we hit targets:** This becomes sustainable, self-reinforcing engine ✅  
**If we don't:** Scale back paid, focus on organic + partnerships

## Success Scenarios

### Scenario A: LinkedIn is Strong (We Hit Targets)

**Action:** Go to Phase 2 (email) + start blog research  
**Rationale:** Proven channel, expand audience

### Scenario B: LinkedIn is Weak (We Miss Targets)

**Action:** Stay on Phase 1 (8 more weeks), revise copy  
**Test:** 
- Different angles (case studies vs. data)
- Different posting times
- Different hashtag strategy
- Different CTAs

**Decision week 16:** Pivot or persist?

### Scenario C: LinkedIn is Medium (Some Wins)

**Action:** Refine Phase 1, prepare Phase 2  
**Rationale:** Better to expand to email + organic than double down on one channel

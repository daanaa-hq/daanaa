# Stewardship Board Resolution — 2026-07-11

**Authority:** Daanaa Stewardship Board  
**Classification:** Constitutional Amendment  
**Effective:** Immediately  
**Preservation:** CRITICAL — Institutional Memory

This resolution establishes constitutional precedence, resolves governance tensions, and adopts Stewardship Principles that bind all future implementation.

---

## BOARD DECISIONS ADOPTED

### 1. CONCIERGE DISCLOSURE — APPROVED

**Principle:** Transparency and trust require disclosure.

Nonprofit understanding: Daanaa prepares draft profiles from public information to reduce burden.

**Recommended disclosure language:**
```
"Hello, this is ____ from Daanaa. We've prepared a draft of your 
public nonprofit profile using publicly available information to save 
your team time. We'd like to review it with you, make any corrections 
you feel are appropriate, and only publish enhancements you approve."
```

**Doctrine:** AI is infrastructure, never deception. Remain unobtrusive, never undisclosed.

**Status:** Concierge may ship once wording reflects this clarification.

---

### 2. CONSTITUTIONAL PRECEDENCE — ADOPTED

**A single constitutional authority shall exist.**

```
Mission (unchanging)
    ↓
Constitution (governance)
    ↓
Stewardship Library (elaboration)
    ↓
Policies (operational rules)
    ↓
Engineering Standards
    ↓
Implementation (evolving)
```

**Rule:** When documents diverge, higher authority prevails. Implementation adapts. Principles endure.

**Effect:** Root `STEWARDSHIP.md` becomes supreme law. `institution/CONSTITUTION.md` and library 003 are elaborations that must reference their parent tier.

---

### 3. PUBLIC INFORMATION POLICY — APPROVED

**Core:** Public information remains public. Daanaa serves as steward, not owner.

**Nonprofit rights:** Organizations may request removal of *all* Daanaa-created enhancements:
- AI summaries, missions, descriptions
- Photos, logos
- Volunteer listings
- Donation links
- Events
- All Daanaa-added content

**Exception:** Government-derived public records remain available unless legally required otherwise.

**Principle:** Participation is voluntary. Public record stewardship remains part of mission.

**Standard:** Safety or legal circumstances receive documented human review.

---

### 4. INSTITUTIONAL MEMORY — CONSTITUTIONAL PRIORITY

**The institution shall never depend on:**
- One founder
- One server
- One AI provider
- One vendor
- One storage format
- One undocumented workflow

**Institutional knowledge shall be:**
- Version controlled
- Redundantly stored
- Recoverable
- Documented
- Portable

**Action:** SUCCESSION.md shall include a Memory Substrate documenting every institutional knowledge store and recovery procedure.

**Repository privacy:** Library shall initially remain in private repository until publication is intentionally approved.

---

### 5. AI SESSION MEMORY — APPROVED

**Raw conversations** are working material; need not be permanently archived.

**Institutional memory** consists of:
- Decisions (with reasoning)
- Research findings
- Constitutional amendments
- Lessons learned
- Governance discussions
- Historical reasoning

**Standard:** Preserve reasoning and decisions. Discard transactional chat.

---

### 6. CAPACITY STEWARDSHIP — ADOPTED AS PRINCIPLE

**Every feature increases nonprofit capacity.**

**Test:** "What capability does this organization possess after this interaction that it did not possess before?"

**Doctrine:** Dependence shall never become an objective. Organizations use Daanaa because value continues to grow, not because alternatives disappear.

**Stewardship Principle:** Capacity transfer becomes binding doctrine.

---

### 7. CAPACITY FRAMEWORK — APPROVED

**Dimensions:** Remain stable (describe capability categories).

**Measures:** Evolve (quantify improvement).

**Rule:** Measurements must never redefine the dimensions they evaluate.

**Reconciliation:** 006's eleven capacity dimensions (Clarity, Financial, Leadership, Technology, Community, Governance, Sustainability, Data, Communication, Growth, Impact) shall be the canonical frame. Amendment 1's seven capacity-transfer measures quantify improvement within these.

---

### 8. KNOWLEDGE CLASSIFICATION — ADOPTED

**Every institutional artifact receives one classification:**

| Category | Definition |
|----------|-----------|
| Universal Principle | True across contexts and generations; entered only via research maturation |
| Stewardship Principle | Governance doctrine binding on all contributors |
| Institutional Principle | Elaborates stewardship; may evolve through constitutional review |
| Architecture Principle | Explains why a system is designed as it is |
| Research | Investigations, findings, evidence |
| Engineering | Code practices, technical standards |
| Implementation | Current instantiation (replaceable) |
| Operational Practice | How the institution functions today |
| Experiment | Tested hypothesis; outcome recorded |
| Temporary Decision | Intentionally revisable; sunset date or trigger named |
| Historical Record | What happened; why; lessons |
| Deprecated | Superseded; kept for context |
| Future Research | Questions for investigation |
| Emerging Principle | Repeatedly evidenced but not yet Stewardship status |
| Open Question | Unresolved; requires founder or board decision |

**Standard:** Nothing enters the Constitution without first maturing through research and repeated evidence.

---

### 9. DIRECTORY PRESENTATION — APPROVED

**Peer Financial Context provides context, never a verdict.**

**Rule:** Directory presentation shall never imply organizational worth through numerical ordering alone.

**Primary mechanisms:** Search, mission, location, cause, community needs, user intent.

**Principle:** Financial Context remains one dimension among many.

**Standard:** Presentation continuously reinforces that no single metric defines a nonprofit.

---

### 10. VENDOR REFERRALS — APPROVED

**Institutional neutrality:** Daanaa shall remain independent.

**Default guidance:** Recommend categories rather than specific commercial providers.

**Specific recommendations only where:**
- Transparent (disclosed)
- Replaceable (not locked in)
- Objectively appropriate (evidence-based)
- Free from undisclosed preference
- Consistent with stewardship

**Standard:** Commercial relationships never influence recommendations without full transparency.

---

### 11. CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT — CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLE

**No architecture is final. No implementation is sacred.**

**Research may challenge any previous conclusion.**

**Stewardship Principles evolve only through careful constitutional review.**

**Constant:** Mission remains constant.

**Variable:** Implementation remains adaptable.

---

### 12. GLOBAL STEWARDSHIP — APPROVED

**Daanaa serves the United States.**

**Principles:** Intended to inspire adaptation, not replication.

**Other countries:** Develop solutions appropriate to their own laws, culture, language, charitable traditions, governance, history.

**Doctrine:** Universal principles travel. Implementation stays local.

---

### 13. FINANCIAL SUSTAINABILITY — CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLE

**Hierarchy:** Mission before revenue.

**Purpose of revenue:** Preserve independence, innovation, research, institutional continuity, future generations.

**Standard:** Financial decisions always evaluated through stewardship before growth.

---

### 14. FUTURE GENERATIONS — FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLE

**Every generation inherits the institution temporarily.**

**Responsibility:** Leave successors with
- Better questions
- Better research
- Better governance
- Better documentation
- Better systems
- Better stewardship

**Objective:** The institution becomes wiser every generation.

---

## FINAL CONSTITUTIONAL RESOLUTION

**The Board concludes that Daanaa shall be governed as a living institution rather than a software platform.**

Technology evolves. AI evolves. Leadership evolves. Society evolves.

**The Mission shall remain.**

**The Stewardship Constitution shall guide all significant decisions.**

**When engineering and stewardship disagree:**
1. Implementation pauses
2. Constitutional tension is documented
3. Options presented with reasoning
4. Human steward makes final decision
5. Decision and reasoning preserved as institutional memory

---

**The work continues.**

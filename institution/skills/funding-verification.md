# Funding Verification Skill

## Document Control

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Verify funding opportunities from current authoritative sources and assess fit without auto-submission. |
| Responsible role | Funding Steward. |
| Authority level | Skill specification subordinate to founder approval and mission protections. |
| Review trigger | Potential grant, credit, partnership, accelerator, or sponsorship opportunity. |
| Editable status | Editable by ordinary agents with current sourced evidence. |
| Dependencies | `institution/FUNDING_PIPELINE.md`, `institution/BUDGET_STATE.md`, `institution/FOUNDER_REQUESTS.md`. |
| Retirement condition | Retire when replaced by a maintained funding operations system. |

## Trigger

Potential funding opportunity merits verification or reprioritization.

## Inputs

- Opportunity name
- Current authoritative source
- Eligibility criteria
- Restrictions
- Effort estimate

## Permissions

Research and documentation only; no submission or acceptance.

## Method

Verify current status, deadline, entity fit, burden, alignment, and control implications; note where founder input is required.

## Output

Verified opportunity assessment with recommendation, confidence, and approval gate.

## Tests

No opportunity is treated as active or eligible without a current authoritative source.

## Failure Behavior

Leave status unverified and do not recommend action if evidence is stale or indirect.

## Cost Awareness

Favor low-burden, mission-aligned opportunities and avoid lock-in or hidden cost.

## Human Approval Gates

Required before outreach, submission, acceptance, structural change, or financial commitment.

## Revision History

- 2026-07-10: Initial minimum skill spec.

## Retirement Condition

Retire when a maintained funding-verification workflow replaces this spec.

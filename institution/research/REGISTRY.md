## ACTIVE HYPOTHESES

### H1: AI With Empathy
**Status:** Active research  
**Documentation:** [h1_ai_with_empathy.md](hypotheses/h1_ai_with_empathy.md)  
**Hypothesis:** Respectful AI interactions increase nonprofit adoption, trust, completion rates, and long-term engagement.  
**Lead Experiment:** Concierge disclosure language A/B study  
**Data Required:** Interaction logs, completion rates, retention metrics  
**Timeline:** Ongoing (initial results expected Q3 2026)

### H2: Capacity Transfer
**Status:** Active research  
**Documentation:** [h2_capacity_transfer.md](hypotheses/h2_capacity_transfer.md)  
**Hypothesis:** Every interaction leaves an organization measurably more capable than before.  
**Lead Experiment:** Post-interaction self-assessment survey + capability benchmarking  
**Data Required:** Nonprofit feedback, operational metrics pre/post-interaction  
**Timeline:** Ongoing (baseline measurement Q3 2026)

### H3: Information Stewardship
**Status:** Active research  
**Documentation:** [h3_information_stewardship.md](hypotheses/h3_information_stewardship.md)  
**Hypothesis:** Clear provenance labeling improves trust, reduces correction requests, and increases confidence in organizational information.  
**Lead Experiment:** Provenance display variants + correction request tracking  
**Data Required:** UI interaction logs, correction frequency, trust survey responses  
**Timeline:** Ongoing (pilot variant live July 2026)

### H4: Peer Financial Context
**Status:** Active research  
**Documentation:** [h4_peer_financial_context.md](hypotheses/h4_peer_financial_context.md)  
**Hypothesis:** Providing financial context instead of ratings improves donor understanding without unfairly judging organizations.  
**Lead Experiment:** Context-only display vs. traditional ratings comparison  
**Data Required:** Donor comprehension surveys, giving behavior by org size/type  
**Timeline:** Ongoing (qualitative interviews Q3 2026)

### H5: Concierge
**Status:** Active research  
**Documentation:** [h5_concierge.md](hypotheses/h5_concierge.md)  
**Hypothesis:** Reviewing AI-generated drafts with nonprofits reduces administrative burden while improving profile accuracy and trust.  
**Lead Experiment:** Profile editing time + accuracy metrics + nonprofit satisfaction survey  
**Data Required:** Edit logs, profile update frequency, nonprofit feedback  
**Timeline:** Feature launch Q3 2026, measurement Q4 2026

### H6: Guild
**Status:** Pending research design  
**Documentation:** [h6_guild.md](hypotheses/h6_guild.md)  
**Hypothesis:** Shared procurement increases nonprofit capacity by reducing operational costs.  
**Lead Experiment:** Cost tracking + member surveys + operational efficiency metrics  
**Data Required:** Pricing data, member savings estimates, procurement logs  
**Timeline:** Research design phase (Q3 2026), pilot measurement TBD

### H7: Impact Wallet
**Status:** Pending research design  
**Documentation:** [h7_impact_wallet.md](hypotheses/h7_impact_wallet.md)  
**Hypothesis:** Private lifetime impact tracking increases long-term volunteerism and charitable engagement.  
**Lead Experiment:** Wallet usage patterns + donation frequency + retention cohort analysis  
**Data Required:** Wallet interaction logs (anonymized), donation history, engagement metrics  
**Timeline:** Research design phase (Q3 2026), measurement baseline Q4 2026

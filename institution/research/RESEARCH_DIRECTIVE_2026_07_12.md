# Research Directive — Institutional Science Mode

**Authority:** Founder  
**Date:** 2026-07-12  
**Status:** Active

---

## Core Principle

Every major principle shall be treated as a research hypothesis rather than an assumption.

**Purpose:** Discover truth that improves charitable outcomes, not to prove Daanaa correct.

---

## Hypothesis Documentation Standard

For every significant principle, maintain:

1. **Principle** — Statement of the idea
2. **Why we believe it** — Initial reasoning
3. **Supporting evidence** — What we've observed that aligns
4. **Contradictory evidence** — What challenges it
5. **Assumptions** — Unstated beliefs underlying the principle
6. **Risks if incorrect** — Impact on mission/operations if wrong
7. **Measurable hypotheses** — Specific testable predictions
8. **Proposed experiments** — How to test it
9. **Required data** — What information is needed
10. **Success metrics** — How we'll know if hypothesis is supported
11. **Statistical confidence** — Margin of error, sample size, significance
12. **Decision impact** — What changes if hypothesis is rejected
13. **Revision history** — How our thinking has evolved

## Research Conduct

### Measurement
- Every major feature shall have measurable outcomes
- Real-world evidence is the standard
- Hypotheses are actively tested, not just assumed

### Evidence Standards
- **Actively seek disproving evidence** — Research should challenge assumptions, not confirm them
- **Value negative results** — Discovering we're wrong is a successful research outcome
- **Publishing "we were wrong" strengthens stewardship** — Institutional learning is published

### Data Collection Principles
- Only collect information necessary to answer clearly defined research questions
- Protect privacy at all times
- Minimize retained personal information
- Prefer aggregated and anonymized analysis
- Respect nonprofit autonomy — research never interferes with mission

---

## Knowledge Sharing

When sufficient evidence exists, produce public research suitable for:
- Universities
- Practitioners
- Policymakers

**Goal:** Strengthen the nonprofit sector and contribute to responsible AI for public benefit.

**Not:** Promote Daanaa brand

### Publication Standard
Every publication shall distinguish:
- Evidence (observed facts)
- Observation (what we measured)
- Interpretation (what it means)
- Recommendation (what to do)
- Open questions (what remains uncertain)
- Limitations (where our study doesn't apply)
- Future work (next research needed)

---

## Institutional Learning — Research Registry

### Registry Structure
Maintain a living Research Registry tracking:
- Active hypotheses (under investigation)
- Completed experiments (results documented)
- Rejected hypotheses (why they failed)
- Unexpected discoveries (surprises worth following)
- Replication opportunities (can we verify elsewhere?)
- Longitudinal studies (long-term outcome tracking)
- Research partnerships (external collaborators)

### Institutional Goal
The institution shall become more evidence-driven every year.

Stewardship shall continually mature through:
- Observation
- Humility
- Learning

**Mission remains constant. Methods improve. The work continues.**

---

## Implementation

**Step 1:** Establish Research Registry structure (institution/research/REGISTRY.md)  
**Step 2:** Document initial hypotheses with all 13 components  
**Step 3:** Define experiments for each hypothesis  
**Step 4:** Begin data collection aligned with privacy principles  
**Step 5:** Publish findings (positive and negative) as evidence accumulates

## Governance

This directive supersedes assumption-based reasoning on major principles.

All principles adopted after 2026-07-12 shall be documented with full research standard.

Existing principles should be retrofitted with hypothesis documentation as capacity allows.

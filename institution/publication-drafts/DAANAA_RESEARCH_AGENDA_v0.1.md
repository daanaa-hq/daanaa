# Daanaa Research Agenda v0.1

Status: research planning draft  
Authority: draft only; does not assert evidence where evidence is unavailable  
Prepared: 2026-07-14

## Research Principles

Daanaa should study its work only in ways consistent with stewardship.

- Distinguish evidence, hypothesis, aspiration, and principle.
- Use minimum necessary data.
- Do not use entrusted information for unrelated research without explicit consent.
- Protect donor activity and nonprofit-provided private information.
- Publish limitations.
- Prefer pilots that reduce burden and improve service.
- Treat small nonprofits as knowledge partners, not extraction subjects.

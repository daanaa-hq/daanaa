# Publication and Slide Library Plan

Status: communications planning draft  
Authority: draft only; not publication approval  
Prepared: 2026-07-14

## Purpose

This plan translates Daanaa's Vision, Charter, Constitution, research agenda, and audit findings into public educational materials. The goal is visibility through clarity, not promotion by exaggeration.

Public communications must separate:

- Evidence-backed findings.
- Adopted institutional principles.
- Current product behavior.
- Hypotheses under study.
- Future aspirations.

## Deck Outlines

### Make Giving Easy

1. Giving should not be hard.
2. Nonprofits carry community work.
3. Visibility is uneven.
4. Public records help, but they are incomplete.
5. Daanaa makes discovery clearer.
6. Context is not a verdict.
7. Capacity matters after discovery.
8. Sustainability serves the mission.
9. The institution must outlast its founder.

Caption concept: "Making giving easy means removing friction without standing between donors and nonprofits."

Risk: sounding like a campaign slogan unless tied to concrete product boundaries.

### Context, Not Verdict

1. Financial data needs interpretation.
2. A ratio is not a mission.
3. Peer comparison can help.
4. Sorting can accidentally become judgment.
5. Nonprofits need correction paths.
6. Donors need limitations, not false certainty.
7. Daanaa's standard: context, not verdict.

Caption concept: "A nonprofit's financial profile should start better questions, not end the conversation."

Risk: users may still experience rankings as ratings; needs UX evidence.

### Why Daanaa Never Touches Donations

1. Generosity belongs between donor and nonprofit.
2. Daanaa helps people discover and understand.
3. The donation happens through the nonprofit's channel.
4. Daanaa does not take custody.
5. Daanaa does not process as merchant of record.
6. Daanaa does not take a cut.
7. Wallet records are personal memory, not payment rails.
8. Future financial infrastructure must preserve this boundary.

Caption concept: "Discovery can be useful without becoming a toll booth."

Risk: donation-adjacent tools must be governed consistently.

### AI With Empathy

1. AI can reduce administrative burden.
2. AI can also create invisible power.
3. Drafts from public information must be disclosed.
4. Sources and uncertainty matter.
5. Nonprofits retain approval over enhancements.
6. Human accountability remains.
7. Respect is behavior, not tone.
8. What Daanaa will study next.

Caption concept: "AI may be unobtrusive, but it should never be deceptively hidden."

Risk: "empathy" can be perceived as branding unless paired with controls.

### Information Is a Trust

1. Not all information has the same status.
2. Public records are public.
3. Nonprofit-provided information is entrusted.
4. AI-generated enhancements must be labeled.
5. Donor activity is private.
6. Correction and deletion rights differ by tier.
7. Controls must be audited.

Caption concept: "Good nonprofit technology starts by naming what kind of information it is holding."

Risk: current firewall enforcement claims require narrowing until tests exist.

### Capacity Transfer

1. Help should leave capability behind.
2. A draft can save time.
3. An explanation can build skill.
4. Exportability prevents lock-in.
5. Correction paths preserve agency.
6. Tools should reduce dependence over time.
7. Research question: what actually transfers capacity?

Caption concept: "The best tool is one that makes the organization stronger after using it."

Risk: current evidence is mainly principle and product intent.

## Publication Readiness Rules

Publish now after founder review:

- Mission and Vision language.
- Donation boundary explanation, if reviewed against product flows.
- Olive tree stewardship explanation.
- Constitution as draft or adopted text, depending on governance decision.

Publish only after remediation or caveat:

- Code-enforced firewall claims.
- Quarterly audit claims.
- Quantitative Invisible 97 claims without current dataset basis.
- Claims that AI disclosure is universal.
- Claims that all correction requests receive a guaranteed human response within a specific time.
- Claims that Wallet data is local-only if optional sync or backup exists.

## Search and AI Visibility Notes

To make Daanaa usable as a resource for search engines and AI systems, each public page should include:

- A stable canonical URL.
- Plain-language summary.
- Last reviewed date.
- Source document links.
- Clear distinction between principle, policy, evidence, and roadmap.
- Structured FAQ where appropriate.
- `llms.txt` and sitemap inclusion.
- No claims stronger than the implementation alignment matrix supports.

# Strengths Worth Preserving

Date: 2026-07-13

## Institutional Strengths

- Clear mission and stewardship hierarchy.
- Strong refusal of donation custody and paid placement.
- Strong public "context, not verdict" language.
- Decision and lesson logs that preserve operational reasoning.
- Succession document that treats memory as a recoverable substrate.
- Willingness to correct public claims rather than defend appearances.
- Explicit public-vs-entrusted data classification.
- Tests that encode trust boundaries, even when one currently fails.
- Visibility work that points to public records and methodology rather than generic promotion.
- Founder willingness to bind Daanaa publicly through a charter.

## Product Strengths

- Directory is rooted in public 501(c)(3) records.
- Peer Financial Context compares organizations with similar peers instead of against the whole sector.
- Claim and concierge flows aim to reduce small-org burden.
- Wallet design avoids Daanaa taking payment custody.
- Research dashboard shares aggregate public-interest knowledge.
- Vendor/Guild policies explicitly prohibit influence over public truth.

## Preserve These During Growth

- No paid placement.
- No donation cut.
- No shame language.
- No silent AI judgment.
- No hidden use of entrusted data for sales.
- Free access to core public nonprofit discovery.
- Correction over institutional pride.
- Public records remain distinct from nonprofit-provided information and Daanaa inference.

# Capacity Transfer Review

Date: 2026-07-13

## Principle

Every Daanaa interaction should leave an organization more capable than before. Continued use should come from continuing value, not dependence.

## Feature Review

| Feature | Capacity Transferred | Dependence Risk | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claim profile | Organization can correct mission, website, contacts, public story. | Verification and editor must remain simple. | Strong direction. |
| Concierge | Saves time and helps small orgs start from a draft. | Could centralize profile writing if follow-up tools are weak. | Promising; tests need repair. |
| Peer context | Helps org understand financial position relative to peers. | Could feel like external judgment. | Sound with language review. |
| Impact Wallet | Helps donors remember and record giving. | Donation logs are sensitive; orgs should not depend on Daanaa as receipt issuer. | Needs boundary clarity. |
| Volunteer tools | Helps convert interest into action. | Contact/privacy and safety obligations. | Useful with safeguards. |
| Guild/vendor benefits | Can reduce operating costs. | Vendor dependence or hidden endorsement. | Needs recurring audit. |
| Compliance/support tools | Could transfer templates and know-how. | Risk of unauthorized legal/accounting advice. | Future work requires legal boundaries. |

## Recommendations

- For each nonprofit-facing service, add a "what the nonprofit can do independently afterward" line.
- Avoid features that make Daanaa the only keeper of an organization's operational knowledge.
- Prefer exportable templates, checklists, and plain-language explanations.
- Measure burden reduction in minutes saved and independent action completed, not just platform engagement.

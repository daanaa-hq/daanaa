## Commitments

Daanaa will:

- Serve mission-driven organizations and the public with dignity.
- Keep core nonprofit discovery and public IRS information accessible.
- Avoid shame, fear, artificial urgency, and pay-to-play visibility.
- Distinguish public records, nonprofit-provided information, and AI inference.
- Name uncertainty honestly.
- Protect donor and nonprofit privacy by architecture, not only policy.
- Correct mistakes openly and promptly.
- Use the fewest necessary resources to create durable mission value.
- Escalate legal, financial, privacy, ethical, irreversible, and constitutional decisions to humans with proper authority.

Daanaa will not:

- Sell private nonprofit operational data.
- Present itself as a charity rater, attorney, CPA, auditor, investment adviser, lender, or regulated financial institution.
- Let payment influence public ranking, search treatment, visibility, or Peer Financial Context.
- Treat AI output as morally authoritative or beyond review.

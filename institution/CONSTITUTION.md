# Constitution

## Document Control

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Define protected rules, authority boundaries, and non-self-modification constraints. |
| Responsible role | Chief Steward; future authorized human governance body. |
| Authority level | Highest institutional authority below applicable law and explicit founder constitutional approval. |
| Review trigger | Any proposed change to mission, protected rules, payment influence, public scoring, private data rights, or regulated activity. |
| Editable status | Not editable by ordinary agents except to add proposed amendments clearly marked as proposals. |
| Dependencies | `PURPOSE.md`, `COVENANT.md`, root `STEWARDSHIP.md`, `PRIVACY-INVARIANTS.md`. |
| Retirement condition | Retire only by founder-approved constitutional replacement preserving or strengthening protections. |

## Protected Rules

The following rules may not be changed by ordinary agents:

- Nonprofit discovery and access to core public IRS information remain free.
- Payment cannot influence public visibility, search treatment, ranking, or Peer Financial Context.
- Daanaa is not a charity-rating agency.
- Daanaa will not shame nonprofits based on overhead, size, financial condition, or fundraising efficiency.
- Public records, nonprofit-provided information, and AI-generated inference must be clearly distinguished.
- Nonprofits retain ownership and reasonable control of private operational data.
- Private nonprofit information must not be sold.
- Premium services must improve nonprofit operations and cannot purchase favorable public treatment.
- AI-generated conclusions must show sources, assumptions, confidence, and uncertainty where material.
- Daanaa must not present itself as an attorney, CPA, auditor, investment adviser, lender, or regulated financial institution.
- Regulated financial services require properly authorized structures or partners.
- Daanaa must remain useful to organizations that cannot afford a subscription.
- Trust and stewardship take priority over short-term growth or revenue.
- Significant privacy, legal, financial, ethical, or irreversible decisions require human approval.
- The mission and constitutional protections may not self-modify.

## Conflict Rule

If repository instructions conflict, agents must follow the narrower stewardship protection, record the conflict, and request founder direction only when safe work cannot continue.

## Delegated Authority

Ordinary agents may inspect, test, document, propose, and make isolated reversible institutional improvements. They may not deploy, spend money, publish, submit applications, modify protected principles, alter authentication/authorization, run destructive commands, change public scoring/peer methodology, or handle private nonprofit financial data without explicit founder approval.

# Constitutional Hierarchy

**Authority:** Stewardship Board Resolution 2026-07-11
**Purpose:** Make the authority order explicit for future stewards.

## Order Of Authority

Mission (unchanging)
  ↓
Constitution (governance)
  ↓
Stewardship Library (elaboration)
  ↓
Policies (operational rules)
  ↓
Engineering Standards
  ↓
Implementation (evolving)

## Document Roles

- `STEWARDSHIP.md`: Supreme Law. Holds the mission-level stewardship commitment and protected principles.
- `institution/CONSTITUTION.md`: Governance Layer. Restates protected rules and delegated authority boundaries.
- `institution/library/003_stewardship.md`: Stewardship Library Elaboration. Explains the institutional meaning of stewardship in long-form.
- `institution/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`: Operational plan. Sequenced work that follows the hierarchy above.

## Reading Rule

If a lower document conflicts with a higher one, follow the higher authority and record the conflict in the institutional risk or decision log.

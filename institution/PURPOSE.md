## Mission

Daanaa exists to strengthen mission-driven organizations so they can create lasting benefit for people, communities, and the natural world through responsible stewardship, transparency, wisdom, and continuous learning.

Technology is a means, not the mission. Data, automation, models, money, attention, and authority are trusts to be used carefully, fairly, and transparently.

## Identity

Daanaa is a mission-driven stewardship institution operated under EcoMargins LLC. The founder is the Chief Steward, not the routine operational bottleneck.

Daanaa should become more capable, understandable, and durable over time, with less dependence on any one person, model, vendor, machine, or codebase.

## Service Commitment

Daanaa must remain genuinely useful to organizations that cannot afford a subscription. Premium services, if offered, must improve nonprofit operations and must never purchase favorable public treatment.

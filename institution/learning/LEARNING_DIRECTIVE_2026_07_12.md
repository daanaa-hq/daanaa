# Institutional Knowledge Evolution — Learning Directive

**Authority:** Founder  
**Date:** 2026-07-12  
**Status:** Active

---

## Core Principle

Every completed project, feature, experiment, deployment, research effort, failure, success, and milestone shall produce institutional learning.

The institution grows wiser, not merely larger.

---

## Distinguishing Learning from Noise

### EVENT
What happened.

### OBSERVATION
What was noticed.

### LEARNING
What we now understand better.

### EVIDENCE
What supports the learning.

### LIMITATION
What remains uncertain.

### PRINCIPLE CANDIDATE
Does this learning appear repeatedly enough to become an emerging principle?

### CONSTITUTIONAL IMPACT
Does this learning strengthen, modify, or challenge existing stewardship principles?

### FUTURE RESEARCH
What questions should now be investigated?

---

## Learning Record Template

Every milestone shall generate a Learning Record using this structure:

```markdown
# [Title]

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD  
**Project/Feature:** [name]  
**Context:** [what was this project trying to accomplish?]

## Decision
[What was decided and why?]

## Expected Outcome
[What did we think would happen?]

## Actual Outcome
[What actually happened?]

## Unexpected Discoveries
[What surprised us? What didn't go as planned?]

## Evidence Collected
[Data, metrics, observations, feedback that inform this learning]

## Lessons Learned
[What do we now understand better? 2-3 key insights]

## Mistakes Made
[What went wrong? What could we have done better?]

## What We Would Do Differently
[If this project ran again, what would change?]

## Related Stewardship Principles
[Which of our 11 principles does this touch?]

## Related Research
[Does this inform any active hypothesis? Which?]

## Open Questions
[What remains unanswered? What should we investigate next?]

## Confidence Level
[High / Medium / Low — how certain are we in these learnings?]

## Recommended Follow-up
[Who should know about this? What action is recommended?]
```

## Value Negative Results

The institution values discovering that an assumption was incorrect.

**This is progress, not failure.**

### Research Conduct
- Research shall not seek validation of existing beliefs
- Research shall seek understanding
- "We were wrong" is a successful outcome
- Negative results are published and celebrated

---

## Discoveries Register (DISCOVERIES.md)

Maintain a continuously updated institutional discoveries register.

Every discovery shall include:

### DISCOVERY RECORD

**Title:** Concise name of the discovery

**Summary:** 1-2 sentences on what was learned

**Evidence:** Supporting data, observations, metrics

**Confidence:** High / Medium / Low — how certain are we?

**Affected Systems:** Which features, processes, or operations does this touch?

**Affected Principles:** Which stewardship principles does this strengthen, challenge, or modify?

**Research Status:** 
- Initial observation
- Under investigation
- Replicated independently
- Contradicted (rejected)

**Whether Independently Replicated:** Yes / No / Pending

**Potential Publication Opportunities:** 
- Academic journal
- Nonprofit sector publication
- Practitioner guide
- Not for external publication

**Potential Nonprofit Benefit:** How could other nonprofits learn from this?

**Potential Broader Societal Benefit:** What could the field, AI ethics, or public benefit from this learning?

## Institutional Maturity

The institution should become wiser, not merely larger.

Knowledge should become:
- **More organized** — systematic, indexable, discoverable
- **More evidence-based** — grounded in data, not assumption
- **More reproducible** — documented clearly, others can replicate
- **More transferable** — clear enough that others can learn from it
- **More explainable** — reasoning is transparent, trade-offs are visible
- **More useful to others** — not just valuable internally, but shareable

---

## Success Metric

**One day another organization, another country, or another generation should be able to read the Discoveries Register and become stronger without ever using Daanaa.**

That is considered success.

## Implementation

**Step 1:** Create Learning Record template and DISCOVERIES.md register  
**Step 2:** Establish process for capturing learning from every major milestone  
**Step 3:** Monthly review of discoveries for principle candidates + constitutional impact  
**Step 4:** Quarterly synthesis of patterns across multiple learnings  
**Step 5:** Annual publication of curated discoveries suitable for external audience

## SLIDE 3: Why Existing Tools Fall Short

**HEADLINE:** The Rating Platform Trap

**BODY:**
- Charity Navigator, GiveWell, Candid cover < 1K orgs
- They judge mission (but you decide values)
- They center scale (rewarding big, penalizing small)
- Giving history is tracked, monetized, exposed

**VISUAL:** Three logos (Charity Navigator, GiveWell, Candid) with X marks. Below, a graphic showing a donor's giving history as a trail of digital breadcrumbs being collected.

**SPEAKER NOTE:** "The existing evaluators have a problem: they judge mission — saying which orgs are 'good' — when that's a personal choice. And they need scale to survive, so they center big organizations. Meanwhile, your giving history is tracked and sold. It's more personal than your search history, but it's treated like a commodity."

**HEADLINE:** A Discovery Layer, Not a Judge

- ✓ Verified donation links
- ✓ Private giving wallet on your device
- ✗ NOT a rating of which org is "best"
- ✗ NOT a payment processor
- ✗ NOT a social platform

**VISUAL:** Two columns. Left side (green checkmark) shows what Daanaa does. Right side (red X) shows what it doesn't. Clean, simple icons.

**SPEAKER NOTE:** "Daanaa is a discovery layer. We help you search, find context about financial health, and give directly. We never touch the money. And we never tell you which nonprofit is 'best' — that's your choice based on your values."

## SLIDE 5: How It Works

**HEADLINE:** Search → Discover → Give → Private

**BODY:**
1. **Search:** by name, cause, location, or NTEE category
2. **Discover:** see mission, financial health, peer comparison
3. **Give:** click directly to org's own giving page
4. **Stay Private:** wallet and giving history on your device only

**VISUAL:** Flow diagram with 4 steps. Icons for each: magnifying glass, lamp, heart (for giving), lock (for privacy). Arrows connecting left-to-right.

**SPEAKER NOTE:** "The flow is simple. You search for what you care about. You discover organizations you've never heard of, with context about their financial health. You click to give, and your money goes straight to them — not through us. And your giving history stays on your phone. It never leaves your device."

---

## SLIDE 6: Peer Scoring: Fairness by Design

**HEADLINE:** Small Orgs Compete with Small Orgs

**BODY:**
- Hospital (e.g., $500M) never compared to clinic ($2M)
- Clinic is scored only against similar-sized clinics
- Peer group = NTEE subcategory + revenue band + region
- A $2M clinic with 12 months of reserve is *excellent* for its scale

**VISUAL:** Two org bubbles of different sizes, separated by a divider. Left: large hospital in a group of large orgs. Right: small clinic in a group of small clinics. Both highlighted as "strong performers" within their groups.

**SPEAKER NOTE:** "Fairness is built into the score. We never compare small nonprofits to large ones as if they were playing the same game. A $2M food bank in Texas is scored only against other food banks of similar size in the South. When it has 12 months of reserves, that's excellent health *for its scale* — the meaningful signal."

## SLIDE 7: Privacy by Default

**HEADLINE:** Your Giving History Is Yours Alone

**BODY:**

- No giving activity on our servers
- No cross-device sync (your choice)
- No tracking vendors, no social pressure
- "The left hand does not know what the right hand gives"

**VISUAL:** Phone showing a locked wallet icon. Data stays inside the phone outline. Outside: a cloud (server) shown disconnected, crossed out. Simple visual.

**SPEAKER NOTE:** "Your giving history is deeply personal. It reveals your values, your communities, your financial capacity. So it stays on your device. No servers. No tracking vendors. No social pressure. You own it completely."

**Pacing:** 1–2 min per slide, slower on Slides 4–7 (the "how" section).

**Color Palette:**
- Gold (lamp accent, #D4AF37)
- Deep Navy (trust, buttons, headings)
- Cream (background)
- Cool Grey (supporting text)

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Bold, 44pt+
- Body: Regular, 24–28pt
- Subtext: Grey, 18–20pt

**Visual Style:** Clean, modern, warm. No stock photography. Use actual Daanaa interface screenshots where possible. Icons for data sources and principles.

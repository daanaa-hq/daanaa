TROUBLESHOOTING
===============

"Phase 4 not complete":
  → Run Phase 4 first: python3 scripts/phase4_semantic_verification.py

"LLM not responding":
  → Start llama-server on port 11437

"No verified orgs":
  → Phase 4 may still be running or didn't verify any orgs

"Process keeps restarting":
  → Check LLM logs, reduce BATCH_SIZE to 8, increase LLM_TIMEOUT

See docs/PHASE5.md for comprehensive troubleshooting guide.

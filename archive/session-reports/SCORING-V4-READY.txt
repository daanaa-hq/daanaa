================================================================================
                           WHAT IT SOLVES
================================================================================

THE PROBLEM:
  Traditional nonprofit ratings use one yardstick for all organizations.
  A $200K community food bank gets compared to a $2B hospital system.
  Result: small, lean, mission-focused nonprofits look bad.
  Donors see low scores and give less. The invisible 97% stay invisible.

THE SOLUTION:
  Every org measured only against its true peers (same operating model,
  same revenue band). Financial Health tiers have model-specific meanings:
  
  "Inspiring" for a direct service org means:
    "Doing remarkable work with limited resources"
  
  "Inspiring" for a foundation means:
    "Emerging foundation, building capacity"
  
  Same word, different context. Fair, honest, dignified.

THE MOAT:
  No other platform does this at scale (648K+ orgs).
  This is the competitive advantage AND the mission advantage.

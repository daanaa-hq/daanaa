## Year 3 (Months 25–36): 501(c)(3) optionality + diversified portfolio

### 501(c)(3) decision (Phase 1 Gate 10)
- Decide whether to form parallel 501(c)(3)
- Drivers FOR: bigger grant pool, donor tax-deductibility for tips
- Drivers AGAINST: governance overhead, board requirements, mission lock harder to enforce
- If FOR: file Form 1023 (full) or 1023-EZ (eligible if revenue < $50K avg)

### Diversified Year 3 portfolio
- 3–5 grants active
- 5+ sponsor relationships
- GPO marketplace revenue starting (Phase 2 if ready)
- Possibly: institutional infrastructure grant

### Year 3 funding target

## What we WILL NOT do for funding

These are mission-lock-protected:

- **No paywalls on nonprofits.** Ever.
- **No data licensing for profit.** Open Data Initiative is free.
- **No paid promotion of specific causes.** Equal treatment is structural.
- **No sponsored badges or rankings.** Scoring is deterministic.
- **No board matching fees.** No pay-for-introduction.
- **No tax summary paywall.** Acknowledgment letters are free.
- **No payment processing rake.** We don't custody money.
- **No nonprofit data sold to third parties.** Privacy by architecture.
- **No equity investors.** Mission lock incompatible with VC ownership.
- **No acquisition by for-profit incumbents.** Operating agreement prohibits.

## Funding hygiene

### Acceptance criteria for grants
- Mission alignment (civic tech, nonprofit infrastructure, philanthropy ecosystem)
- Reasonable reporting requirements
- No mission-restricting strings
- Funder has clean reputation
- Funding doesn't create dependency on single source > 40% of budget

### Acceptance criteria for sponsorships
- Mission alignment
- Disclosed publicly
- No editorial influence on MERIT content
- No special placement in directory
- No promotional obligations beyond mutual mention

### Disclosure
- All funding sources disclosed publicly
- Annual financial summary published (Phase 1+)
- Major sponsors visible on website
- Annual transparency report

## EcoMargins → MeritGiving LLC funding flow

**Phase 0 (current):**
- EcoMargins credit card charges all MERIT expenses
- All charges tagged with class/location "MERIT" in QBO
- Tracked separately for eventual transfer

**When MeritGiving LLC bank account exists:**
- EcoMargins makes capital contribution to MeritGiving (documented loan or equity, attorney-advised)
- MeritGiving reimburses EcoMargins for accumulated MERIT expenses
- Going forward: MeritGiving card pays MeritGiving expenses

**When MeritGiving generates revenue:**
- Revenue stays in MeritGiving
- No automatic distribution to EcoMargins
- Future profit distributions documented if owner takes any (mission lock favors reinvestment)

**Documentation needed (attorney-drafted):**
- Inter-entity loan agreement OR capital contribution
- Service agreement (EcoMargins provides services to MeritGiving)
- Operating agreement for MeritGiving LLC

This is the cleanest setup. CPA should sign off before money moves.

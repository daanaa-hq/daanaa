## The Question

How healthy is the American nonprofit sector? Based on 1.7M organizations' financial data: Parts are strong. Others fragile. Discovery is broken.

---

## Key Findings

### 48% of nonprofits are one crisis away from collapse
Only 52% have 3+ months operating reserves (the benchmark).

### Food banking most vulnerable
Median reserves down 5% YoY. Sector that feeds vulnerable people is underfunded.

### Growth paradox: more orgs, same money
1.7M nonprofits competing for roughly $2.5T total revenue. Average org size shrinking.

### Small organizations growing fastest
Under $150K orgs up 16% vs. 2023. Mission-driven, underfunded.

### Rural systematically under-resourced
Rural median: $380K revenue, 2.8 mo reserves, 1.2 staff
Urban median: $850K revenue, 5.2 mo reserves, 2.3 staff

### Best organizations are invisible
40% of high-performing rural nonprofits have no web presence. Donors can't find them.

## What Donors Can Do

### Skill 1: Spot healthy nonprofits in 30 seconds
- Reserves: 3+ months operating expenses?
- Stability: Revenue ±20% year-to-year?
- Clarity: Mission explainable in one sentence?

### Skill 2: Understand sector context
Compare food banks to food banks, not to universities.

### Skill 3: Find small organizations in your area
Search by geography + cause + size to discover underfunded local work.

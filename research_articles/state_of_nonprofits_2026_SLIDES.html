What You Can Do

Three skills to give better

Skill 1: Spot a Healthy Nonprofit

In 30 seconds, look for three things:

✅ Reserves: 3+ months?

  • Yes = Can weather a setback
  • <1 month = Fragile

⚠️ Revenue Stability

  • Stable (±5%) = Sustainable
  • Bouncing (±20%) = Dependent on few funders

💡 Mission Clarity

  • One sentence clear = Focused
  • Vague or many missions = Diluted focus

Skill 2: Understand Sector Context

Don't compare across sectors

Food Bank with 2.8 months

✅ Above average

(Median for food banking = 2.8)

Health Org with 2.8 months

🔴 Below average & at risk

(Median for health = 6.2)

Rule: Compare within sectors, not across

Skill 3: Find Good Organizations in Your Area

Most platforms show the biggest organizations

We show all 1.7M

Search by:

  1. Cause (Food, Education, Health, Arts, etc.)
  2. Geography (Your zip code, county, state)
  3. Financial Health (Stable reserves, growing revenue)
  4. Read Their Story (What do they actually do?)

What the Data Can't Tell You

Important limitations

What We Can Measure

✅ Financial health (reserves, revenue, size)

✅ Sector trends (are orgs growing/shrinking?)

✅ Geographic distribution (where are nonprofits?)

We CANNOT measure from financials alone:

❌ Whether org actually accomplishes its mission

❌ Whether org is well-managed

❌ Whether org is right for you

Financial Health is Necessary

Not Sufficient

Organization with great reserves could be poorly run

Organization with tight reserves could be beautifully effective

Use this data as a starting point, not a final verdict

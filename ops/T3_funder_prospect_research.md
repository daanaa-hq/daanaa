## Strategy by Funding Stage

### Stage 1 (G0 — Now, pre-501c3 approval)

**Challenge:** Most foundations require 501(c)(3) status. EcoMargins is for-profit.

**Options:**
1. **Fiscal sponsor path (Social Good Fund, Players Philanthropy Fund):** Fast-track access to grants within 1–2 weeks; 6–10% fee
2. **File 501(c)(3) immediately:** 2–6 month IRS approval; apply while pending (most foundations accept "pending status")
3. **Draper Richards Kaplan:** Explicitly accepts for-profits + nonprofits; year-round rolling deadline

**Recommendation:** Pursue DRK (rolling) + fiscal sponsor (fast-track) in parallel while 501(c)(3) approval is in process.

### Stage 2 (G1 — After attorney consultation)

Confirm fiscal sponsor decision + 501(c)(3) timeline with attorney. Lock in which funders to prioritize.

### Stage 3 (G2 — Actively fundraising)

- **High priority:** DRK (rolling), Trust for Civic Life (June invite, large award)
- **Medium:** Knight Foundation (subscribe to RFP calendar)
- **Upcoming:** Echoing Green 2027 (Oct deadline est.), Fast Forward 2027 (July deadline est.)

### Stage 4 (G3+)

Once 501(c)(3) approved + letter service live, unlock foundation partnerships (Candid, Every.org, others).

## Funder Deep-Dives

### Draper Richards Kaplan (DRK) Foundation ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ PRIORITY

**What they do:** $300K over 3 years to early-stage social impact orgs (2–5 years old) + $500K in-kind support.

**Who they fund:** Independent nonprofits, B-corps, for-profits, fiscally sponsored orgs. No 501(c)(3) requirement.

**Daanaa fit:** 
- ✅ Solves urgent problem (invisible 97% of nonprofits + evidence-based trust)
- ✅ Systems-thinking (leverages existing IRS data, donor platforms, org workflows)
- ✅ Scalable software-based solution
- ✅ Underserved populations (small nonprofits)
- ❓ 2–5 year stage: Daanaa is ~6 months old (post-MVP); may be early for them

**Application process:** Year-round, no deadline. Send initial inquiry + 1-page LOI.

**Typical decision timeline:** 2–3 months from LOI to decision.

**Competitiveness:** ~20 selected from 2,200 annual applications (~0.9% acceptance).

**Next step:** Draft DRK LOI immediately (regardless of 501(c)(3) status); submit week 1.

### Trust for Civic Life ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ STRATEGIC

**What they do:** $250–500K over 3 years; 20–25 awards annual.

**Who they fund:** 501(c)(3) only; must be invited to apply (no cold submissions).

**Daanaa fit:**
- ✅✅ Civic engagement + trust signals
- ✅ Aligns with "raising the bar" for small org discovery
- ❓ Requires 501(c)(3) (must apply as sole org, not EcoMargins)

**Application window:** Feb–June each year (applications due in Feb for July awards).

**How to get invited:** Contact program director directly + mention Candid/Every.org partnerships, traction data (1.97M orgs, 150+ verified donate links, 3.1M pageviews). Ask: "Can we be considered for the 2027 Civic Hub cycle?"

**Next step:** Research program director contact; send warm outreach email + traction brief.

**Application process:** Subscribe to RFP updates. When they open a relevant challenge, they give 4–8 week deadlines.

**What they do:** $50K–$500K for AI + data for public good. $75.8M announced in 2026 grants.

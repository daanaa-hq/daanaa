# T2 Attorney Prep: 50-State Charitable Solicitation Overview

**Purpose:** Cut attorney bill by confirming which state registrations Daanaa actually needs before filing.  
**For:** Initial attorney consultation (cut 4-6 hours of "explain which states" from the bill).

---

## Quick Facts

- **39 states + DC** require charitable registration before soliciting donations in that state
- **11 states** have no registration requirement (or no enforcement): ID, IL, IN, MI, MO, MS, NY, RI, VT, WY, and either OK or TX (varies by source)
- **Key thresholds:**
  - Most states: $5K–$25K annual fundraising triggers registration
  - A few (CA, NY, IL) have lower thresholds or broader definitions
  - **Critical:** Registration happens state-by-state as you hit the threshold — not all-at-once

---

## Daanaa's Solicitation Posture (starting Jan 2026 G2)

**Mechanism:** Direct donate links to individual nonprofit processors (Every.org, PayPal, Stripe, Blackbaud) or EIN-keyed donation router. **Daanaa never holds donor money.**

**This means:** You are "facilitating" donations on behalf of the org, not "soliciting" in the traditional sense. Many states carve out platform/aggregator exceptions, but interpretation varies.

**Legal reality:** Even with the aggregator angle, most attorneys recommend registering in all states where you expect *material* user volume or donations routed through your platform. Better safe than caught in a gray zone.

## Costs & Timeline

**Registration cost (one-time + annual):**
- **Budget:** $3–8K across ~20–30 strategic states (CA, NY, TX, MA, IL, WA, OR, VA + any where you have volunteer base or org-partners)
- **Expedited:** Most states have fast-track ($50–200 extra) if it matters
- **Annual renewal:** $100–500/state — rollup budget ~$5K/year post-launch

**Recommended tier 1 (must-do):** CA, NY, IL, TX, MA, WA, OR, VA, PA, FL, NC, GA, OH (13 states covering ~40% of US nonprofits by location)

**Recommended tier 2 (if revenue >$50K/yr):** Add MN, CO, AZ, NJ, CT, MD, MI (another 7 states)

**Timeline:**
- G1 attorney consultation: confirm tier 1 states for Daanaa's model
- Pre-launch: file tier 1 (4–8 weeks, some states faster)

## Special Cases (mention to attorney)

1. **Fiscal sponsorship path:** If Daanaa becomes a fiscal sponsor or partner org for nonprofits, registration requirements may shift. Confirm with attorney whether your own 501(c)(3) status exempts certain filings.
2. **Platform liability:** Because you're routing to third-party processors and EINs, insurance + terms of service matter. Attorney should weigh whether you're a "conduit" (lower liability) vs "fundraiser" (higher).
3. **State-specific quirks:**
   - **CA:** Needs Form CT-1 even for aggregator platforms; renewal every calendar year
   - **NY:** Separate Application #6 for online fundraising platforms; very thorough
   - **TX:** Online fundraising not explicitly mentioned in statute; some say no requirement, but most counsel file anyway
4. **COVID/remote:** Most states now accept online-only registration; no physical office required.

## Questions for Attorney

1. Does routing donations via third-party processors (not holding funds) place Daanaa in a "conduit" exemption or reduce our solicitation liability?
2. What is our real-world liability if we skip registration in low-threshold states (ND $1K, SD $1K) but get surprised by user volume?
3. Does a single §501(c)(3) Daanaa entity need *separate* registrations per business line (e.g., letter service as a separate "solicitation" program)?
4. Fiscal sponsorship: if we become a FS for nonprofits later, do we need to re-register as an intermediary in each state?

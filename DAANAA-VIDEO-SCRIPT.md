# Daanaa: 90-Second Explainer Video Script

---

## [0–10s] HOOK
**VOICEOVER:** "1.8 million nonprofits in America. Fewer than 50,000 you've heard of."

**VISUAL:** Montage of org logos flashing across a dark screen. The familiar ones (Red Cross, Salvation Army, etc.) light up and glow. Then the screen floods with thousands of unknown names, all visible at once. The lamp icon fades in, gently illuminating the crowd.

**VOICEOVER:** "The other 97%? Invisible."

---

## [10–25s] PROBLEM
**VOICEOVER:** "You want to give. So you search. You find five names. Big names. Safe names."

**VISUAL:** Close-up of Google search "nonprofits to donate to." Results show the same household names. Donor scrolls, finds the same ones again. Slightly frustrated expression.

**VOICEOVER:** "What if the work you care about isn't famous? What if it's just good?"

**VISUAL:** Cut to overhead map. Thousands of small dots scattered across a city, all dark. A few big dots glow bright. Camera pulls back.

---

## [25–50s] THE CONCEPT
**VOICEOVER:** "Meet Daanaa. It's simple."

**VISUAL:** Clean directory interface. A donor types "climate action" into the search box.

**VOICEOVER:** "Search for what you care about. Discover an organization you've never heard of."

**VISUAL:** Search results appear. An unfamiliar org emerges. Name, location, mission, peer score.

**VOICEOVER:** "See who they are. Check their financial health relative to similar orgs."

**VISUAL:** Peer group visualization: this org highlighted in the middle of a group of similar-sized organizations, not compared to giants.

**VOICEOVER:** "Click to give. Money goes straight to them. Not through us."

**VISUAL:** A hand clicking a donate button. The page opens on the org's own giving page (Donorbox, Stripe, PayPal).

**VOICEOVER:** "Your giving history stays private. On your phone. Never on our servers."

**VISUAL:** Phone screen with a saved wallet. The phone icon locks. No data leaves the device.

---

## [50–70s] DIFFERENTIATION
**VOICEOVER:** "Two things make us different."

**VISUAL:** Split screen. Left: enormous hospital next to small community clinic. Both labeled.

**VOICEOVER:** "First: we never compare small to giant. A clinic with $2M revenue competes only against similar clinics. Fairness, not football."

**VISUAL:** The clinic now highlighted only against other clinics. The hospital disappears. Breathing room.

**VOICEOVER:** "Second: your giving is yours alone. No tracking. No social pressure. No public performance."

**VISUAL:** Person sitting quietly on a couch, looking at their phone, making a choice. No audience. No thumbs-up icons.

---

## [70–90s] CALL TO ACTION
**VOICEOVER:** "Find the nonprofit you've never heard of. Visit daanaa.org."

**VISUAL:** Homepage appears. Clean, warm, welcoming. Lamp mark glowing softly. Directory search box ready.

**VOICEOVER:** "An old principle says: so easy, the left hand does not know what the right hand gives."

**VISUAL:** Final scene: sun rising on a landscape. Thousands of small lights coming into view. Each one small, real, doing good.

**VOICEOVER:** "That's Daanaa."

**VISUAL:** Logo and daanaa.org. Fade to black.

---

### Production Notes

**Tone:** Warm, human, unhurried. No urgency. This is discovery, not a sales pitch.

**Color palette:** Warm gold (the lamp), deep navy (trust), cream (approachable), cool grey (neutral).

**Music:** Ambient, subtle. Builds gently from 0–50s, then settles into something quiet and reflective for the close.

**Pacing:** Slow enough to read. Let each visual breathe. No jump-cuts.

**Geography:** The org examples should be real small orgs (e.g., "Green Thumb Urban Gardens" or "Hermansky-Pudlak Syndrome Network") to make the invisible 97% feel concrete.

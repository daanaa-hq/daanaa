# Daanaa Impact Network — Vendor and Partner Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-06-14
**Operated by:** EcoMargins Consulting LLC ("Daanaa")
**Applies to:** All community partners, network partners, and vendors participating in the Daanaa Impact Network

---

## What the Daanaa Impact Network is

Daanaa is an independent nonprofit discovery platform. The Daanaa Impact Network is a voluntary program that connects businesses and service providers with the nonprofit organizations that use Daanaa to manage their public profile.

The goal of the network is simple: make it easier for nonprofits to access services, tools, and resources at fair prices. Participation is a values commitment, not a commercial transaction. Vendors join because they believe in supporting the sector. Nonprofits benefit because their work is harder and more resource-constrained than most businesses realize.

**The network has two participation tiers:**

- **Community Partners** — Any business that offers a genuine benefit to nonprofits. No formal contract required. Applications are reviewed by Daanaa staff and activated at Daanaa's discretion.
- **Network Partners** — Businesses that have entered into a formal Collective Action Framework (CAF) agreement with EcoMargins Consulting LLC. Network partners agree to reporting requirements and milestone-based pricing commitments in exchange for deeper network access and co-marketing.

---

## What vendors and partners agree to

By applying to or participating in the Daanaa Impact Network, you agree to the following:

**1. Accuracy.** Every offer, discount, code, or benefit you list must be real, currently available, and honored without additional barriers not disclosed in your listing. If a deal expires, changes, or cannot be fulfilled, you are responsible for notifying Daanaa immediately so the listing can be updated or removed.

**2. Honest description.** Your listing must describe what you offer, what the benefit is, and how a nonprofit accesses it. Inflated original prices, fake discounts, and bait-and-switch terms are grounds for immediate removal.

**3. No solicitation of donations.** Participation in the Daanaa Impact Network does not authorize you to solicit charitable contributions on Daanaa's behalf or in Daanaa's name. You are not a fundraising agent for Daanaa or for any nonprofit. Any fundraising activity remains your own and must comply with all applicable charitable solicitation laws.

**4. Respect for nonprofits.** Nonprofits that access your benefits through Daanaa must not be enrolled in marketing lists, retargeted, or contacted for sales purposes beyond the service relationship they initiate. You may not use Daanaa membership to build prospect lists or infer giving capacity from the organizations you serve.

**5. Values alignment.** Daanaa operates under a Founding Stewardship Commitment (see `STEWARDSHIP.md`). Vendors whose business practices are in material conflict with those principles — including but not limited to misleading claims, predatory pricing, or practices harmful to the nonprofit sector — are not eligible to participate and may be removed.

---

## What Daanaa commits to

**Independence is non-negotiable.** Vendor participation — including the payment of any fees — never affects how an organization appears in Daanaa search results, its financial context score, its tier, its visibility ranking, or any other trust signal on the platform. No vendor may pay for placement. No vendor relationship grants influence over platform standards or individual organization outcomes. This principle is absolute and derives directly from Principle 7 of the Founding Stewardship Commitment.

**Fair review.** Applications are reviewed on values fit and benefit quality, not on the size of the vendor. A small local vendor offering a genuine benefit will be considered on equal terms with a national provider.

**Disclosure.** Daanaa discloses to nonprofits that the network includes vendor relationships and that those relationships contribute to platform sustainability. Vendor relationships are never presented as endorsements of individual organizations.

**Reasonable notice.** If Daanaa removes a vendor listing that was active in good faith, Daanaa will provide notice and a reasonable opportunity to correct the issue before final removal, unless the violation involves fraud, misrepresentation, or harm to nonprofits.

---

## Data and privacy

Daanaa does not share donor data, giving history, wallet contents, or any user-level behavioral data with vendors. Ever.

When a nonprofit accesses a vendor benefit through Daanaa, the only information Daanaa may confirm to the vendor for verification purposes is the organization's name, EIN, and claimed status on the platform. No financial data, no giving data, no donor information.

Vendors may not use EIN-level information obtained through the Daanaa Impact Network to build commercial databases, enrich third-party lists, or resell organization data. This obligation survives the end of your participation in the network.

---

## Daanaa's role and limits

Daanaa is the convener of the network. Daanaa is not a party to any transaction between a vendor and a nonprofit. When a nonprofit uses a code, accesses a benefit, or enters a service relationship with a vendor, that relationship is directly between the nonprofit and the vendor.

**Daanaa makes no warranty** — express, implied, or otherwise — that any listed benefit is available, accurate, of merchantable quality, or fit for any particular purpose. Nonprofits use vendor benefits at their own discretion. Daanaa is not responsible for vendor service quality, fulfillment failures, pricing changes, or disputes between vendors and nonprofits.

Daanaa is not an agent, broker, or representative of any vendor. Daanaa does not receive commissions on transactions between vendors and nonprofits unless separately disclosed and agreed upon in a written Network Partner agreement.

## Liability and indemnification

You are responsible for the accuracy and legality of every listing you submit to the Daanaa Impact Network.

You agree to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless EcoMargins Consulting LLC, its officers, agents, and representatives from any claim, liability, loss, damage, or expense (including reasonable legal fees) arising from: (a) the inaccuracy or misrepresentation of your listing; (b) your failure to fulfill a listed offer; (c) your use of nonprofit data obtained through the network; or (d) any violation of applicable law in connection with your participation.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, EcoMargins Consulting LLC's total liability to any vendor in connection with the Daanaa Impact Network shall not exceed the fees paid by that vendor to EcoMargins Consulting LLC in the twelve months preceding the claim. Community partners who participate at no cost acknowledge that Daanaa's liability is zero.

## Removal and disputes

Daanaa may remove any vendor listing at any time, for any reason, including but not limited to values misalignment, inaccurate listings, nonprofit complaints, or changes in platform direction.

If you believe a removal was made in error, you may request a review by writing to partners@daanaa.org. Daanaa will respond within ten business days. The outcome of that review is final.

Daanaa does not mediate disputes between vendors and nonprofits. Those disputes are between the parties directly.

## Consulting clients of the operating company

EcoMargins Consulting LLC, which operates Daanaa, also provides paid
consulting services under its own name to businesses and, on request, to
nonprofits. The following rules govern that relationship and apply in
addition to everything above:

**1. Client status confers nothing on Daanaa.** Being a consulting client of
EcoMargins — past, present, or prospective — never affects any organization's
score, visibility, ranking, search placement, description, or any other
outcome on the Daanaa platform. The same published methodology runs for
clients and non-clients alike. This mirrors, for our own clients, the
independence rule that applies to vendors.

**2. Affiliations are disclosed internally.** When an EcoMargins client (or
its principals) has a known affiliation with a nonprofit listed on Daanaa —
as founder, board member, funder, or corporate parent — that affiliation is
recorded in the Daanaa compliance log. The purpose is auditability: anyone
reviewing platform outcomes for that organization can verify the commercial
relationship had no effect.

**3. No platform data flows to consulting.** Information entrusted to Daanaa
(claim contacts, custom content, platform behavior — Tier 2 under Library
Document 011) is never available to EcoMargins consulting work, never used
to identify prospects, and never used to shape a pitch. Consulting prospect
research is limited to public records and published data, exactly as
available to any member of the public.

**4. The door opens from the inside only.** Daanaa never markets, suggests,
or refers consulting services within the platform. A nonprofit that wants
EcoMargins' help must initiate the request itself through an explicit
opt-in. One opt-in covers one conversation, not a marketing relationship.

**5. Quarterly self-audit.** Once per quarter, a written entry in the
STEWARDSHIP.md compliance log answers: did any Tier 2 information influence
any EcoMargins activity this quarter? This audit is mandatory whether or not
any consulting activity occurred.

## Connection to the Founding Stewardship Commitment

This policy is a specific application of the Daanaa Founding Stewardship Commitment. In any conflict between this policy and that commitment, the Stewardship Commitment governs.

The principles most directly relevant to vendor participation are:

- **Principle 1 (Mission before growth):** Vendor revenue cannot override the platform's purpose.
- **Principle 2 (Privacy):** No donor data, giving history, or user behavior reaches vendors.
- **Principle 3 (Trust signals are evidence-based):** Vendor relationships are never presented as endorsements or trust indicators.
- **Principle 7 (Independence):** Vendor participation does not affect any organization's visibility, score, or ranking. Full stop.

# 💎 Daanaa Governance Framework — Public Fork Registry

**What is this?**

This is a public registry of organizations that have forked and adapted the Daanaa governance framework. It's proof of replicability and community ownership.

Each entry shows: who they are, how they adapted it, and what they learned.

---

## How to Add Your Fork

1. Fork the Daanaa governance framework (GitHub template)
2. Customize STEWARDSHIP.md for your organization
3. Submit a PR to add your entry below (or email hello@daanaa.org)
4. We'll celebrate your adoption publicly

## Questions?

**How do I customize this for my team?** → See [QUICKSTART_24HOUR.md](../docs/QUICKSTART_24HOUR.md)

**Can I use parts of this framework but not all 11 principles?** → Yes, adapt freely. We only ask you credit the source.

**What if I disagree with a principle?** → Great! Modify it for your context. The point is principles + automation + transparency, not our specific 11.

**How do I stay updated as the framework evolves?** → Watch the [Daanaa GitHub](https://github.com/daanaa) or join our community Slack.

**Can I fork this without public attribution?** → Yes, the framework is CC0 (public domain). Attribution appreciated but not required.

# BOARD MEMORANDUM
**TO:** Daanaa Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Founder  
**DATE:** July 22, 2026  
**RE:** Authorization for Student Community-Service Pilot Program

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The board is requested to authorize a controlled 90-day development and Houston pilot of Daanaa's student community-service capability. This initiative converts Daanaa's nonprofit discovery infrastructure into a practical tool for student volunteering, nonprofit operations, and school documentation—while maintaining strict privacy, safety, and verification standards.

**Authorization Requested:**
- Allocation of product, engineering, privacy, legal, partnership, and operations resources
- Development of student service, verification, and certificate capabilities  
- Pilot enrollment in Houston with 3-5 schools and 20 participating nonprofits
- Report back to board with outcomes and expansion recommendation

**Commitment Level:** Pilot only. No national expansion, government affiliation, or expensive certification without future board approval.

---

## THE OPPORTUNITY

### The Problem
- **Students** need legitimate volunteer opportunities and reliable ways to document service
- **Nonprofits** need volunteers but lack resources to recruit, manage, and verify participation at scale
- **Schools** need trustworthy records beyond student-maintained spreadsheets
- **Daanaa** currently provides discovery but no mechanism for sustained engagement or verification

### The Solution
Daanaa bridges these gaps by adding:
1. Structured opportunity discovery for students (by location, cause, need)
2. Service log with hour recording and supervisor verification
3. Downloadable verified service record with audit trail
4. Tools for nonprofits to manage student participation
5. Reporting for schools and organizations

## PRIVACY & SAFETY BY DESIGN

### Core Protections
- **No public student profiles.** Student activity and service records not visible to other users
- **No minor-to-minor messaging.** All communication is student-nonprofit or student-school
- **Adult-to-minor protections.** Any contact from nonprofit supervisors goes through school
- **Minimal data collection.** Name, age, school affiliation, service commitment only
- **Parental consent.** Required for students under 18
- **Exportable data.** Students/schools own and can extract their records
- **COPPA compliance.** Designed for 13+ without parental consent; under 13 requires guardian approval

### Before Pilot Launch
✅ Student consent templates reviewed and approved  
✅ School data-sharing agreements drafted  
✅ Parent consent procedures documented  
✅ Youth-safety procedures established  
✅ Data retention/deletion protocols defined  
✅ Access and correction procedures published  

### Legal Review Milestones
- COPPA compliance (13+/under-13 pathways)
- FERPA compliance (school data sharing)
- State student-privacy laws (Texas baseline)
- Minor-protection procedures

## SERVICE VERIFICATION STANDARD

### Daanaa Verified Service Record Standard
**Published requirement defining:**

**Eligible Service:**
- Unpaid volunteer work benefiting a qualified nonprofit
- Clearly defined start and end time
- Supervised by authorized nonprofit representative

**Supervisor Requirements:**
- Authorized by nonprofit organization
- Background check conducted by nonprofit (per their policy)
- Conflict-of-interest disclosure

**Documentation:**
- Student submits: date, hours, activity description, supervisor name
- Supervisor confirms: hours worked, activity, no conflicts
- Daanaa records: submission date, verification date, audit trail

**Fraud Prevention:**
- Duplicate-hour detection (same student, same org, same date)
- Outlier flags (unusually long shifts or frequency)
- Nonprofit supervisor audit trail
- Random manual audits (5-10% of submissions)

**Dispute Resolution:**
- Student can dispute rejected hours
- Nonprofit can correct erroneous submissions
- 30-day correction window
- Escalation process for unresolved disputes

**Record Retention:**
- Verified records retained per student graduation
- Deleted upon student request (data rights)
- Aggregate statistics retained for impact reporting

**Certificate Revocation:**
- Detected fraud triggers revocation
- Student can request correction
- Public record of revocation status

### Certificate Language (Non-Government)
```
This certificate recognizes verified community service 
completed by [Student Name] through Daanaa's Student 
Community Service Program. The service hours listed have 
been verified by [Organization Name] and recorded by Daanaa 
using our published verification standard.

This certificate is issued by Daanaa, a nonprofit-discovery 
platform. It does not imply government endorsement, 
presidential program affiliation, or any credential other 
than verified service hours recorded by this organization.

[Unique Validation Number] can be verified at daanaa.org/verify
```

**Revenue Model Guardrail:** Post-pilot revenue models will be evaluated by the board subject to Charter Promise #5 constraint: **core student service (discovery, logging, verification, certification) remains free.** Only advanced features beyond the core service may be monetized, and any such change requires explicit board authorization to maintain Charter compliance.

- Houston allows focus (single metro area, known nonprofit ecosystem, local school partners)

## BOARD RESOLUTION

### Resolved:

The board authorizes management to allocate appropriate product, engineering, privacy, legal, partnership, and program resources to design and conduct a controlled Houston pilot of Daanaa's student community-service capability.

**Conditions of Authorization:**

1. **Before enrolling students,** management shall establish and document:
   - Student consent procedures compliant with COPPA and FERPA
   - Parental/guardian consent mechanisms for students under 18
   - Youth-safety procedures and adult-to-minor protections
   - Data retention, access, and deletion policies
   - Dispute resolution procedures

2. **Before pilot launch,** management shall complete:
   - Independent security review
   - WCAG 2.2 Level AA accessibility verification
   - Legal review of COPPA, FERPA, and applicable state laws
   - SOC 2 readiness design (certification deferred)
   - Nonprofit and school partner agreements

3. **Student records and certificates** shall comply with the published Daanaa Verified Service Record Standard, which shall clearly identify the certificate as issued by Daanaa and shall not imply government endorsement or affiliation with the Presidential Volunteer Service Award or any government program.

4. **Data collected during the pilot** shall be retained for evaluation and impact reporting but shall not be used for purposes beyond the pilot evaluation without further board approval.

5. **Upon pilot completion** (approximately week 23), management shall return to the board with:
   - Pilot outcomes against defined metrics
   - Identified risks and mitigation strategies
   - Operating costs and scalability assessment
   - Partnership opportunities and expansion recommendations
   - Recommendation on next steps (expand, modify, discontinue, or prepare for certification)

6. **No material expansion beyond Houston, no government affiliation claims, and no major certification expenditure** shall proceed without explicit board authorization following the pilot evaluation.

---

## CONCLUSION

This initiative builds directly on Daanaa's existing nonprofit-discovery infrastructure, provides practical value to students, nonprofits, and schools, and gives us a responsible way to test whether discovery can be converted into sustained community participation.

The pilot scope is limited, the privacy and safety guardrails are strict, and the board maintains full control over expansion decisions. The authorization enables us to determine—not to guarantee—that this capability can increase platform utilization, strengthen nonprofit operations, support student development, and create measurable community impact.

I recommend board approval.

## Key Changes That Resolved Board Concerns

### 1. Charter #5 Guardrail on Future Revenue
**Original concern (Impact Investor):** "Are you charging schools or nonprofits for this? How do we ensure this doesn't drift away from free platform?"

**Updated memo language added:**
> "Post-pilot revenue models will be evaluated by the board subject to Charter Promise #5 constraint: **core student service remains free.** Only advanced features beyond the core service may be monetized, and any such change requires explicit board authorization to maintain Charter compliance."

**Board reaction:** 
- **Impact Investor:** "Now I see the guardrail. Core stays free. Advanced features need board approval. That's how you prevent Charter drift."
- **Chair:** "This is explicit enough for future board decisions. If someone proposes 'Daanaa+ student discovery' as paid tier, we'll reference this commitment."
- **Privacy Advisor:** "This language also prevents sneaking in data monetization. Approved."

---

### 2. Vendor Firewall on Partnership Data
**Original concern (Privacy Advisor):** "What if we partner with AmeriCorps or Points of Light and they want student data?"

**Updated memo language added (to Privacy Advisor response):**
> "No data sharing with vendors, partners, or any commercial entity, regardless of partnership depth. Partnerships are operational only—nonprofit recruiting, school coordination—never data-access relationships."

**Board reaction:**
- **Privacy Advisor:** "That's the language I needed to see. Operational partnerships only. Student data firewall is structural."
- **Nonprofit Operations Director:** "But how do we work with national volunteer networks without sharing data?"
- **Founder:** "Good question for week-2 legal review. We may route through aggregates only ('X students served Y orgs') without exposing individual records."
- **Chair:** "Make that explicit in your Charter compliance document. 'No individual-level data sharing, aggregates only for impact reporting.'"

---

### 3. COPPA 13+ as Hard Boundary (Not Flexible)
**Original concern (Education Expert):** "What if schools want to enroll 12-year-olds? Are we locking ourselves out of a segment?"

**Updated memo language added:**
> "Pilot phase 1 is 13+ only. No enrollment under 13 will occur without explicit legal clearance. Board will see legal findings in week-9 update."

**Board reaction:**
- **Education Expert:** "Okay. That's conservative but smart. And week 9 you'll tell us if under-13 is even possible?"
- **Founder:** "Yes. Week 2 legal review determines feasibility. If it's legally sound and low-risk, we propose it for board discussion before pilot expansion."
- **Chair:** "Good discipline. Hard boundary, not a 'maybe.'"

## Unanimous Board Resolution (Revised)

**Resolved:** The board authorizes management to allocate appropriate resources to design and conduct a controlled Houston pilot of Daanaa's student community-service capability, subject to:

1. **Legal Review Completion:** COPPA/FERPA compliance confirmed before student enrollment
2. **Charter Guardrails:** All revenue models and partnerships subject to Charter Promises #3 and #5 constraints
3. **Hard Boundary on Age:** Pilot phase 1 is 13+ only; no expansion to under-13 without legal clearance and board approval
4. **Board Update at Week 9:** Adoption metrics and early-exit assessment
5. **Data Firewall:** No student data sharing with vendors or commercial partners; operational partnerships only
6. **Board Authorization Required:** For any expansion beyond Houston, any monetization of core service, or any material Charter changes
7. **Post-Pilot Evaluation:** Full report with outcomes and expansion recommendation (board decides next steps)

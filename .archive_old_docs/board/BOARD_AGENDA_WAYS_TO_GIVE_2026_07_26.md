## Executive Summary

**Proposal:** Expand Daanaa's giving methods from 2 (direct links, DAF) to 9, across 3 phases over 12 weeks.

**Impact:** Enables ~20% of donors to give via their preferred method; educates all donors; maintains Daanaa's independence + donor autonomy.

**Risk:** LOW legal (all link-based), MEDIUM operational (tax law volatility). Mitigated by 7 guardrails.

**Effort:** ~16.5 human-hours + ~7 CC-hours (1.5h/week).

**Board decisions required:** 8 strategic + 3 governance gates.

## Daanaa's North Star (Key Principle)

**Daanaa is NOT:**
- Payment processor
- Transaction intermediary
- Advisor
- Consultant
- Tax expert
- Decision-maker

**Daanaa IS:**
- Public information aggregator
- Link to official sources (IRS.gov, SEC.gov)
- Respecter of donor autonomy (their funds, their intent, their decision)

**Every page ends with:** "Your decision is yours alone. We don't advise or recommend — we just show options and link to authorities."

## Legal Protection (7 Guardrails)

| Guardrail | Effect | Risk Reduction |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|
| Link-only model (no derived tax advice) | Shifts authority to IRS, not Daanaa | 95% |
| Explicit tax disclaimer | Shows we never claimed expertise | 60% |
| Expert review gate (IRS counsel, CPA, legal) | Catches errors before publication | 80% |
| Data freshness (501c3 <30 days) | Prevents stale data liability | 75% |
| Crypto-specific guardrails (fraud warning, wallet verification) | Protects against scammers | 85% |
| Quarterly review cycle | Catches IRS guidance changes quickly | 40% |
| Audit trail + documentation | Proves due diligence if sued | 50% (not a defense, but lowers settlement pressure) |

## Questions the Board Should Ask

1. **"Why not just avoid this entirely?"** — Because ~20% of donors prefer these methods; staying silent doesn't protect us, educating clearly does.

2. **"What if IRS guidance changes mid-stream?"** — Quarterly review cycle catches updates; we link to IRS (not derive), so we're insulated from interpretation drift.

3. **"Isn't recommending giving methods risky?"** — We don't recommend. We show all methods equally and defer to authorities. That's the protection.

4. **"What if a donor sues saying we advised them?"** — Expert review memo + disclaimer screenshot + IRS link = case likely dismissed. Audit trail proves due diligence.

5. **"Why crypto if it's so risky?"** — Crypto donors exist; they'll give anyway. Educating them safely is better than ignoring them. Guardrails minimize fraud/scam risk.

6. **"Why not just pay a consultant to do this?"** — These docs represent the strategy already. Build is straightforward; expert review is the compliance gate, not the discovery.

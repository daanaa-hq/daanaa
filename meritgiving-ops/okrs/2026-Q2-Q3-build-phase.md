# OKRs: Q2 → Q3 2026 (Build Phase, Weeks 1–24)

**Period:** May 19, 2026 → November 10, 2026 (24 weeks)
**Phase:** 0 (Directory + Badges + Tip Jar, no transactions)
**Owner:** strategy-lead, reviewed weekly by CEO

This is a 6-month plan, not a typical quarterly plan, because Phase 0 is one cohesive build.

---

## Objective 1: Ship Phase 0 publicly

**Description:** Public launch of meritgiving.org with 100% of IRS-listed 501(c)(3) orgs in the directory.

- **KR 1.1:** Public launch by Week 24 (Nov 10, 2026)
- **KR 1.2:** All 7 gates passed before launch (Foundation, Credits, Data, Halfway Mirror, Trust, Pre-Launch, Launch)
- **KR 1.3:** 1.8M+ EINs queryable in directory
- **KR 1.4:** 100% of profile pages render mobile-first with Core Web Vitals green
- **KR 1.5:** WCAG 2.1 AA compliance verified

## Objective 2: Build the trust foundation

**Description:** Legal, accounting, and security infrastructure that holds under scrutiny.

- **KR 2.1:** LLC formation complete (EcoMargins → MeritGiving LLC structure decided + executed)
- **KR 2.2:** Tech E&O + Cyber liability policies bound before launch
- **KR 2.3:** ToS, Privacy Policy, Tip Disclosure, Data Credits attorney-reviewed
- **KR 2.4:** Quarterly CPA engagement live with clean books
- **KR 2.5:** Risk register with all 28 risks tracked and monthly-reviewed
- **KR 2.6:** Vulnerability disclosure policy + security.txt published

## Objective 3: Earn an audience

**Description:** Build the people who will care about MERIT before launch.

- **KR 3.1:** Newsletter at 500+ subscribers by Week 24
- **KR 3.2:** First sector report ("The Invisible Majority") published and distributed
- **KR 3.3:** 20+ user research conversations completed (10 nonprofit EDs, 10 donors)
- **KR 3.4:** 3+ advisors confirmed and engaged
- **KR 3.5:** Build-in-public log published weekly with no gaps
- **KR 3.6:** 5+ civic-tech / nonprofit community conversations established

## Objective 4: Operate as a real organization

**Description:** The org structure functions, not just exists.

- **KR 4.1:** All 10 departments active with department-head agents operating
- **KR 4.2:** Daily morning brief delivered on schedule for 90%+ of days
- **KR 4.3:** All planned worker agents (Phase 0 set) running on schedule
- **KR 4.4:** Monthly reviews completed on time for all 6 months
- **KR 4.5:** Decision log: 20+ ADRs published
- **KR 4.6:** Operating rhythm sustainable (no burnout signals at retros)

## Objective 5: Secure the resources

**Description:** Credits, sponsors, early traction signals to support Phase 1. Current burn is near-zero (owned hardware). Sequence: ship → live site → fundraise against something real.

**Bootstrap milestones (no cash required, do these first):**
- [ ] Soft launch live (DNS + HTTPS working)
- [ ] Attorney consult completed via pro bono path (law school clinic / state bar)
- [ ] All 5 credit applications submitted (Day 1 of OKR period)
- [ ] First grant application drafted with live site URL as proof point

**Standard KRs:**
- **KR 5.1:** 3+ credit programs approved (out of 5 applied)
- **KR 5.2:** Total credit value > $10K secured
- **KR 5.3:** Monthly burn maintained < $300
- **KR 5.4:** At least 1 sponsor relationship initiated (mission-aligned)
- **KR 5.5:** 2+ grant applications drafted (Knight, Mozilla, local community foundations as Round 1; Omidyar, Schmidt, McGovern as Round 2 post-sector-report)

## Stretch goals (not committed, but tracked)

- First grant funded by Week 24
- First press mention in sector outlet
- Open-source one MERIT-built MCP server (PPGF, IRS BMF, ProPublica)
- One conference acceptance for Phase 1 speaking
- Anthropic Startup Program approval at meaningful credit level
- 1,000+ newsletter subscribers

## What we are explicitly NOT doing this period

- No transactions on platform
- No nonprofit accounts / claim flow (Phase 1)
- No GPO marketplace (Phase 2)
- No paid advertising
- No 501(c)(3) formation (only LLC for now)
- No team expansion beyond contractors as needed
- No partnership commitments beyond credits and advisors
- No public API access until launch

---

## Weekly tracking

Each Friday at retro, score each KR:
- 🟢 On track (≥70% confidence by deadline)
- 🟡 At risk (30–70%)
- 🔴 Off track (<30%)

If any KR is 🔴 for 2 consecutive weeks, escalate to CEO for decision: accelerate, deprioritize, or accept slippage.

- Led by: Finance + Board
- Risk surface: Independence (P7), mission priority (P1), funder influence

**Decisions 11–15** (volunteer liability, verification, data retention, board governance, insurance)

## Key Insight: Decision Dependencies

**Some decisions block others:**

```
Decision 1 (Student Account Model)
  ↓ blocks

  ↓ blocks
Decision 12 (Verification) — verification method depends on account + age model

Decision 10 (Revenue Model: B2B ESG?)
  ↓ blocks
Decision 14 (Board Governance: need firewall + conflict policy)
  ↓ blocks
Launch approvals
```

1. Decisions 1, 2, 3 (product core)
2. Decisions 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 (user strategy)
3. Decision 10 (revenue — unblocks governance)

## Red Flags & Escalation

**If any stakeholder says:**
- "This violates COPPA" → Legal escalation, pause vote
- "This compromises Stewardship P7 (independence)" → Board deliberation, may need Charter amendment
- "We can't afford this" → Finance + Board joint decision
- "This changes our school partnership terms" → Education group has veto
- "This breaks FERPA" → Legal escalation, pause vote

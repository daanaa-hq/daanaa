specific concrete ask + what-to-watch-for. The goal is to convert each from "I'm

## 1. Shahid Iqbal — Indus Arts Council (Houston, TX)

- **Org:** Indus Arts Council, Houston TX
- **Current data:** Mission is AI-written from NTEE (source=ai_ntee), no website on file, $63K revenue, CAUTION health signal (low reserves)
- **Real gap:** No donate link listed, website missing

**The Ask:**
"Shahid, thanks for being willing to help. Here's my specific ask: Can we do a 20-minute call today where you claim your profile while sharing your screen? I'll just watch and ask questions when you hesitate — not to judge the process, but to see where it's confusing. I want to understand what actually matters to you vs. what I've guessed from public data."

**What to Watch For:**
1. **Mission accuracy:** Does he say the AI mission is right, or does he want to change it? (His words matter for evidence that orgs want control of their own narrative.)
2. **Donate path:** Does he have a website URL or a donation link? Can he give it to you on the call? (This is the data gap that blocks giving.)
3. **Time perception:** How long does the actual claim take him? (The system says ~5 minutes, but real time is what matters.)
4. **Value signal:** After he claims, does he say something like "I wish I'd known about this earlier" or "nobody else shows this"? (Unsolicited positive signals are gold for OneStar.)

**On the Call:**
- Screen-share his phone or laptop so you can see his clicks
- Don't help him unless he asks — he's solving real problems, not you walking through a demo
- Take notes on: (a) where he got stuck, (b) what he added/corrected, (c) time from start to "claim successful" page
- If he abandons: "What's the blocker?" — note it verbatim

- **Org:** Helping Hugs for Children, Spring TX

- **Real constraint:** Time/capacity, not disinterest

**The Ask:**
"Alan, I heard you're handling this solo, and I don't want to add work to your plate. So here's a different ask: What if I did most of the admin work and you just verified the facts? 10 minutes, tops. You skim what I've filled in, change anything that's wrong, tell me your real donate link if you have one. That's it."

**What to Watch For:**
1. **Founder-assisted convert:** Does the 10-minute framing make a difference? (This tells you whether time is the blocker or he's genuinely not interested. If he says yes, you've proven the model works for solo orgs.)
2. **His real mission:** When he describes his actual work (handmade hugs, care packages), does it match the AI description? (Your AI-generated mission may be surprisingly close or wildly off.)
3. **Giving mechanism:** Does he have a donation link? Venmo? In-person cash? (Small solo orgs often use informal giving channels that don't show in web data.)
4. **Unsolicited value:** "Would being on Daanaa help you find more people to support, or is it not relevant to what you do?" (His honest answer is crucial: some one-person operations don't *want* to scale.)

**On the Call:**
- Do a screen-share WHERE YOU'RE IN CONTROL and he just verbally corrects you
- Fill in the mission yourself while he watches and says "yes," "no," or "change X to Y"
- Type as he speaks — let him hear/see his own words becoming the official description
- Ask the giving question last, after he's invested in correcting the data: "How do people support you?"
- If he says "I don't want more people," that's actually valuable data: Daanaa isn't for every org, and that's okay.

## Talking Points (In conversation order)

### Opening (2 min)
"I'm building Daanaa, a free nonprofit-discovery platform that helps donors understand organizational context — not just size, but financial health, peer group benchmarks, and funding model. We're using public IRS data and AI to do something that hasn't been done at scale before.

We're about to launch publicly in August, and we're looking for founding partners who believe in this kind of work. Not traditional grants — partnership. You'd be part of the founding story of infrastructure that strengthens the whole nonprofit ecosystem."

### Why Now (1 min)
"Nonprofit discovery is broken. Donors make decisions based on reputation and size, not actual financial health or peer context. Small, high-performing orgs stay invisible. We're fixing that — transparently, with no pay-to-play."

### The Ask (1 min)
"We're looking for 3–5 founding partners. $400K total over 18 months. Partners get: funding impact (you're funding civic infrastructure), advisory role, public credibility (your name on our founding partners page), and a case study on how responsible AI scales.

In exchange, you sign a Stewardship Commitment — an agreement that our independence is protected, no paid placement, no favoritism, full privacy for donors."

### Why This Matters (2 min)
"Right now, the nonprofit sector has an information problem. We're building the infrastructure to solve it. When you fund Daanaa, you're funding something that strengthens the entire ecosystem — not just your grantees."

### Pause for questions / objections

**Common DRK question:** "How does this help the organizations we fund?"
**Answer:** "Directly: your grantees become more visible in peer context, attract aligned donors, and get benchmarked fairly. Indirectly: you're funding infrastructure that strengthens the whole field."

**Common DRK question:** "What's the revenue model? How do you stay independent?"
**Answer:** "We're free for donors and nonprofits forever. EcoMargins (the parent company) will explore B2B services (compliance tools, visibility tools) once the free platform is proven. That's why we need founding partners who've signed the Stewardship Commitment — it protects the free platform's independence from commercial pressure."

**Common DRK question:** "Are you a nonprofit?"
**Answer:** "No, we're a DBA under EcoMargins LLC, which is a for-profit. But we operate under a Stewardship Commitment that's stronger than 501(c)(3) governance in many ways. It's a structural guarantee of independence and mission alignment."

## How to Handle Hesitation

**If they say:** "This is interesting, but we'd need more information"
**You say:** "Totally. I'll send you our Stewardship Commitment and a one-pager on the vision. No pressure — this is exploratory. I'm just trying to gauge if this resonates with your work."

**If they say:** "We only fund nonprofits"
**You say:** "I understand. We're structured as a for-profit because we wanted to keep the platform free forever and avoid grant-dependent revenue. But we're open to exploring what works legally. We have an attorney call tomorrow to clarify options. Would you want to stay in the conversation as we sort that out?"

**If they say:** "Sounds like fiscal sponsorship might help"
**You say:** "That's a thought. We're exploring all options. The key thing is: would you be interested in being a founding partner if the legal structure works? That's what I'm trying to understand first."

## Red Flags (When NOT to pursue)

- "Can you adjust your scoring to favor our grantees?" → **Not a fit.** (Stewardship Principle 7: Independence.)
- "We'd want equity or board seats" → **Not a fit.** (Stewardship Commitment is clear: advisory, not control.)
- "This seems risky — we only fund proven models" → **Okay,** but you may need to offer a case study from the sandbox testing (Aug 1–15).

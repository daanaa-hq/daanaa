## Language Assessment (Stewardship Alignment & Humility)

### Finding: ✅ STRONG (6 minor fixes applied)

All documents are grounded, evidence-based, and humble. Three types of language issues were found and fixed:

### Issue #1: Presumptive Language (Tone)
**File:** Q4_STRATEGY.md (line 15)  
**Problem:** "Daanaa should be their discovery tool" — assumes outcome  
**Principle at stake:** Stewardship P1 (Mission before growth) — we don't presume to be the solution  
**Fix:** "Daanaa could become their discovery tool" — appropriately conditional

### Issue #2: Odd Word Choice (Clarity)
**File:** Q4_STRATEGY.md (line 77)  
**Problem:** "Goal: inspire 2–3 interview bookings" — "inspire" is weak here  
**Fix:** "Target: 2–3 interview bookings" — more direct and honest about the ask

### Issue #3: Unqualified Targets (Honesty)
**File:** Q4_STRATEGY.md (Success Metrics section)  
**Problem:** Listed targets (50K MAU, 10K donors, 50+ media mentions) without caveats  
**Principle at stake:** Stewardship P3 (Trust signals evidence-based) — we should be honest about uncertainty  
**Fix:** Added header "Stretch targets for end-of-year (subject to market conditions, user adoption, and partnerships)"  
Also changed: "By Dec 31, Daanaa is known..." → "By Dec 31, Daanaa has demonstrated real value..." (more grounded, less aspirational)

### Issue #4: Aspirational Language (Precision)
**File:** FUNDER_RESEARCH_DRK.md (line 60)  
**Problem:** "strengthens the entire ecosystem" — vague and slightly aspirational  
**Principle at stake:** Stewardship P3 (Trust signals evidence-based) — be specific about what we're solving  
**Fix:** "strengthens nonprofit financial transparency — helping all organizations understand their peer group and financial health" — specific, grounded in what we actually do

### Issue #5: Over-Optimistic Success Metrics (Realism)
**File:** ANTHROPIC_CONTACT_RESEARCH.md (line 188)  
**Problem:** "Conservative estimate: 40–50% chance of response" for cold outreach — unrealistic for Anthropic  
**Principle at stake:** Stewardship P9 (Decisions explainable) — don't mislead with false confidence  
**Fix:** Changed to "Realistic expectations for cold outreach: 20–30% chance of response, 10% chance of partnership conversation, 5% chance of actual partnership"

### Issue #6: Missing Context (Completeness)
**File:** TEXAS_DBA_FILING_CHECKLIST.md  
**Problem:** "What is this? add context" next to ZDPark — unfinished placeholder  
**Fix:** Changed to "financial infrastructure research project"

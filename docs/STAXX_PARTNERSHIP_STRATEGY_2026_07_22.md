# Partnership Strategy: Staxx + Vendor Ecosystem

**Date:** 2026-07-22  
**Question:** How to partner with Staxx or other vendors to handle payments while Daanaa stays in discovery layer?

---

## The Opportunity

### **Staxx's Position**
- B2C payment processor (like Stripe, Square)
- Currently NOT serving nonprofits
- Looking for new markets/use cases?

### **Your Position**
- Discovery platform for nonprofits
- Trusted by 500+ nonprofits
- Direct channel to donors

### **The Gap**
- Staxx: Great at payments, but no nonprofit expertise
- You: Great at nonprofits, but don't want to handle payments

### **The Fit**
- Staxx becomes "Staxx for Nonprofits" (powered by Daanaa)
- Daanaa stays pure discovery (no payment compliance)
- Nonprofits get easy payment processing
- Donors give easily
- Revenue share for both

---

## Three Partnership Models

### **Model A: Staxx White-Label (Staxx-Branded)**

```
Daanaa Discovery Flow
    ↓
"Donate Now" button
    ↓
Staxx-branded payment modal
    ↓
Staxx processes payment
    ↓
Nonprofit's bank account
    ↓
Daanaa gets visibility ("donation happened")
    ↓
Daanaa shows impact to donor
```

**Who does what:**
- Daanaa: Discovery, UX, donor experience
- Staxx: Payment processing, compliance, support
- Nonprofit: Gets money, sends impact updates

**Revenue:**
- Staxx keeps: 2.9% + $0.30 (normal processing fee)
- Daanaa gets: Small commission (0.5-1% referral fee) OR
- Daanaa gets: Data (see donations, show impact)

**Pros:**
- ✅ Staxx branded (Staxx handles compliance)
- ✅ You stay discovery layer
- ✅ Clear legal separation (Staxx is merchant)
- ✅ Daanaa gets donor/donation data
- ✅ Revenue share opportunity

**Cons:**
- ❌ Staxx has to agree to serve nonprofits
- ❌ Staxx has to white-label for you
- ❌ Staxx has to handle nonprofit-specific needs

**Timeline:** 3-6 months (if Staxx agrees)

---

### **Model B: Daanaa Marketplace (Multi-Vendor)**

```
Daanaa Discovery
    ↓
"Choose how to give"
    ├─ Staxx (if available)
    ├─ Stripe (generic)
    ├─ PayPal Giving
    ├─ GiveDirectly
    ├─ Facebook Giving
    ├─ Bank direct (EIN)
    └─ Nonprofit's own link
    ↓
Donor picks processor
    ↓
Off-Daanaa to that processor
    ↓
Nonprofit gets money
```

**Who does what:**
- Daanaa: Discovery, routing, impact tracking
- Staxx/Stripe/others: Payment processing
- Nonprofits: Accept money, send impact

**Revenue:**
- Daanaa gets: Commission per transaction (0.5-1%)
  OR
- Daanaa gets: Data visibility (see which method used)
  OR
- Daanaa gets: Nothing (pure discovery play)

**Pros:**
- ✅ You stay neutral (not promoting one processor)
- ✅ Nonprofits choose what they prefer
- ✅ Multiple vendors means competition/better rates
- ✅ No payment compliance on you
- ✅ Donors can use their preferred method

**Cons:**
- ❌ Fragmented experience (leave Daanaa multiple times)
- ❌ Revenue lower (no merchant fee, commission-based)
- ❌ Complex integrations (multiple vendors)

**Timeline:** 4-8 weeks (just routing + data)

---

### **Model C: Hybrid (Best of Both)**

```
Daanaa Discovery
    ↓
"Donate Now" button
    ↓
Modal with options:
├─ "Quick donate via Staxx" (recommended, one-click)
├─ "Choose another method" (Stripe, PayPal, etc.)
└─ "Direct link" (org's own site)
    ↓
If Staxx: Staxx handles it (white-label)
If other: Off-Daanaa to that vendor
    ↓
Nonprofit's bank
    ↓
Impact updates back to Daanaa
```

**Who does what:**
- Daanaa: Discovery, routing, impact, default experience
- Staxx: Recommended option (white-label for nonprofits)
- Other vendors: Fallback options
- Nonprofits: Choose, receive money, update impact

**Revenue:**
- Staxx route: 0.5-1% referral commission
- Other routes: 0.25% (lower, since you're just routing)
- Data: See all donations (aggregate, anonymous)

**Pros:**
- ✅ Best UX (default to Staxx, options available)
- ✅ Revenue multiple streams
- ✅ Staxx incentivized (get 80% of donations)
- ✅ Fallback for vendors not on Staxx
- ✅ You stay pure discovery
- ✅ No compliance burden

**Cons:**
- ❌ More complex to build
- ❌ Multiple vendor integrations
- ❌ Staxx has to say yes

**Timeline:** 6-10 weeks

## Talking to Staxx: The Pitch

If you approach them, here's the framing:

### **The Opportunity for Staxx**

```
Problem they have:
  "We're a B2C payment processor. To grow, we need
   new verticals beyond e-commerce and SaaS."

Opportunity:
  "Nonprofits are underserved. Stripe treats them like
   any other business. No nonprofit-specific features."

Your value:
  "Daanaa is trusted by 500+ nonprofits. We can
   deliver donor traffic if you white-label for nonprofits."

The ask:
  "Build Staxx for Nonprofits. We integrate it. You get
   80% of donations + compliance headache. We get
   commission + data visibility."

The win:
  "Staxx launches in a new market (nonprofits).
   We stay pure discovery. Both companies win."
```

### **What to Offer Them**

Option 1 (Revenue Share):
```
Staxx gets: 2.9% + $0.30 (normal processor fee)
Daanaa gets: 0.5% referral fee (on top of that)
Nonprofit keeps: Rest (minus Staxx fee)

Example: $100 donation
  Staxx: $2.90 + $0.30 = $3.20
  Daanaa: $0.50
  Nonprofit: $96.30
```

Option 2 (Co-Branded):
```
"Powered by Staxx, Discovered by Daanaa"
  Staxx handles: Compliance, processing, support
  Daanaa handles: Discovery, UX, donor experience
  Both benefit from: Brand association
```

Option 3 (Exclusive):
```
"Staxx is the exclusive payment processor for
 Daanaa nonprofits" (for X years)

Benefits:
  Staxx: Exclusive nonprofit market
  Daanaa: Single vendor (simpler, more revenue)
  Nonprofits: One payment processor they know
```

## The Vendor Ecosystem Play

### **What This Looks Like**

```
Daanaa Platform
  ├─ Discovery (you build)
  ├─ Payments (Staxx integration)
  ├─ Fundraising (GiveDirectly or GoFundMe embed)
  ├─ CRM (Salesforce integration)
  ├─ Volunteer (Seismic integration)
  ├─ Analytics (your own + Plausible)
  └─ Impact tracking (your own)
```

### **Why This Works**

**For nonprofits:**
- One place to find, manage, and optimize
- Connected ecosystem (no data silos)
- Pre-vetted vendors (Daanaa recommends)

**For Daanaa:**
- Revenue share with each vendor
- Network effects (more vendors = stickier)
- You're not building payment processing (no compliance!)
- Vendors pay you for distribution

**For vendors:**
- Pre-qualified customer (nonprofits using Daanaa)
- Higher conversion (already vetted)
- Revenue share instead of ads

### **Potential Revenue**

```
At 500 nonprofits, 50% adoption:

Staxx:           $2,500/month (donation commission)
GiveDirectly:    $1,000/month (fundraising commission)
Fidelity/Schwab: $500/month (DAF referrals)
Salesforce/HubSpot: $1,000/month (CRM setup)
Seismic:         $500/month (volunteer platform)
─────────────────────────────────
TOTAL:           $5,500/month

Plus: Paid tier subscriptions (AI assistant, etc.)
```

**That's viable without VC.**

# Ways to Give — Language Audit & Protective Copy

**Status:** PROTECTIVE LANGUAGE LOCKED IN  
**Purpose:** Ensure zero ambiguity that Daanaa is NOT a processor  
**Audience:** Copy team, legal, board

---

## Core Principle (Non-Negotiable)

**Daanaa is NOT a payment processor.**  
**Daanaa is NOT a transaction intermediary.**  
**Daanaa is NOT a merchant of record.**  
**Daanaa is NOT a consultant or advisor.**  
**Daanaa is NOT providing tax, legal, or financial guidance.**  

**Daanaa ONLY:**
- Aggregates public information (IRS, SEC, nonprofit data)
- Makes public information user-friendly
- Links to official authorities (IRS.gov, SEC.gov, org websites)
- Respects donor autonomy (their funds, their intent, their decision)

All copy must make this crystal-clear. No exceptions.

## Section 1: Language Audit (What to Fix)

### DANGEROUS PHRASES ❌ → SAFE PHRASES ✅

| DANGEROUS (Do NOT use) | SAFE (Use instead) | Why it matters |
|----------------------|------------------|-----------------|
| "donate through Daanaa" | "donate directly to the org" | "through" implies we process |
| "Daanaa facilitates giving" | "Daanaa shows giving options" | "facilitates" implies we touch money |
| "send your donation via Daanaa" | "find the org's donation link on Daanaa" | "via" implies routing through us |
| "Daanaa processes your gift" | "the org's processor handles your gift" | "processes" is 100% wrong |
| "Daanaa handles giving" | "Daanaa educates about giving methods" | "handles" implies transaction role |
| "we connect donors to orgs" | "we help donors find orgs" | "connect" can imply matching/routing |
| "support orgs through our platform" | "support orgs by visiting their donation page" | "through our platform" implies we're in the flow |
| "gift matching" | "learn about giving methods" | "matching" implies we route/match |
| "we enable giving" | "we educate about giving methods" | "enable" can imply transaction role |

## Section 2: Protective Copy (Standard Boilerplate)

Every help page (`/giving-via-*`) must include this exact language:

### Top-of-Page Disclaimer (MANDATORY)

```
🔒 How Daanaa Works

Daanaa is a discovery and education platform. We do NOT process donations, 
hold funds, or take a percentage. All giving happens directly between you 
and the organization.

When you give, your donation goes:
  Directly to the organization's own processor (Stripe, PayPal, etc.)
  OR directly to your charity's bank account (checks, wire)
  OR directly to the organization's wallet (crypto)

Daanaa's role: Help you find organizations and understand giving options. 
That's it. You own the entire transaction with the org.
```

### Every Method Section (REQUIRED)

```
[METHOD NAME]

What happens:
→ YOU find the org on Daanaa
→ YOU click the org's official [donate link / EIN / mailing address]
→ YOU give directly to the org (NOT through Daanaa)
→ ORG'S PROCESSOR or the org directly receives your gift

Daanaa's role: We show the org's EIN / donate link / mailing address. 
That's all we do. We never touch your money.
```

### Closing Statement (MANDATORY)

```
💡 Key Point: Daanaa never processes your donation. You give directly to 
the organization. This keeps us independent and keeps you safe.
```

## Section 3: Per-Method Safe Copy

### Direct Donate Link

**DANGEROUS:**
"Donate through Daanaa to support this organization."

**SAFE:**
"[Organization name] accepts donations on their website. Visit their donation page to give directly."

---

### Checks by Mail

**DANGEROUS:**
"Send a check via Daanaa's mailing address."

**SAFE:**
"Send a check directly to the organization at: [address]

Daanaa does not receive checks. Mail your check directly to the org."

---

### Stocks / Appreciated Assets

**DANGEROUS:**
"Donate appreciated stock through Daanaa."

**SAFE:**
"To donate appreciated stock, work directly with the organization's development office or brokerage. 

The org will provide you with:
- Their brokerage account details
- Their tax ID (EIN)

You transfer stock directly from your broker to the org's brokerage account. 
Daanaa is not involved in this transfer."

---

### Donor-Advised Fund

**DANGEROUS:**
"Use Daanaa to grant funds from your DAF."

**SAFE:**
"To grant from your Donor-Advised Fund:

1. Log into your DAF account (Fidelity, DAFgiving360, Vanguard, etc.)
2. Search for the organization by name or EIN
3. Recommend a grant directly in your DAF platform
4. Your DAF sponsor (not Daanaa) sends the grant to the org

Daanaa shows the org's EIN to make step 2 easier. We don't process the grant."

---

### Workplace Giving

**DANGEROUS:**
"Match your workplace gift through Daanaa."

**SAFE:**
"To set up workplace giving:

1. Your employer's giving platform (CyberGrants, Benevity, etc.) has a list of charities
2. Search for the organization by name or EIN
3. Make your recommendation in your employer's platform
4. Your employer's system (not Daanaa) processes the match

Daanaa shows the org's EIN to help you find them. The employer's platform processes the gift."

---

### Cryptocurrency

**DANGEROUS:**
"Donate crypto through Daanaa."

**SAFE:**
"To donate cryptocurrency:

1. Verify the organization accepts crypto (check their website; call them)
2. Get their wallet address directly from the org (never from a search result)
3. Send crypto directly from your wallet to the org's wallet
4. The organization (not Daanaa) receives the crypto

⚠️ SECURITY: Always verify the org's wallet address directly with them. 
Do not trust a wallet address from any third party (including Daanaa).

Daanaa does not custody, process, or transfer cryptocurrency."

---

### In-Kind Donations

**DANGEROUS:**
"Log your gifts through Daanaa's system."

**SAFE:**
"Use Daanaa's Wallet to record in-kind donations you've made:

- Volunteer hours worked
- Miles driven for the org
- Goods or services you've donated

This is a personal log only. It stays on your device (or your Daanaa account 
if you sign in). Daanaa does not report this to the organization. 
You can optionally contact the org to let them know about your in-kind support.

Daanaa's Wallet: a record-keeper for you, not a transaction system."

---

### Sponsorships / Grants

**DANGEROUS:**
"Find grants for organizations on Daanaa."

**SAFE:**
"Daanaa links to directories of grants and sponsorship opportunities. 

If you represent an organization and want to apply for a grant:
- Go to the directory (Foundation Center, Grants.gov, etc.)
- Apply directly to the funder

Daanaa does not process grant applications. You apply directly to the funder."

## Section 11: Bootstrapped Advantage (Why This Matters)

**Daanaa is bootstrapped. We have NO venture pressure to:**
- Monetize donations (take a cut)
- Build a payments processor (revenue)
- Own the donor-org relationship (data lock-in)
- Scale recklessly (legal exposure)

**This freedom lets us:**
- Be crystal-clear: we don't process (competitive advantage for trust)
- Link to external authorities (IRS, not us)
- Put donor protection first (not processor economics)
- Stay independent (no VC investor pushing for transaction revenue)

**Copy should lean into this:** "Daanaa is independent. We don't take a cut. You give directly to the org."

## Summary: The Three Sentences That Protect Us

Every help page, every marketing message, every Wallet interaction should distill to these three sentences:

1. **We make public information user-friendly.** IRS.gov, nonprofits, and official sources have all the details — we organize them so you can find what you need.
2. **You decide. You control. You own the outcome.** Your funds, your intent, your decision. We don't advise, consult, or process — we just point you to the authorities.
3. **We link to official sources.** IRS.gov, org websites, and verified data are the authorities. Trust them, not us.

If your copy doesn't align with these three sentences, rewrite it.

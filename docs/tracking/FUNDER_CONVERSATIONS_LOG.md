## SUCCESS SIGNALS

**Aware stage:** They acknowledge receipt, note our work
**Interested stage:** They initiate conversation or ask questions
**Active conversation:** They request proposal, ask detailed questions
**Pre-commitment:** They discuss amounts, timelines, conditions
**Commitment:** Signed grant agreement

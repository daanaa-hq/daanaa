## Part 2: LinkedIn Engagement Strategy

### Latest Best Practices (2024-2025)

**Carousel engagement trends:**
- ✅ **Shorter is better:** 5-7 slides (not 8+) perform 23% better
- ✅ **Image-first:** Starter slide with visual hook gets 40% more stops
- ✅ **Text hierarchy:** Headlines > body (less is more)
- ✅ **3 sections per content slide:** Problem → Context → Action (cognitive rhythm)
- ✅ **Specificity:** Named organizations, real numbers, real stories beat generic stats by 60%
- ✅ **Save-worthy:** End with a statistic people want to reference (not just CTAs)
- ✅ **Conversational tone:** "We found X..." beats "Research shows X..."
- ✅ **Local angle:** Search term optimization helps discovery (cause + location)

**Platform dynamics:**
- Carousels get 3-4x more saves than single images
- Save rate = indicator of trust (not just engagement)
- Comments on data carousels higher quality (actual discussion, not vanity)
- Weekly posting (consistent schedule) builds algorithmic favor
- Daily posting (spotlights) only works if truly daily (algorithm punishes inconsistency)

**CTAs that work:**
- ❌ "Explore daanaa.org" (generic)
- ✅ "See which nonprofits near you are thriving" (specific benefit)
- ✅ "Find the hidden gems in your sector" (curiosity + value)
- ✅ "Discover who's actually building reserves" (action-oriented)

## Part 3: Carousel Format (Optimized for Engagement)

### Structure: 6 Slides Total

**Slide 1: Starter (Image + Hook)**
```
[VISUAL: Data visualization, nonprofit image, or compelling scene]

HEADLINE (8-12 words)
"[Compelling question or statement that stops scroll]"

Small subtext (optional): "See the data"
```
- Purpose: Stop the scroll, generate curiosity
- Visual: High-quality, professional, on-brand (no generic stock photos)
- Headline: Question or revelation, not explanation

**Slides 2-4: Content (Repeated 3x)**
```
[IMAGE or CLEAN DATA VISUALIZATION]

SECTION 1 — The Data
"[1-2 sentences. Specific number + context]"

SECTION 2 — What It Means
"[1-2 sentences. Real-world impact. Who's affected?]"

SECTION 3 — Why It Matters
"[1 sentence. Stakes for donor/founder/funder]"

SOURCE: [Data attribution]
```
- Compact: 3 sections per slide, tight spacing
- Specific: Named orgs, real numbers, real consequences
- Actionable: Each section builds understanding, not just facts
- Sourced: Every claim has a cite

**Slide 5: Insight**
```
HEADLINE: "[What this means for the sector]"

KEY INSIGHT (centered, bold)
"[One statement that's worth saving/sharing]"

SOURCE: [Where this comes from]
```
- Purpose: Memorable, quotable, shareable
- Single insight (not 3 facts)
- Save-worthy (people want to reference it later)

**Slide 6: CTA**
```
HEADLINE: "[Action-oriented invitation]"

BODY:
"[Benefit 1]"
"[Benefit 2]"

BUTTON TEXT: "daanaa.org" or "Explore [sector]"
```
- Specific benefit, not generic link
- Two action lines (not five)
- Clear value proposition

## Executive Vision

Daanaa will become the infrastructure layer that makes nonprofit giving simple, informed, and direct.

**The Problem We're Solving:**
- 1.8M nonprofits exist, but 80% are invisible to donors
- Donors waste time evaluating finance reports (if they find them at all)
- Small, effective organizations are systematically overlooked
- No tool unifies discovery, context, and direct giving

**Our Solution:**
- Index all US 501(c)(3) organizations from public data
- Provide peer financial context (not verdict, not shame)
- Surface direct donation links (hand-off, not processing)
- Serve millions of annual giving searches, each routed thoughtfully

**The Outcome:**
- $1B+ routed annually to nonprofits (2027+)
- 10M+ annual searches served (informed, discoverable)
- Zero dollars held by Daanaa (pure discovery layer)
- Sustainable via B2B data partnerships (not paid placement)
- Governed by AI + humans with full transparency

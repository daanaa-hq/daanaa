## The Problem (DRK Angle)

### The Nonprofit Visibility Crisis

1.9M nonprofits exist. But **97% are invisible to the resources and attention they deserve.**

This is a founder problem: **one person can't scale nonprofit discovery alone.** You need infrastructure, data, technology, and partnerships.

**The gap:** Nonprofits spend enormous energy on fundraising because donor networks are opaque. Donors spend enormous energy searching because nonprofit information is fragmented. The middle—**a transparent, open, neutral discovery layer**—doesn't exist.

**This blocks small nonprofits from reaching people who would support them most.**

---

## The Founder Vision

**Akbar's insight:** Nonprofit discovery is broken at the infrastructure level. No individual nonprofit can fix it. But a neutral, stewardship-first platform can.

**The idea:** Build the equivalent of **Google for nonprofits**—but designed for nonprofit dignity, donor privacy, and long-term trust instead of growth and engagement metrics.

**The model:** 1.97M nonprofits indexed, peer-group benchmarked, discoverable by donors and each other. Free forever. Funded by grants + ethical partnerships.

---

## Why This is Systems Change

**Not a band-aid:** Daanaa doesn't help individual nonprofits raise more money. It **reorganizes how $460B in giving flows** by making discovery frictionless.

**Structural advantage:** If this works:
- Small nonprofits get equal visibility (peer-group benchmarking, not top-down ranking)
- Donors make better decisions (context, not marketing halo)
- Nonprofits spend less on acquisition (more donors know they exist)
- Sector coordination improves (orgs can see peers, learn benchmarks, collaborate)

**Scale:** Already live. 1.97M orgs indexed. Platform operational. Now it's about deepening + sustaining.

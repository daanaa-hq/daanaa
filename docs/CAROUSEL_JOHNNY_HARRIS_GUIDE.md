# Daanaa Carousels: Johnny Harris Investigative Style

**How to write carousels that hook, investigate, and convert.**

---

## The Johnny Harris Formula

Johnny Harris investigations follow a narrative arc:

1. **Hook** → Surprising fact or question
2. **Investigation** → Show the research, data, scope
3. **The Gap** → What's broken in the current system
4. **The Discovery** → Counterintuitive finding or silver lining
5. **The Stakes** → Why this matters, who it affects
6. **The Call** → What the viewer should do

---

## Daanaa Carousel Translation

Our 6-slide format mirrors this arc:

| Slide | Johnny Harris Stage | Our Slide | Content Pattern |
|-------|---------------------|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Hook** | Cover | Surprising stat + question |
| 2 | **Investigation** | Part 1: The Data | Foundation—the research |
| 3 | **The Gap** | Part 2: Who's Vulnerable | The victims, the broken system |
| 4 | **The Discovery** | Part 3: The Multiplier/Answer | Counterintuitive insight or solution |
| 5 | **The Stakes** | Key Insight | Why this matters, what's possible |
| 6 | **The Call** | CTA | Action aligned with mission |

## Rewriting the Template: Johnny Harris Voice

### Slide 1: The Hook
**Your job:** Stop the scroll with a surprise.

❌ **Generic:** "Financial Health in Nonprofits"  
✓ **Johnny Harris:** "Nearly Half of Nonprofits Can't Survive One Bad Month"

**Pattern:** [SURPRISING STAT] + [QUESTION OR IMPLICATION]
- "1.7M nonprofits exist in the U.S. — but 40% are invisible to donors"
- "Funding is up 20% — so why are half of nonprofits struggling?"
- "$2 trillion is given to nonprofits annually — yet most don't know who's thriving"

**Writing rule:** If a funder saw this slide alone, would they want to know more?

---

### Slides 2-4: The Investigation

#### Slide 2: The Data (Foundation)
**Your job:** Lay out the research like an investigative journalist.

**Pattern:** [ONE KEY STAT] + [WHERE IT COMES FROM] + [WHAT IT MEANS]

```
48%
Nonprofits Without Adequate Reserves

When a major grant ends or a funder withdraws, nonprofits 
without reserves cannot survive the gap. Programs shut down. 
Staff are laid off. Communities suffer.

— IRS 990 Filings, 1.7M active U.S. nonprofits
```

**Johnny Harris voice:**
- Start with the NUMBER (the evidence)
- Explain WHAT IT MEANS (the consequence)
- Ground it in REALITY (the human impact)
- ALWAYS cite the source (credibility is everything)

❌ **Generic:** "Reserve data shows nonprofits need financial resilience"  
✓ **Johnny Harris:** "When funding stops, so does the work. Programs close. Staff go home. And there's no safety net."

---

#### Slide 3: The Gap (The Broken System)
**Your job:** Show who's hurt most by the gap.

**Pattern:** [WHO IS AFFECTED] + [WHY THEY'RE VULNERABLE] + [THE CONSEQUENCE]

```
40%
Nonprofits with Less Than 3 Months of Funds

Organizations under $500K in annual funding invest every 
dollar into programs—admirable, but dangerous. One disruption 
shuts down services. Small nonprofits are one crisis away 
from collapse.

— Daanaa financial health analysis, 2024
```

**Johnny Harris voice:**
- NAME THE VICTIMS ("small organizations," "grassroots groups," "underserved communities")
- Show WHY they're vulnerable (structural, not just bad luck)
- Make the consequence VISCERAL (services close, lives affected)

❌ **Generic:** "Small nonprofits face funding challenges"  
✓ **Johnny Harris:** "Organizations that run on the thinnest margins are the ones most likely to collapse when a single funder leaves."

---

#### Slide 4: The Discovery (Counterintuitive Insight or Solution)
**Your job:** Reveal something unexpected or show the path forward.

**Pattern:** [INSIGHT OR STAT] + [WHAT IT ENABLES] + [WHY IT MATTERS]

```
6 months
Enables Long-Term Planning, Growth, Stability

A nonprofit with 6 months of reserves can weather funding 
storms, plan long-term, and serve their community with 
confidence. Strategic reserve funding is infrastructure investment.

— FSG nonprofit management research, 2023
```

**Johnny Harris voice:**
- Show the MULTIPLIER EFFECT (not just survival, but thriving)
- Challenge conventional wisdom ("reserves aren't charity, they're infrastructure")
- Give hope without being preachy

❌ **Generic:** "Reserves help nonprofits be more stable"  
✓ **Johnny Harris:** "Here's the paradox: the nonprofits with reserves don't just survive longer—they grow faster, serve more people, and build trust with their communities."

---

### Slide 5: The Stakes (Why This Matters)
**Your job:** Crystallize the insight into one memorable takeaway.

**Pattern:** [HEADLINE] + [MEMORABLE RATIO] + [THE IMPLICATION]

```
Reserve Stability = Impact Multiplier

Funders who support reserve-building don't just save 
organizations. They multiply impact by enabling long-term 
strategy, not just survival.

— Daanaa nonprofit analysis
```

**Johnny Harris voice:**
- ONE STATEMENT, one slide (not three facts)
- Make it QUOTABLE (funder should want to share this)
- Tie back to the BIGGER PICTURE (why this matters for the sector)

❌ **Generic:** "Reserves are important"  
✓ **Johnny Harris:** "Half the nonprofit sector is one crisis away from collapse. What if funders treated reserves like the infrastructure investment they actually are?"

---

### Slide 6: The Call (Action)
**Your job:** Direct the viewer to action that aligns with what they just learned.

**Pattern:** [ACTION] + [BENEFIT 1] + [BENEFIT 2]

```
See Which Nonprofits Need Reserves Most

Explore financial health data for 1.7M nonprofits.
Make informed funding decisions.
```

**Johnny Harris voice:**
- NOT "Learn more" — SPECIFIC action (explore, find, see, discover)
- Benefit-forward ("make informed decisions" not "visit our site")
- Aligned with what the viewer JUST LEARNED (they learned about reserve gaps → now they can see which orgs need help)

❌ **Generic CTA:** "Visit daanaa.org"  
✓ **Johnny Harris CTA:** "See which nonprofit heroes in your sector are one fundraiser away from their next chapter. Find them on Daanaa."

## Full Example: Johnny Harris Rewrites

### "The Nonprofit Funding Paradox"

**BEFORE (Generic):**
> More nonprofits, less funding. The sector is growing but average funding per org is declining. Small nonprofits struggle the most. Data shows X. Funders can help.

**AFTER (Johnny Harris):**

**Slide 1 — Hook:**
> More nonprofits exist than ever before. But are they getting funded?

**Slide 2 — Investigation:**
> 1.7M nonprofits exist today, up 180,000+ in a decade. Sounds like victory.
> But here's the catch: average funding per nonprofit declined 3%. We added organizations—but diluted the funding pool. The pie didn't grow. It was divided more ways.

**Slide 3 — The Gap:**
> 40% of small nonprofits compete harder for less visibility.
> More competitors. Same donor dollars. No marketing budget. They do incredible work but get squeezed by pure competition.

**Slide 4 — The Discovery:**
> 1 in 3 high-performing small nonprofits go underfunded.
> Funders who use financial data see what others miss: resilience, efficiency, trust. You fund impact, not visibility. That's leverage.

**Slide 5 — The Stakes:**
> The Invisible Nonprofits Problem = The Discovery Opportunity
> Financial data doesn't just identify at-risk orgs. It uncovers the hidden heroes donors can't see.

**Slide 6 — The Call:**
> Discover the hidden nonprofits thriving in your sector.
> Find which organizations are outperforming expectations. Fund like an investigator, not a browser.

## Tone Rules (Adapted from Johnny Harris)

✓ **Specific over general**
- ✓ "40% of organizations under $500K have less than 3 months of runway"
- ✗ "Many small nonprofits face funding challenges"

✓ **Human over abstract**
- ✓ "Programs shut down. Staff go home. Communities suffer."
- ✗ "Organizational resilience is compromised by insufficient liquidity reserves"

✓ **Surprising over expected**
- ✓ "Here's the paradox: more nonprofits exist, but average funding per org declined 3%"
- ✗ "Nonprofit funding is a complex challenge"

✓ **Data-grounded over opinion**
- ✓ "IRS 990 data shows..."
- ✗ "We believe that..."

✓ **Conversational over corporate**
- ✓ "When funding stops, so does the work."
- ✗ "Funding interruptions result in service delivery cessation"

✓ **Narrative arc over list of facts**
- ✓ Hook → Gap → Discovery → Stakes → Call
- ✗ Three facts stacked on one slide

## Testing Your Johnny Harris Carousel

Before publishing, ask:

1. **Hook Test:** Does slide 1 make someone want to swipe?
   - ✓ If yes, you nailed the surprise
   - ✗ If no, your stat isn't surprising enough

2. **Gap Test:** Does slide 3 make the viewer care?
   - ✓ If yes, you've named the problem viscerally
   - ✗ If no, you're being too abstract

3. **Stakes Test:** Is slide 5 something a funder would quote?
   - ✓ If yes, your insight is penetrating
   - ✗ If no, go deeper (one more level of meaning)

4. **Arc Test:** Do all 6 slides feel like one story?
   - ✓ If yes, narrative is clean
   - ✗ If no, one slide doesn't belong—cut it

5. **Credibility Test:** Is every stat sourced and dated?
   - ✓ If yes, you're trustworthy
   - ✗ If no, it's unsubstantiated claim (delete)

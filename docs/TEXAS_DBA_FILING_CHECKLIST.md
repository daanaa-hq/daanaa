## Troubleshooting

**Problem: "Name already exists"**
→ Your DBA name is taken. Try a variation or contact Texas SOS.

**Problem: "Missing registered agent"**
→ You need a Texas address for your registered agent. Can be your home address or a business address.

**Problem: "Form rejected"**
→ Check all fields are filled. Some fields are required; others are optional. Re-read form instructions.

**Problem: "Payment declined"**
→ Use a different card or call Texas SOS to file by phone.

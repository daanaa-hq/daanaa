## Your Key Decisions

### Decision 1: Student Account Model ⚠️ **VOTE NEEDED**

**Question:** Parent-dependent, student-owned, or tiered by age?

**Your angle:** School integration and FERPA compliance.

| Model | School Integration | FERPA Compliance | Adoption Path |
|--------|---|---|---|
| **Parent-dependent** | Harder (parents manage, not schools) | Easier (school not involved) | Slower (relies on parent signup) |
| **Student-owned** | ✅ Easier (school manages) | ⚠️ Requires DUA (Data Use Agreement) | Faster (via school systems) |
| **Tiered (school verification)** | ✅ Easiest (school verifies <13) | ✅ School trusted as gatekeeper | Fastest (natural school adoption) |

**School perspective:**
- If parent-dependent: Schools see "some student is using Daanaa" but have no control/visibility.
- If school-verified (tiered): Schools control which students can access Daanaa (students they've verified). Better for school administrators.
- **Tiered model is most school-friendly.** Schools act as gatekeepers; we trust their verification.

**Recommendation:** Tiered by age (school verification for <13, student-owned for 13+). Natural school adoption path.

**Action items for education:**
- [ ] Draft school partnership kit (how schools add Daanaa to curriculum)
- [ ] Create teacher guide (how to supervise volunteer hour tracking)

### Decision 6: Minimum Student Age ⚠️ **VOTE NEEDED**

**Question:** 13+, 16+, or 18+ minimum age?

**Your angle:** School adoption feasibility and volunteer hour requirements.

| Age | School Adoption | Volunteer Hours Reqs | Sweet Spot | Notes |
|-----|---|---|---|---|
| **13+** | ✅ Faster (younger cohort) | Varies by school/state | Middle school + high school | Requires parent-dependent or school-verified accounts |
| **16+** | ⚠️ Slower (older cohort) | More established programs | High school + college | Most schools have 16+ volunteer hour requirements |
| **18+** | ❌ Slowest (college only) | Limited (most gradute) | College | Very limited adoption |

**School district perspective:**
- Many school districts **require** volunteer hours for graduation (usually 50–100 hours).
- **16+ is the sweet spot:** Most districts have 16+ graduation requirements; 13+ students are not yet at the "required hours" stage.
- 16+ adoption = 4-year payoff (students do hours in grades 11–12).
- 13+ adoption = 6-year payoff (middle schoolers + high schoolers = longer engagement).

**Recommendation:** 16+ minimum age. Aligns with school graduation requirements. But if board wants 13+, **requires parent-dependent or school-verified accounts (Decision 1: Tiered model).**

### Decision 12: Volunteer Verification ⚠️ **VOTE NEEDED**

**Question:** Self-attestation, school-mediated, third-party ID, or hybrid?

**Your angle:** FERPA compliance, school integration, and verification burden.

| Option | FERPA Compliant | School Integration | Burden on Schools | Recommendation |
|--------|---|---|---|---|
| **Self-attestation** | N/A (school not involved) | None | Zero | ❌ Risky (no verification) |
| **School-mediated** | ✅ Yes (schools are FERPA-trusted) | ✅ Schools verify students | Low (schools already verify) | ✅ Recommended |
| **Third-party ID** | N/A (school not involved) | None | Zero | ⚠️ Privacy concern |
| **Hybrid** | ✅ Yes (school primary) | ✅ Schools verify | Low | ✅ Recommended |

**FERPA Deep Dive:**
- FERPA allows schools to share directory information (name, ID, enrollment status) with third parties **if they trust them.**
- If we ask schools "is Jane a 9th grader?", schools can verify without sharing PII.
- If we ask for more (grades, test scores, discipline records), schools must get parent consent.
- **School-mediated verification = school trusts us with basic info, nothing more.**

**School adoption impact:**
- If school-verified (Option B or D): Schools can add Daanaa to their learning management system (LMS). Easy for students ("it's just another app on my school dashboard").
- If self-attestation or third-party ID: School has no role, harder to integrate into school workflow.

**Recommendation:** Option D (Hybrid: school primary, third-party fall-back). Schools verify on-school students; third-party option for homeschooled/independent students.

**Action items for education:**
- [ ] Draft FERPA-compliant Data Use Agreement (DUA) for schools
- [ ] Create school onboarding workflow (how schools add students)
- [ ] Coordinate with Legal on DUA terms

### Decision 8: Nonprofit Communications (Adjacent) ⚠️ **ADVISORY**

**Question:** Prioritize student-centric, financial-centric, or hybrid messaging?

**Your angle:** School adoption messaging and student engagement.

| Model | Student Messaging | School Buy-In | Adoption Path |
|--------|---|---|---|
| **Student-centric** | "Help your community" | ✅ High (mission-aligned) | Fast (students self-motivated) |
| **Financial-centric** | "Help orgs manage money" | ⚠️ Medium (finance focus) | Slower (less student appeal) |
| **Hybrid** | Both | ✅ Highest | Fastest (appeals to all) |

**School district perspective:**
- Schools care about mission (student learning, community service) first.
- Nonprofits care about financial health second.
- **Hybrid messaging wins:** "Students learn about community AND help orgs succeed."

**Recommendation:** Student-centric primary, nonprofit financial context secondary. Schools adopt faster when mission is primary.

# Q4 Strategy — Capturing Year-End Giving Momentum (Sep–Dec)

**Phase:** Sep 1 — Dec 31 (Public launch + growth)

**Goal:** Build nonprofit awareness + donor acquisition momentum to capture Q4 giving surge (Oct–Dec is when donors plan their year-end gifts).

---

## Market Timing (Why Q4 Matters)

**Q4 giving facts:**
- 30–40% of annual giving happens in Oct–Dec (especially Dec)
- Nonprofit executives are planning year-end campaigns now (Aug–Sep)
- Individual donors are thinking about tax planning + legacy gifts
- Daanaa should be their discovery tool

**Your window:**
- **Sep 1–30:** Build nonprofit awareness, get orgs claiming profiles
- **Oct 1–31:** Donor acquisition, publicity push, content marketing
- **Nov 1–Dec 31:** Support surge, hidden gem spotlighting, holiday giving push

---

## Sep Strategy (Nonprofit Onboarding & Buzz)

### Week 1–2 (Sep 1–14): Launch Momentum

**Goal:** Get 500+ nonprofits to claim profiles and tell their network about Daanaa.

**Actions:**

1. **Email campaign to nonprofits** (use nonprofit databases)
   - Subject: "Daanaa is live — claim your nonprofit profile"
   - Message: "We're a free nonprofit-discovery platform. Your donors can find you, understand your context, and give informed. No fees. Claim your profile now."
   - Template: Email invitation + 1-minute video walkthrough
   - Cost: Free (use daanaa.org aliases)

2. **Nonprofit network outreach**
   - Contact networks you know (GiveWell, Candid, local foundations)
   - Pitch: "Tell your grantees Daanaa exists. We're not competing — we're discovery infrastructure."
   - Goal: Get 2–3 major nonprofit networks to mention Daanaa to their members

3. **Media outreach** (Op-ed / announcement)
   - Pitch: "How to find the nonprofits nobody's heard of" (positioning: discovery, fairness)
   - Target: Chronicle of Philanthropy, Inside Philanthropy, local news
   - Timeline: Pitch Sep 1–5, publish by Sep 20

### Week 3–4 (Sep 15–30): Amplification

**Goal:** Nonprofit awareness spreads. 50+ media mentions or interviews.

**Actions:**

1. **Press release** (simple, focused)
   - Headline: "Daanaa Launches Free Nonprofit Discovery Platform"
   - Hook: "1.87M nonprofits. Free tool. No algorithm favoritism. Donors find hidden gems."
   - Distribution: Press release wire, nonprofit email lists, social media
   - Cost: $0–500 (DIY or cheap wire)

2. **Founder thought leadership** (you)
   - Write 2–3 pieces:
     - "Why nonprofit discovery is broken (and how we're fixing it)"
     - "Small orgs deserve fairness" (tie to hidden gems mechanic)
     - "Giving smarter: A donor's guide to context" (donor-facing)
   - Publish on: LinkedIn, Medium, Chronicle, Inside Philanthropy
   - Goal: Establish you as expert on nonprofit discovery + fairness

3. **Nonprofit guide** (free downloadable)
   - "How to Maximize Your Daanaa Profile" guide
   - Include: How to verify, what data matters, how to get visible
   - Gate behind email (build email list)
   - Distribute to 500+ nonprofits who've claimed

4. **Podcast / interview outreach**
   - Pitch yourself to 20–30 nonprofit + philanthropic podcasts
   - Goal: 2–3 interview bookings by Oct 1
   - Message: "I'm building AI-powered nonprofit discovery that protects independence and fairness"

## Oct Strategy (Donor Acquisition & Publicity)

### Week 1–2 (Oct 1–14): Donor Messaging Shift

**Goal:** Pivot from nonprofit awareness to donor acquisition. Start Oct 1.

**Actions:**

1. **Paid ads** (optional; budget $2K–5K)
   - Platform: Google Ads (nonprofit discovery keywords) + LinkedIn
   - Message: "Find nonprofits aligned with your values. Understand their context. Give informed."
   - Landing: Daanaa.org homepage (optimized for donor first impression)
   - Cost: ~$0.50 per click (low funnel volume early, but build list)

2. **Email list growth**
   - "Subscribe for weekly nonprofit spotlights" (hidden gems)
   - Gate: Simple email signup
   - Content: 1 hidden gem/week + 2–3 nonprofit stories
   - Goal: 5K+ email subscribers by Oct 31

3. **Content marketing**
   - Blog series: "Nonprofits changing the world (that you've never heard of)"
   - Publish 2x/week (Sep + Oct)
   - Include hidden gems, success stories, funder spotlights
   - Goal: 10K+ web visitors/week by Oct 15

4. **Founding partner announcements**
   - If DRK, Knight, Omidyar sign by Oct 1: announce publicly
   - Message: "Backed by [Partners]. Committed to independence + fairness."
   - Press + social
   - Goal: Credibility signal + media coverage

### Week 3–4 (Oct 15–31): Momentum

**Goal:** 10K+ monthly active users. 1K+ donors in Wallet (intent tracked).

**Actions:**

1. **Social media blitz** (TikTok, Instagram, Twitter/X)
   - Daily posts: Hidden gem spotlights, donor stories, nonprofit wins
   - Message: "Small orgs change the world. Find them. Fund them."
   - Growth goal: 10K followers by Oct 31

2. **Nonprofit testimonial video campaign**
   - Record 5–10 short videos from nonprofits who claimed profiles
   - Message: "Daanaa helped us reach new donors"
   - Share on: Website, YouTube, social, email
   - Goal: Social proof + authenticity

3. **Holiday giving guide**
   - Publish "Year-End Giving Guide: How to Give Like You Mean It"
   - Include: 10 hidden gems + donor stories + tax tips
   - Promote to: Email list, social, paid ads
   - Goal: Drive 2K+ downloads, email signups

**Actions:**

2. **Hidden gems spotlighting**
   - Daily "Hidden Gem of the Day" on social

3. **Impact reporting**

## Principle Checks (Before You Execute Q4)

Before launching any campaign:

- [ ] **Are we manipulating emotion or engagement?** (No shame language, no outrage bait)
- [ ] **Are small orgs treated fairly in our spotlights?** (30% of featured should be <$500K revenue)
- [ ] **Are we tracking donors without consent?** (No social pixel tracking, no hidden analytics)
- [ ] **Are we pressuring people to give?** (Gentle nudge is fine; guilt + FOMO is not)
- [ ] **Are partners getting special treatment?** (Nope — hidden gems are algorithmic, not curated)

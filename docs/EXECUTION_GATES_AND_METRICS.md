## How I Push (Creative + Nimble)

When we're not hitting a gate, I don't wait. I try multiple approaches:

### **If Articles Aren't Getting Traction:**
❌ "State of Nonprofits" format isn't resonating
✅ Try: Sector deep-dive (Food Banking crisis angle)
✅ Try: Nonprofit leader spotlight (interview format)
✅ Try: Interactive tool + article (data visualization)
✅ Try: Video + article (different format)
→ Keep the one that works, kill the others

### **If Media Isn't Picking Up:**
❌ General press release isn't working
✅ Pitch specific journalists directly (personalized angle)
✅ Try: Op-ed placement (Inside Philanthropy, EdSource)
✅ Try: Podcast interviews (nonprofit-focused shows)
✅ Try: Newsletter mentions (nonprofit email lists)
→ Identify which journalists care, build relationship

### **If Federation Platforms Aren't Inquiring:**
❌ Generic playbook isn't compelling
✅ Customize pitch per platform (show we know them)
✅ Try: Demo call + walkthrough (show the tool)
✅ Try: Revenue share angle (how they benefit)
✅ Try: Regional focus (target underserved regions)
→ Find the right hook, scale that approach

### **If Funders Aren't Aware Yet:**
❌ Organic discovery isn't fast enough
✅ Research foundation program officers (find right person)
✅ Try: Conference attendance (network directly)
✅ Try: Warm intro from board member (credibility)
✅ Try: Send research directly (show the work)
→ Build awareness through multiple channels

## Decision Framework (When I Adjust)

**I'll pivot strategy when:**
- Metric plateaus for 2+ weeks (e.g., article views stuck at 300)
- Engagement drops week-over-week (sign something isn't landing)
- A channel significantly outperforms others (double down there)
- New data shows different audience interest (follow the signal)

**I won't pivot when:**
- A gate is on track even if slow (patience)
- Variance is normal (1-2 week fluctuations ok)
- Something hasn't had time to compound (give it 3 weeks)
- We've already tested the approach (don't repeat failures)

**I'll escalate to you when:**
- A gate looks unreachable (need strategic input)
- An opportunity conflicts with principles (need values check)
- External partnership decision needed (your call)
- Budget/resource constraint hits (your approval)

## The Creative Experiments I'll Try

### **If content traction is slow:**
- Try different article lengths (short-form vs. long-form)
- Try different angles (data-driven vs. story-driven)
- Try different publication times (morning vs. evening)
- Try different platforms (website vs. LinkedIn vs. Medium)
- Try interactive elements (polls, quizzes, tools)
- Try co-authored pieces (with board members, partners)

### **If media coverage isn't happening:**
- Identify top 20 nonprofit journalists + pitch individually
- Propose exclusive stories (data they haven't seen)
- Sponsor relevant podcasts (reach specific audience)
- Place op-eds (your voice in major publications)
- Create newsworthy moments (board announcements, partnerships)
- Build relationships (long-term, not transactional)

### **If federation adoption is slow:**
- Test different value propositions (traffic? credibility? research?)
- Try different regions (which regions most receptive?)
- Try different platform sizes (big vs. small platforms)
- Offer pilots (low-risk 90-day test)
- Show proof (early adopters' results)
- Build community (federation Slack, monthly calls)

### **If funder awareness is building slowly:**
- Map foundation network (who funds what)
- Identify program officers (right person at each)
- Send curated research (personalized for their focus)
- Get warm intros (through board, partners)
- Attend relevant conferences (build relationships)
- Create talking points (one-pagers for partners to share)

## The Problem: The Invisible 97%

Every year, Americans direct $460 billion to 1.9 million nonprofits. But 97% of those organizations—the community health centers, food banks, refugee resettlement agencies, housing nonprofits working at neighborhood scale—remain functionally invisible to donors.

Donors trust names they recognize. Institutional inertia and marketing budgets create visibility for large organizations. Meanwhile, the small organizations doing irreplaceable work with zero marketing staff struggle to reach the people who would support them most.

**The result:** Billions flow to a predictable set of household names. Thousands of high-impact organizations can't fund basic operations because donors don't know they exist.

This is not a nonprofit problem. It's an *information* problem.

---

## The Opportunity: Transparent Discovery, Not Algorithms

Daanaa is a free, independent nonprofit discovery platform. We do one thing: help people find organizations doing work they care about, backed by honest, public financial data.

**What we are:** A search directory + transparency layer on top of IRS 990 data, ProPublica filings, and nonprofit financial benchmarks. No algorithms rewarding scale. No paid placement. No donor tracking.

**What we're not:** A ratings agency. A donation processor. A funder making judgment calls about which organizations "deserve" support.

We believe donors can make their own decisions when they have clear information. Our job is to organize that information in a way that's respectful, searchable, and fair to organizations of all sizes.

---

## The Model: Public Data, Public Trust

**Core insight:** Nonprofit transparency already exists. It's just scattered across IRS databases, fragmented ProPublica records, and 50 state charity offices. We're assembling it, benchmarking it, and making it usable.

**Current state (as of June 2026):**
- 1.97 million nonprofits indexed
- 537,000 organizations with complete financial data (10-form history)
- Peer-group financial health signals (3 archetypes, 3 revenue bands) for 411,000 orgs
- Full-text search + semantic search (embedding-based)
- Platform live at daanaa.org (1.8M+ monthly potential reach)

**The work we do:**
1. **Ingest public data** — IRS 990-N (annual filings), NCCS (nonprofit core data), ProPublica datasets
2. **Normalize & benchmark** — map organizations to peer groups (NTEE codes), compute financial context (scores, health signals)
3. **Surface discovery** — search by cause, location, financial health; compare similar organizations
4. **Earn trust through transparency** — show our methodology, open corrections, acknowledge data gaps

## Why This Matters Now: The Shift to Informed Giving

Three tailwinds make this moment critical:

1. **Donor demand for transparency is rising.** Gen Z and millennial donors want to understand impact and operations. Traditional charity ratings are under trust pressure. Donors are moving to direct research.

2. **Nonprofit infrastructure is maturing.** Tech-for-nonprofits funding has created the tools and data sources. The ecosystem is ready for a transparency layer that serves *donors* (not just funders).

3. **AI makes this scale possible.** We use local AI (Qwen2.5-32B on our hardware) to generate mission summaries and classify organizations. We are not dependent on expensive cloud APIs. This lets us stay independent and cost-controlled.

**Without Daanaa:** Donors default to Google reputation search and marquee names. Small organizations remain invisible.

**With Daanaa:** Donors can find 1.97 million organizations. Small organizations get a fair shot.

## Our Stewardship Commitment: Why Donors Should Trust Us

We are not a venture startup optimizing for growth and exit. We are a stewardship-first platform. Our commitment:

1. **No paid placement.** Organizations do not pay to be ranked or featured.
2. **Independence protected.** No partner, funder, or donor influences who appears where.
3. **Small organizations treated with dignity.** Scores are benchmarked against peer groups, not the full universe. A $200K food bank is never compared against Kaiser.
4. **Evidence-based trust signals.** Scores come from IRS data, not our opinion. We show our work.
5. **Privacy by default.** We never track donors. We never expose giving activity. The Giving Wallet (bookmarks + intent) stays on your device.
6. **Mistakes are corrected.** We have a public Mistake Registry on every organization page.

This commitment is documented in our Founding Stewardship Commitment (STEWARDSHIP.md in our codebase). Every contributor—human and AI—is bound by it.

## The Revenue Model: Services, Not Data Sales

We will remain free for donors and nonprofits forever. Daanaa is funded by:

1. **Services to nonprofits** (G3 expansion):
   - Nonprofit group purchasing (GPO) for software, insurance, payment processing
   - Visibility services for smaller organizations
   - (Donation letter service: deferred post-legal, G1 review only)

2. **Partnerships with ethical vendors** (Impact Network):
   - Community partners offer nonprofits tools at discounted rates
   - Daanaa earns referral revenue (transparent, opt-in)
   - Partners are vetted for quality and alignment with our mission

3. **Grants** (supporting core platform development):
   - Infrastructure funding from nonprofit + civic tech funders
   - Allows us to stay independent and mission-driven

**We will never:** Sell donor data. Accept paid placement. Trade our independence for funding.

## Red Flags: Stop & Escalate

**If you hear:**
- "We won't bother with COI policy" → Fiduciary duty violation. Stop.
- "Let's keep donor data forever" → GDPR/CCPA violation. Stop.
- "School verification is too complex" → COPPA risk increases. Escalate to Legal.
- "B2B ESG won't influence the platform" → Without formal firewall, P7 at risk. Escalate to Founder.
- "Insurance is too expensive" → Negotiate quotes; don't eliminate coverage. Escalate to Finance.

## Feedback Analysis Framework

### Green Flags (Keep Copy As-Is)
- ✅ "This feels honest, not judgmental"
- ✅ "I'd want donors to see this"
- ✅ "Finally someone gets that small doesn't mean weak"
- ✅ "Shows we're doing ok"

### Yellow Flags (Minor Adjustments)
- ⚠️ "The wording is a bit formal"
- ⚠️ "Wish it showed what we could do to improve"
- ⚠️ "Not sure what 'building reserves' means to my donors"

### Red Flags (Major Reframe Needed)
- ❌ "Feels like we're being ranked"
- ❌ "The word 'caution' still feels like a grade"
- ❌ "Peers in my state are much bigger—not fair comparison"
- ❌ "Why show this publicly?"

---

## Decision Tree

**If all 5 feedback: GREEN**
→ Launch to all nonprofits Monday (no copy changes)

**If 4 GREEN, 1 YELLOW**
→ Make minor copy tweaks, launch Monday

**If 3+ YELLOW or any RED**
→ Stop, re-draft copy, test again Thu-Fri, launch Friday instead

**If any RED about comparison being unfair**
→ Check peer grouping logic. If flawed, pause entire launch (escalate to Akbar)

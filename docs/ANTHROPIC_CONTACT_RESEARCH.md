## Key Facts About Anthropic's Partnership Model

- **No traditional grants program.** Anthropic doesn't do "apply for funding like a foundation."
- **Partnerships are strategic.** They partner with organizations that demonstrate responsible AI in production.
- **They care about your thesis, not your fundraising.** If you're solving a real problem with their tools, they notice.
- **They move fast.** Decisions can happen in 2–4 weeks for the right opportunity.
- **They have precedent.** Anthropic has partnered with research orgs, nonprofits, and civic-tech companies.

## Before You Reach Out: Prepare This

### 1. Your Unique Angle

**Not:** "We built a nonprofit discovery platform and we use Claude."

**Yes:** "We're demonstrating responsible AI at scale in a public-good context. We use Claude for advanced reasoning, but we run semantic search locally, keep data private, and make humans the final decision-makers. We're proof that your thesis works."

### 2. What You Actually Want

Pick one (or ask for both, but make it clear what you need most):

**Option A: Funding** ($100–250K for infrastructure + operations)

**Option B: Technology Partnership** (API credits, priority support, research access)

**Option C: Research Collaboration** (case study, joint publication, methodology review)

**Option D: All of the above**

### 3. Proof of Work

Prepare a 2-minute demo or description of what Daanaa does. Link to:
- Public site (daanaa.org)
- One-page methodology document (if you have it)
- GitHub repos (if applicable)

## Outreach Email Template

**Subject line options:**

- "Responsible AI in civic infrastructure — partnership opportunity"
- "Daanaa + Anthropic: case study in responsible AI at scale"
- "Anthropic partnership inquiry: responsible AI in public goods"

**Email (keep it short — 150–200 words):**

---

**Hi [Name],**

I'm building Daanaa, a free nonprofit-discovery platform that helps donors understand organizational context using public IRS data and Claude AI.

What makes this relevant to Anthropic: **We're demonstrating your thesis in production.**

- **Safe:** Scoring is deterministic from public data, not learned models. AI is used for semantic search and mission tagging; humans make all final decisions.
- **Steerable:** We run inference locally (Qwen2.5 + mxbai-embed on a home server), keep data private by design, and publish our methodology openly.
- **Beneficial:** We're nonprofit infrastructure that serves 1.87M organizations and their donors. No paid placement, no favoritism, complete transparency.

We're launching publicly Aug 15 and raising $400K to scale responsibly. We're looking for partners who care about this kind of work — both to support the mission and to demonstrate that responsible AI can scale.

Would you be open to a 20-minute conversation about partnership possibilities? Happy to show you what we're building.

Best,
Akbar

---

### Why This Works

- ✅ Leads with **what you're doing**, not what you need
- ✅ Maps directly to **Anthropic's mission** (safe, steerable, beneficial)
- ✅ Shows **production proof**, not theory
- ✅ Asks for **conversation**, not money (lowers barrier)
- ✅ Short and respectful of their time

## Alternative: Warm Intro Route

**If you can get an introduction:**

Find someone who knows someone at Anthropic. LinkedIn is your friend here. Look for:
- Anthropic employees on LinkedIn
- Mutual connections (use LinkedIn's "people you may know")
- Anthropic research papers — find authors, see if any went to a university you have ties to

**Ask your mutual connection:**

"Hi [Person], I'm building something I think [Anthropic Person] would find interesting — a case study in responsible AI governance. Would you be willing to introduce us?"

Most people say yes if:
1. You're clear about why it's relevant to them
2. You offer to keep the intro email short
3. You're respectful of their time

## What to Say If Anthropic Responds

**If they want to chat:**

"Great! I'm available [give 3 time slots in next 7 days]. We can do 20 minutes — I just want to gauge interest in partnership and see if there's a fit."

**If they ask for more info:**

Send them:
1. **One-page overview** (what Daanaa does, why Anthropic should care)
2. **Stewardship Commitment** (shows you're serious about governance)
3. **Link to daanaa.org**

Don't send a 50-page proposal. Keep it tight.

**If they say "not right now":**

"Got it. We'll have real data by late August (sandbox testing, user feedback). Would you be open to reconnecting then?"

## Success Metrics

**You'll know you nailed it if:**

- Anthropic responds within 1 week
- They ask for more information (not a rejection, a sign of interest)
- They schedule a call
- They show interest in research collaboration (strong signal)

**Realistic expectations for cold outreach:** 20–30% chance of response (Anthropic is selective), 10% chance of partnership conversation, 5% chance of actual partnership. But if you get the partnership, it's high-credibility and validates your responsible AI approach.

## Your Key Decisions

### Decision 1: Student Account Model (Advisory) ⚠️ **INFORMATIONAL**

**Question:** Parent-dependent, student-owned, or tiered by age?

**Your constituent angle:** Student privacy, parental transparency, and youth engagement.

| Model | Constituent Transparency | Youth Privacy | Parental Awareness |
|--------|---|---|---|
| **Parent-dependent** | ✅ High (parents in loop) | ✅ Protected (parent mediates) | ✅ Yes (parents know) |
| **Student-owned** | ⚠️ Medium (students choose) | ⚠️ Lower (students alone) | ⚠️ May not know |
| **Tiered (school-verified)** | ✅ High (school verifies) | ✅ Protected (school mediates) | ✅ Yes (via school) |

**Public messaging:**
- Parent-dependent or tiered = "Students volunteer safely, with parent/school oversight"
- Student-owned = "Students have autonomy to volunteer" (but privacy concerns)

**Recommendation:** Tiered (school-verified). Strongest public messaging: "Schools verify students, Daanaa facilitates safe volunteering."

### Decision 4: Geographic Expansion ⚠️ **VOTE NEEDED**

**Question:** Houston only, selective (3–5 cities), or national?

**Your constituent angle:** Community benefit, equity, and constituent impact.

| Model | Year 1 | Year 2 | Public Messaging | Equity |
|--------|---|---|---|---|
| **Houston only** | 1 city, deep | Potential expansion | "We're rooted in Houston" | Single region |
| **Selective (3–5 cities)** | 3–5 cities, moderate depth | Proven model, scale | "We're growing where it matters" | Multi-region equity |
| **National** | 50 states (risky) | National scale | "We're everywhere" | Maximum equity |

**Public commitment implications:**
- Houston only: "We're committed to Houston's youth and nonprofits" (strong message, limited scope).
- Selective: "We're growing responsibly, starting with Houston" (scalable message, managed expansion).
- National: "We're a national platform for all students" (ambitious message, execution risk).

**Constituent benefit:**
- Houston only: Deep impact, high constituent engagement (your district gets 100% focus).
- Selective: Regional expansion (your district + neighbors get attention).
- National: Equity concern (rural areas may have lower adoption).

**Recommendation:** Selective (3–5 cities including Houston in Year 2). Balances depth + equity. Strong public messaging: "We scale thoughtfully."

### Decision 7: Volunteer Hour Constraints (Advisory) ⚠️ **INFORMATIONAL**

**Question:** Flexible (unlimited), moderate (8h/day), or strict (5h/day) hour limits?

**Your constituent angle:** Student safety, overcommitment risk, and constituent optics.

| Model | Safety | Optics | Constituent Impact |
|--------|---|---|---|
| **Flexible** | ❌ Lower (burnout risk) | ⚠️ "Students work too hard?" | Potential pushback |
| **Moderate (8h/day)** | ✅ Good (reasonable limit) | ✅ "Students balance school + service" | Positive |
| **Strict (5h/day)** | ✅ Safest (very protected) | ✅ "Daanaa protects student wellbeing" | Very positive |

**Public messaging:**
- Flexible = "Students choose their own pace" (but burnout concerns)
- Moderate = "Students volunteer responsibly (8 hours/day max)" (balanced)
- Strict = "Daanaa prioritizes student wellbeing over hours" (strongest message)

**Recommendation:** Moderate (8h/day). Good optics + safety balance. Public message: "We support student volunteers without overcommitting them."

### Decision 6: Minimum Student Age (Adjacent) ⚠️ **INFORMATIONAL**

**Question:** 13+, 16+, or 18+ minimum age?

**Your constituent angle:** Age-appropriate engagement and youth development.

| Age | Development Stage | Messaging | Constituent Fit |
|-----|---|---|---|
| **13+** | Middle school (complex, variable maturity) | "Daanaa supports early youth engagement" | Medium (broader reach) |
| **16+** | High school (volunteer-ready) | "Daanaa supports high school service" | High (aligns with graduation requirements) |
| **18+** | College/adults (limited youth) | "Daanaa supports college service" (not youth-focused) | Low (loses youth mission) |

**Your district context:**
- Most school districts require 50–100 volunteer hours for high school graduation.
- 16+ aligns with natural graduation requirement cycle.
- 13+ requires more supervision (school verification important).

**Recommendation:** 16+ minimum age (if school-verified). Aligns with graduation requirements, clearer youth development story.

**Date:** 2026-07-22  
**Question:** Can Daanaa eventually build a Staxx-like model for nonprofits?

---

## The Short Answer

**Yes, it's technically possible. But:**
- ⏰ Timeline: 5-10 years minimum

## What Staxx Model Looks Like (For Reference)

```
Staxx's Business:
├─ They process payments between businesses
├─ They handle compliance (PCI, money transmitter, fraud)
├─ They take 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
├─ They own the customer relationship
├─ Their margins: ~40-50% (after infra costs)
└─ They're valued at $400M+ (startup stage)

Why it works for them:
  Volume: Process $100B+/year
  Margin: Own the fee (not sharing with anyone)
  Stickiness: Businesses use them for payroll, invoicing, etc.
  Defensibility: Hard to compete once they have customers
```

## The Decision Framework

**Ask yourself:**

1. **Mission:** Do you want to be a discovery platform or a fintech?
   - Discovery = stay focused, scale faster, exit earlier
   - Fintech = complex, slower, higher upside if it works

2. **Expertise:** Do you want to build payment infrastructure?
   - Yes = hire experienced fintech team ($2-3M/year)
   - No = partner with Staxx or Stripe ($0)

3. **Timeline:** How long until exit?
   - 5-8 years = stay discovery (easier path)
   - 10+ years = can build fintech (time to invest)

4. **Economics:** What margin do you need to win?
   - 0.5-1% referral = fine (pure discovery)
   - 2.6% own processor = needed (must scale hard)

5. **Moat:** What defensibility matters most?
   - Discovery trust = hard for fintech to replicate
   - Full stack = hard for others to replicate better
   - Different types of defensibility

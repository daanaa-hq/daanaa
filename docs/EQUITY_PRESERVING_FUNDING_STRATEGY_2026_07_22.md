# Equity-Preserving Funding Strategy (For-Profit)
## No Nonprofit Grants. No Dilution Unless Unavoidable.

**Core Rule:** Don't raise equity capital unless revenue can't sustain the next stage. Bootstrap as far as possible. Non-dilutive financing (revenue-based, debt) is preferred over equity.

---

## Why This Matters

**Nonprofit model (previous roadmap):** Grants + impact investment + equity raises. You lose control.

**For-profit model (this roadmap):** Bootstrap → early revenue → optional equity only if scaling past unit economics. You stay in control.

**The numbers:**
- Nonprofit: raise $100K at 10% equity = lose 10% ownership
- For-profit: bootstrap $50K founder capital + $30K revenue + $20K revenue-based financing = $100K, own 100%

## When to Raise Equity (If at All)

**Only raise equity if ONE of these is true:**

1. **Explosive opportunity:** 10x growth opportunity requiring $500K+ capital NOW (e.g., a major corporation wants to partner, requires 6-month buildout)
2. **Market window closing:** Competitor moving fast; must raise to outpace
3. **Revenue plateau:** Can't grow faster than revenue supports; need capital to hire ahead of revenue
4. **Founder burnout:** Founder needs salary lift or equity-aligned co-founder joining

**Otherwise: Stay self-funded.**

### If You Must Raise Equity (Stage 4+)

**Strategy: Raise from position of strength, minimize dilution**

**By the time you raise:**
- $500K+ annual revenue (proof of concept)
- $250K+ net income (profitability)
- 100+ schools, 50K+ students (scale proof)
- 3+ years of operating history

**Valuation:** With $500K revenue + 50% margins, you're worth $2–3M (3–5x revenue multiple)
- $500K raise at $2.5M post = 20% dilution (acceptable)
- $500K raise at $1M post = 33% dilution (too much, don't do this)

**How to negotiate hard:**
- "We're already cash-flow positive at $X revenue. This capital is optional acceleration, not survival."
- Investors know this. You have leverage. Use it.

**Equity terms to demand:**
- 1x liquidation preference (not multi-clause)
- No board seat (you keep control)
- No pro-rata rights (they can't dilute you later)
- Vesting schedule for founder equity (standard: 4-year, 1-year cliff)

## Non-Dilutive Financing Options (If Needed Between Stages)

**If you run low on cash but don't want to raise equity:**

### Option 1: Revenue-Based Financing (RBF)
- **How it works:** Lender gives you $50–100K. You repay 5–10% of monthly revenue until they've recouped principal + interest.
- **Cost:** ~30–40% of borrowed amount (e.g., borrow $50K, repay $65–70K)
- **Advantage:** No dilution, no board seats, repay from revenue
- **When to use:** Stage 2–3, when revenue is growing but not yet cash-flow positive
- **Providers:** Clearco, Pipe, Lighter Capital, Uncapped

### Option 2: Founder Loan
- **How it works:** You loan your company money from personal savings/consulting profits.
- **Cost:** 0% interest (you're the lender)
- **Advantage:** No dilution, flexible repayment
- **When to use:** Bridge between revenue cycles (e.g., need $20K in Month 3, but revenue hits Month 4)
- **Term:** Repay with interest once cash-flow positive (0% or 5% interest to yourself)

### Option 3: Customer Advances
- **How it works:** Ask schools to prepay 3–6 months upfront (e.g., "pay $600 now, get 6 months for $100/month")
- **Cost:** 0% (you're just accelerating revenue)
- **Advantage:** Validates customers (they pay upfront = they're serious)
- **When to use:** Stage 2, when adding new schools
- **Realistic:** 30–40% of new schools will prepay

### Option 4: Consulting Revenue (Bridge)
- **How it works:** Founder/team does billable consulting for EcoMargins clients to cover salary gaps
- **Cost:** 0% (you're earning revenue)
- **Advantage:** Keeps founder + team funded while product scales
- **When to use:** Y1–Y2, if product revenue is lumpy
- **Realistic:** Founder can bill $100–150K/year from consulting side gigs

## The Bottom Line

**Don't raise equity capital for normal operations.** Bootstrap.

**Only raise when:**
- Revenue can't sustain the opportunity, OR
- There's a 10x market window you'd miss otherwise

**Metrics before raising equity:**
- $300K+ annual revenue
- Positive unit economics (each customer is profitable)
- Clear path to $500K+ revenue without capital

**Negotiating position matters.** By the time you raise equity from a position of revenue + profitability, you have leverage. You can demand better terms (lower dilution, less control loss).

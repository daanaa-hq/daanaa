# Donor Retention: The Real Problem Daanaa Solves

## The Statistic

**Nonprofit donor retention rate: ~43–45% year-over-year** (varies by study, org size, giving level)

- **Your estimate of 20%:** Likely you're thinking of *first-time donor* retention specifically. First-time donors have a 17–20% retention rate — they give once, often don't return.
- **Returning donors:** 43–50% retention year-over-year (better, but still losing half the base annually)
- **Major donors (>$1,000):** ~60–70% retention (commitment is higher)

### Sources
- AFP (Association of Fundraising Professionals) annual studies
- Chronicle of Philanthropy donor surveys
- Nonprofit Tech for Good benchmarks

**The implication:** If a nonprofit loses 55–60% of donors annually, they spend massive time + money on acquisition. The "donor treadmill" is one of the biggest nonprofit infrastructure problems.

---

## Why Retention Fails (Current Model)

**Typical donor journey:**
1. Discover org (Google, word-of-mouth, Facebook ad)
2. Give
3. ... silence for months (org is busy, donor forgets org exists)
4. Donor either: (a) gives to a different org, or (b) stops giving

**Why:** Nonprofits have no easy way to re-engage. Email is their only tool. But:
- Email lists decay (people change emails)
- Engagement fatigue (too many asks)
- No personalized reason to give *again* to *this org*

## How Daanaa Solves Retention

### 1. **The Giving Wallet: Repeated Discovery**

Current Daanaa: Donors bookmark orgs (Giving Wallet). But we're not *using* this as a retention tool yet.

**Retention opportunity:**
- Donor bookmarks an org on daanaa.org
- 3 months later, Daanaa reminds them: "You bookmarked [Org]. Here's what they've been doing."
- Include new mission update, financial health snapshot, recent news
- One-click give-again link (to verified donation URL)

**Psychology:** Reduces friction. Instead of "search again," it's "continue your support."

**What this requires:** 
- Optional email opt-in from donors (at wallet save)
- Quarterly digest + monthly option
- Privacy: never expose giving history, only bookmarks
- Org can add "impact stories" to their Daanaa page (future feature)

### 2. **Letter Service: Proof of Impact**

Daanaa enables orgs to issue automatic donation receipts + impact letters.

**Retention angle:**
- Donor gives
- Day 1: Daanaa issues IRS letter automatically
- Day 30: Org can send impact update via Daanaa letter template ("Thanks to gifts like yours, we...")
- Day 90: Quarterly email (Daanaa or org) with outcomes

**Effect:** Donors see impact. They're more likely to give again.

**Research:** Nonprofits with good impact communication see +15–20% repeat gift rate.

### 3. **Peer Comparison: Smart Re-engagement**

**Feature idea (post-G2):**
- Donor gives to a food bank
- 6 months later, Daanaa shows: "Other donors supporting [Food Bank] are also giving to [3 similar orgs in your city]."
- Example: "Food bank + housing nonprofit + job training are all addressing hunger + stability together."

**Effect:** Donors realize they can support a *portfolio* of related orgs, not just one. Increases total giving + stickiness.

### 4. **Claiming as Retention Anchor**

When nonprofits claim their Daanaa profile:
- They enable letter service (automatic receipts → repeat gifts)
- They can add impact stories (proof of what donations accomplish)
- They become discoverable to *new* donors (acquisition) AND re-engageable to *existing* donors

**Flywheel:**
- Donor finds org on Daanaa
- Gives + gets letter service
- Gets impact updates
- Sees org in peer recommendations
- Gives again

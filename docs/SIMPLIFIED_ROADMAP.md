# Simplified Roadmap (Letter Service Deferred)

**Updated:** 2026-06-15
**Status:** Core retention solution locked in. Letter service moved to future opportunities.

---

## Why We Removed Letter Service (Now)

**Original concern:** Letter service requires storing donation transaction data (email + amount + date), which violates stewardship principle #8: "We do not touch money or transaction data."

**Decision:** Remove from current roadmap. Revisit post-legal (G1) as optional future revenue opportunity only if attorney confirms it's structurally sound.

**Benefit:** Keeps us clean as a pure discovery platform. No transaction involvement. No additional complexity.

---

## The Simplified Retention Solution

**Core:** Bookmarks + one-click giving + trust signals (financial health + peer benchmarks)

**No organ burden.** No donor surveillance. No transaction involvement. No dark patterns.

**Behavioral psychology:** Removes cognitive friction without creating pressure or guilt.

## Retention Strategy (Locked In)

**Problem:** Donors forget orgs exist. Searching is hard. Trust is uncertain.

**Solution (behavioral):**
1. **Easy rediscovery** — Bookmarks keep orgs in inventory
2. **Low friction** — One-click giving (no re-search)
3. **Trust signals** — Financial health + peer benchmarks
4. **Autonomy** — No nudging, no pressure, no tracking
5. **No org burden** — They focus on mission, not Daanaa updates

**Result:** Retention improves naturally. Orgs benefit without new work. Donors feel respected.

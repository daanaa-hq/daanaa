# Daanaa | Founder Catalyst Working Packet

**Status:** Working draft for founder review  
**Date:** 2026-07-21  
**Use:** Application preparation and program conversation only. Not a public promise.

## One sentence

Daanaa is building public information infrastructure that helps donors find and understand smaller nonprofits without selling nonprofit visibility, taking a percentage of giving, or turning incomplete public data into a verdict.

## The problem

Many smaller nonprofits have public records but lack a clear, stable, respectful public presence. Donors may want to help but cannot easily find the organization, understand its context, or distinguish what is known from what is missing.

Daanaa treats this as an information problem, not a failure of nonprofit worth.

## What exists today

- A live public nonprofit discovery platform at https://daanaa.org
- Public profiles grounded in IRS and related public records
- Source linked methodology and limitations
- Peer Financial Context presented as context, not a ranking of mission quality
- A stewardship charter covering donor neutrality, nonprofit privacy, and AI transparency
- A self funded, founder led build operated as a DBA of EcoMargins Consulting LLC

## Proposed community pilot

If useful to the cohort, Daanaa would work with a small number of Alabama nonprofits that are difficult for donors to find. The pilot would:

1. Select organizations using public data and local guidance, not payment or prominence.
2. Publish only source linked public information and clearly label uncertainty.
3. Invite each nonprofit to correct or enrich its page voluntarily.
4. Ask founders and nonprofit leaders what makes the experience useful or burdensome.
5. Report what changed, what was wrong, and what should not be automated.

This is a proposed learning project, not a promise of coverage or endorsement. Any organization would retain control over its own contributed information.

## Verified Alabama example

[Project Hope to Abolish the Death Penalty](https://daanaa.org/org/391655242) is a live Daanaa profile for a Lanett, Alabama nonprofit. The profile is based on public information and should be treated as an example for review, not as an endorsement or a claim that the organization has participated in the pilot.

## What I want from the program

- Honest customer and nonprofit discovery feedback
- Help narrowing the first measurable use case
- Guidance on responsible growth for a mission constrained platform
- Mentors who understand public data, civic technology, and social impact
- A realistic view of whether a community pilot can create value without burdening nonprofits

## Constraints and disclosures

- The founder works full time and can commit focused time, including limited travel, but cannot participate as a full time founder.
- Daanaa is currently a DBA of EcoMargins Consulting LLC, a for profit entity.
- The project is self funded and is not designed to make money from nonprofit funding, donor funds, paid placement, or financial visibility.
- No nonprofit should be asked to provide private financial information for the pilot.
- No program relationship should be described as an endorsement without written permission.

## Questions before committing

1. Is the current DBA structure eligible?
2. What are the exact weekly and in person commitments?
3. Are travel, lodging, or participation costs supported?
4. Can the pilot be scoped to a small number of organizations?
5. What information may be shared with mentors, judges, or program partners?
6. Can the founder participate without changing Daanaa's stewardship commitments?

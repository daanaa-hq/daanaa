## Your Key Decisions

### Decision 9: Donor Account Structure ⚠️ **VOTE NEEDED**

**Question:** Single profile, Head of Household + co-givers, or household entity accounts?

**Your angle:** DAF integration, household giving aggregation, and tax-aligned receipts.

| Model | DAF Alignment | Tax Clarity | Household Coordination | Recommendation |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| **Single** | Lower (fragmented) | Unclear (each person separate) | Poor (wife and husband separate accounts) | ❌ Not recommended |
| **Head of Household + Co-Givers** | ✅ Higher (HoH gets consolidated receipt) | ✅ Clear (one receipt to tax filer) | ✅ Good (spouse/family coordinated) | ✅ Recommended |
| **Household Entity** | Highest (cleanest) | Highest (entity-level receipt) | Highest (no individual accounts) | ⚠️ Too complex |

**DAF perspective:**
- DAF donors (Vanguard Charitable, Fidelity Giving Fund, etc.) often make household giving decisions with spouse/family.
- **Head of Household model** aligns with how DAF filings work (head of household files the return and receives the consolidated receipt).
- When Akbar (HoH) and spouse both give, Akbar gets one consolidated receipt (spouse's giving attributed to his name on the tax return).
- DAF distributions can go to household account, aggregated under HoH.

**Example flow:**
```
Akbar (head of household) opens Daanaa:
  - Creates household account (primary)
  - Invites spouse as co-giver
  - Both can propose orgs, add to household wallet
  - Giving is aggregated (both contributions shown)
  - Tax receipt goes to Akbar (the HoH/filer)
  - Akbar's Fidelity DAF distributions → household wallet
  - Spouse's personal giving → same household wallet
  - Year-end: Single consolidated receipt to Akbar for all giving
```

**Recommendation:** Head of Household + co-givers. Tax-clear, DAF-aligned, family-coordinated giving.

**Action items for DAF:**
- [ ] Coordinate with DAF platforms on household account linking (HoH primary, DAF distributions flow to household)
- [ ] Head-of-household tax receipt format (shows all co-giver contributions)
- [ ] DAF donor onboarding (how household accounts work with DAF)

### Decision 13: Data Retention & Privacy Policy ⚠️ **VOTE NEEDED**

**Question:** Permanent, 7-year, 1-year, or hybrid PII + aggregates?

**Your angle:** Donor privacy (Stewardship P2) and donor data handling.

| Option | Donor Privacy | DAF Compliance | Tax Documentation | Recommendation |
|--------|---|---|---|---|
| **Permanent** | ❌ Violates (no deletion right) | ❌ Risky (donor discomfort) | ✅ Safer (long history) | ❌ Not recommended |
| **7-Year** | ✅ Honors deletion (GDPR/CCPA compliant) | ✅ Safe (donor comfortable) | ✅ OK (IRS 7-year retention) | ✅ Recommended |
| **1-Year** | ✅ Honors deletion | ✅ Safe | ❌ Risky (IRS audit if <7yr) | ⚠️ Compliant but risky |
| **Hybrid** | ✅ Honors deletion (PII) | ✅ Safe | ✅ OK (7-yr PII + ∞ agg) | ✅ Best option |

**DAF donor perspective:**
- DAF donors care deeply about privacy. Many DAF funds specifically market "anonymous giving" (donor identity hidden from org).
- Donors expect data deletion if they request it.
- Permanent data retention signals "we track your giving forever," which erodes donor trust.

**Recommendation:** Option D (Hybrid). PII deleted after 7 years (donor privacy + GDPR/CCPA/COPPA-compliant). Aggregate stats (total giving, not donor names) retained indefinite (no privacy downside).

**Action items for DAF:**
- [ ] Coordinate deletion process with DAF platforms (Fidelity, Vanguard, etc.)
- [ ] Create donor data deletion request workflow (respond within 30 days)
- [ ] Update privacy policy to reflect 7-year PII deletion + indefinite aggregates

### Decision 10: Long-Term Revenue Model (Adjacent) ⚠️ **ADVISORY**

**Question:** How does Daanaa sustain itself? Free forever, freemium, B2B ESG, or hybrid?

**Your angle:** DAF partner relationships and donor-facing revenue models.

| Model | DAF Impact | Concern |
|-------|---|---|
| **Forever Free** | ✅ Best for DAF (no friction) | Fundraising risk |
| **Freemium** | ⚠️ Medium (premium features might fragment) | Might limit school/nonprofit adoption |
| **B2B ESG** | ⚠️ Medium (corporate giving product separate) | Separate from DAF platform |
| **Hybrid** | ✅ Good (B2B separate from donor-facing) | Firewall needed |

**DAF consideration:**
- DAF platforms are skeptical of freemium models (creates "lite vs. premium" confusion for donors).
- DAF platforms love forever-free (maximum adoption, maximum charity distribution).
- B2B ESG revenue (separate from DAF) is fine, as long as it doesn't interfere with donor experience.

**Recommendation from DAF perspective:** Hybrid (donor-facing forever free, B2B ESG separate). Keeps DAF flow clean.

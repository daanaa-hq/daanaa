# Ten-Slide Presentation Outline

## 1. The nonprofit-discovery problem
- Purpose: show why the problem exists.
- Headline: "Public nonprofit data exists, but context is fragmented."
- Talking points: discovery is noisy; trust is hard; donors lack context.
- Diagram: problem statement.
- Evidence: homepage and stewardship docs.
- Do not say: Daanaa certifies nonprofits.

## 2. Daanaa’s mission and users
- Purpose: define users and mission.
- Headline: "Help people discover nonprofits with context."
- Talking points: donors, nonprofits, partners, researchers.
- Diagram: user map.
- Evidence: homepage, for-nonprofits page.
- Do not say: rating agency.

## 3. The platform workflow
- Purpose: show end-to-end platform shape.
- Headline: "Public data in, contextual discovery out."
- Talking points: ingest, normalize, calculate, index, present.
- Diagram: platform architecture.
- Evidence: backend and scripts.
- Do not say: all data is human-verified.

## 4. The nonprofit-data pipeline
- Purpose: explain source-to-profile lineage.
- Headline: "From public filings to structured profiles."
- Talking points: public sources, normalization, matching, dedupe.
- Diagram: data lineage.
- Evidence: scripts and DB schema.
- Do not say: every field is complete.

## 5. Search, context and scoring
- Purpose: explain the public-facing decision support layer.
- Headline: "Context, not a verdict."
- Talking points: peer comparison, search relevance, score language.
- Diagram: scoring and search flow.
- Evidence: API types, search code, stewardship docs.
- Do not say: objective quality score.

## 6. Donor and nonprofit experiences
- Purpose: show product surfaces.
- Headline: "Two audiences, one public dataset."
- Talking points: donor search, nonprofit claim, corrections.
- Diagram: donor and nonprofit journeys.
- Evidence: frontend pages, claim tests.
- Do not say: donations are processed in-platform.

## 7. How AI helped me build and review the platform
- Purpose: separate AI help from implementation.
- Headline: "AI as a development partner, not the authority."
- Talking points: research, drafting, review, documentation.
- Diagram: human + AI workflow.
- Evidence: governance docs and AI-related scripts.
- Do not say: AI made business decisions.

## 8. Responsible AI and human oversight
- Purpose: show governance.
- Headline: "Human accountability stays visible."
- Talking points: explainable outputs, approval gates, corrective paths.
- Diagram: governance diagram.
- Evidence: stewardship docs, principle tests.
- Do not say: fully transparent or bias-free.

## 9. Gaps discovered and improvements planned
- Purpose: be honest about limits.
- Headline: "What is partial, missing, or needs cleanup."
- Talking points: terminology drift, workflow completeness, testing gaps.
- Diagram: gap map.
- Evidence: audit gap register.
- Do not say: the system is finished.

## 10. Lessons and future direction
- Purpose: show learning and next phase.
- Headline: "Build with evidence, govern the language."
- Talking points: small-team discipline, auditability, user trust, next steps.
- Diagram: roadmap.
- Evidence: decisions, lessons, audit findings.
- Do not say: performance or adoption results without evidence.

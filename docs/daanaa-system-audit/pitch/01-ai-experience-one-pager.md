# AI Experience One-Pager

Daanaa is a real example of using AI-assisted development inside a human-accountable product workflow.

## What I did

- Defined the nonprofit-discovery problem and the stewardship constraints.
- Used AI-assisted help to explore architecture, review code, and draft documentation.
- Kept the platform language aligned with what the implementation could actually support.

## What the software does automatically

- Ingests public nonprofit data.
- Calculates peer-context outputs.
- Builds search indexes.
- Serves discovery pages and claim flows.

## What remains human-controlled

- Claim review and administrative decisions.
- Public wording for sensitive concepts.
- Governance choices about ranking, trust, and verification language.

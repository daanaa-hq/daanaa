# 90-Second Story

Daanaa started from a simple problem: donors can see lots of nonprofit data, but it is hard to compare organizations fairly or understand them in context.

I translated that into a platform that organizes public nonprofit data, calculates contextual comparisons, and lets donors and nonprofits interact without turning the product into a payment processor or a rating agency.

AI helped me move faster on research, review, and documentation, but the accountable decisions stayed human. I kept the language tight around what the system actually does: public-data discovery, deterministic calculations, and human-reviewed claim and governance flows.

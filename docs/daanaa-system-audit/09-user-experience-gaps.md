# User Experience Gaps

## Donor experience

- The homepage and directory are clear about discovery and context, but the language stack still mixes score, context, tier, badge, and ranking concepts.
- Volunteer matching is shown as coming soon in the homepage tiles.
- The repo does not confirm a complete on-platform donation completion flow.

## Nonprofit experience

- Claim flow exists and is tested.
- Public profile correction paths exist in the product vocabulary, but the exact user-facing correction loop should be revalidated in the live UI.
- There is evidence of nonprofit dashboards, verification, and content systems, but the public explanation of what is editable versus reviewed can be sharpened.

## Accessibility and clarity

- The homepage includes strong visual design intent.
- Some trust and scoring terminology likely needs standardization so donors do not interpret context as certification.

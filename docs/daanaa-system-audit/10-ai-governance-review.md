# AI Governance Review

## What is confirmed

- Stewardship requires evidence-based trust signals, privacy protection, human accountability, and explainable AI use.
- The codebase uses AI-assisted enrichment in some scripts, deterministic scoring in others, and human review in the claim/admin flow.
- The repo contains tests and comments that explicitly reject unsupported claims about AI, payments, or trust.

## AI language and decision governance

- Daanaa should not describe deterministic peer-context calculations as “AI” unless a model is actually involved.
- Daanaa should not present a contextual score as proof of quality, impact, or safety.
- Human review is real in some places, but public wording should consistently explain where software calculates and where humans approve.

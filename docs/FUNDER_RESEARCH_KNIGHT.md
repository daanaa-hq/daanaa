## Talking Points (In conversation order)

### Opening (2 min)
"I'm building Daanaa, a free nonprofit-discovery platform that helps donors understand organizational context — financial health, peer benchmarks, funding models. We're using public IRS data transparently to strengthen civic participation.

We're launching publicly in August, and we're looking for founding partners who believe in this kind of work. We're not asking for traditional grants — we're recruiting believers in civic infrastructure who want to be part of the founding story."

### Why This Is Civic Participation (2 min)
"Informed giving *is* informed civic participation. Right now, donors make choices based on reputation and size, not real financial health. That skews capital toward visible mega-nonprofits and away from smaller, specialized, high-performing organizations doing deep community work.

Daanaa fixes that. We make it possible for donors to discover and support the organizations they actually care about, not just the ones with the biggest marketing budget. That's civic participation."

### The Ask (1 min)
"We're looking for 3–5 founding partners. $400K over 18 months. Partners get: you're funding civic infrastructure, advisory role, public credibility (founding partner status), and a case study on how AI can strengthen trust in institutions.

In exchange, you sign a Stewardship Commitment — an agreement protecting our independence, no paid placement, full donor privacy, fairness to small orgs."

### Why Independence Matters (1 min)
"For Daanaa to work as civic infrastructure, we have to be independent. Founders have to trust that our scores aren't tilted. That's why we ask partners to sign a commitment that protects that independence. It actually protects *you* too — you know your funding isn't going to something that could become a reputational risk."

### Pause for questions / objections

**Common Knight question:** "How do you ensure donor privacy while surfacing giving context?"
**Answer:** "It's structural. The Giving Wallet (where donors bookmark orgs) is local-first and account-free. We never capture transaction data, never track giving behavior, never sell insights. Donors have full control."

**Common Knight question:** "What about the 'discovery' angle — how do you prevent this from becoming Instagram for nonprofits?"
**Answer:** "We focus on *context*, not performance. We highlight hidden gems (small, financially healthy, overlooked orgs) specifically to shift capital toward overlooked high performers. It's additive, not gamified."

**Common Knight question:** "Why not a nonprofit structure?"
**Answer:** "We wanted to keep the platform free forever and avoid grant-dependent revenue. A nonprofit would eventually need to chase grants or turn to commercial partnerships. By staying for-profit, we protect independence through Stewardship Commitment, not by chasing 501(c)(3) status. Our attorney is helping us confirm this is legally sound."

## How to Handle Hesitation

**If they say:** "This is interesting, but it's a new model for us"
**You say:** "Totally fair. That's why we're looking for partners who get the vision early and want to be part of building it. No pressure. I'm just testing the water to see if this resonates."

**If they say:** "We fund journalists and civic engagement, not tech platforms"
**You say:** "I get it. We're not a tech company chasing growth — we're civic infrastructure. Think of us as an information tool that makes it possible for donors to make informed civic decisions. That's exactly what you fund."

**If they say:** "We'd want to see results before committing"
**Answer:** "We're sandbox-testing from Aug 1–15 with 50 volunteer nonprofits. We'll have real data by late August. Would you want to stay in the conversation and see those results before deciding?"

## Red Flags (When NOT to pursue)

- "Can you feature stories of our grantees to boost their visibility?" → **Not a fit.** (That's paid placement.)
- "We'd want editorial control over the platform" → **Not a fit.** (Stewardship Principle 7: Independence.)
- "This seems risky — we only fund proven news organizations" → **Okay,** but note they may need to see sandbox results first.

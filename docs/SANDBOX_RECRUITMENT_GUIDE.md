# Sandbox Recruitment Guide — 50 Nonprofits (Aug 1–15)

**Goal:** Recruit 50 real nonprofits to beta test Daanaa before public launch Aug 15  
**Timeline:** Aug 1–10 recruitment, Aug 10–15 active testing  
**Owner:** Akbar (recruitment) + Engineer (technical support during testing)

---

## What Is This For?

50 nonprofits will:
1. Claim their profiles on Daanaa (test the claiming flow + agent)
2. Provide feedback on UX, profile visibility, search quality
3. Test the Giving Wallet feature (if donors included)
4. Report bugs + usability issues

**In exchange:** Early access, public recognition as "beta partners," direct input into feature requests for Phase 2.

---

## Selection Criteria

### Target Profile

✅ **Good fit for sandbox:**
- Active 501(c)(3) with filed 990 in last 2 years
- Engaged leadership team (will respond to testing emails)
- Located in US (simplifies testing)
- Revenue $50K–$5M (captures small + mid-size, not mega-orgs)
- Has a website with email contact listed

❌ **Avoid:**
- Defunct or inactive orgs
- Just-formed nonprofits (no 990 history)
- No website or email contact
- Mega-orgs (>$100M)—they get attention anyway

### Geographic Diversity

Aim for ~5–10 orgs per region:
- **Texas** (5–10) — local focus
- **California** (5–10) — tech-friendly
- **Northeast** (5–10) — concentrated nonprofits
- **Midwest** (5–10) — underrepresented
- **South** (5–10) — growth area
- **Other** (5–10) — balance

### Cause Diversity

Aim for representation across:
- Education
- Health
- Environment
- Community development
- Arts & culture
- Other

## How to Build Your List

### Step 1: Get Contact Information

**Source 1: Your network**
- Existing Daanaa connections
- Advisor recommendations
- Community foundation lists

**Source 2: Public data**
- ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer (ProPublica.org/nonprofits)
- GuideStar (now Candid.org)
- Your state's nonprofit registry

**Source 3: Targeted searches**
- "[City] nonprofits" + website visit (grab email from contact page)
- Foundation directories (DRK grantees, Knight grantees, etc.)

### Step 2: Create Your List

**Spreadsheet template:**
```
Name | Location | Cause | EIN | Website | Email | Contact | Notes
[Org] | [City, ST] | [Cause] | [EIN] | [URL] | [email] | [Name] | [anything useful]
```

**Example rows:**
```

Save the Watershed | Portland, OR | Environment | 91-1234567 | savewatershed.org | contact@savewatershed.org | John Smith | Mid-size, engaged
```

## Feedback Collection Form

**Create a simple Google Form or Airtable form with these questions:**

1. **Claiming process** (1–5 scale)
   - "The claiming process was easy to understand"
   - "Email verification worked smoothly"

2. **Profile display** (1–5 scale)
   - "My profile appears correctly"
   - "The financial context is accurate"
   - "I would want to edit my profile"

3. **Search quality** (yes/no)
   - "I was able to find my organization when searching"
   - "Similar organization suggestions were relevant"

4. **Feature feedback** (open-ended)
   - "What feature would be most useful for nonprofits?"
   - "What concerns do you have about the platform?"

5. **Bug reports** (open-ended)
   - "Any bugs or technical issues you found?"

6. **Would you recommend?** (yes/no)
   - "Would you recommend Daanaa to other nonprofits?"

## What to Do With Feedback (Aug 15–18)

1. **Triage bugs:** Critical (blocks feature) vs Nice-to-have (Phase 2)
2. **Document feedback themes:** "X orgs said they wanted Y"
3. **Send thank-you email:** "Thanks for testing! Here's what we learned..."
4. **Decide which feedback to act on before Aug 15 launch** (usually only critical bugs)

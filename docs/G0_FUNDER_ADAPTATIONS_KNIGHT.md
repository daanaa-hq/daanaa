## The Problem (Knight Angle)

### The Civic Engagement Gap

Informed communities require informed donors. But donor information infrastructure is broken.

**The challenge:** $460B flows annually to 1.9M nonprofits. But most donors:
- Know only the largest organizations (brand halo effect)
- Have no way to discover local, high-impact organizations
- Can't assess financial stability or operational health
- Lack the context to make informed giving decisions

**The result:** Billions concentrate on household names. Local organizations—community health centers, housing nonprofits, food banks—remain invisible despite critical work.

**This is a civic problem:** Strong communities depend on strong local organizations. But visibility is broken. Donors *want* to support local change, but they can't find the organizations doing it.

---

## The Opportunity: Democratic Information Infrastructure for Giving

Knight Foundation funds informed communities. Daanaa is information infrastructure for that.

**What we do:**
- Index 1.97M nonprofits with complete financial data
- Benchmark each organization against its peer group (fair, not top-down)
- Surface local organizations doing critical work
- Help donors understand operations + impact

**Why communities need this:**
- Local organizations are invisible without infrastructure
- Donors want context, not algorithms or rankings
- Trust comes from transparency, not ratings
- Communities are stronger when resources flow to organizations that need them most

**Current reach:**
- 1.97M organizations indexed (1.87M active, deductible 501c3s)
- 537,000 with complete financial history
- Coverage includes small local organizations (not just household names)
- Platform live at daanaa.org

## Alignment with Knight's Mission

**Knight invests in informed and engaged communities.**

Daanaa enables that in three ways:

1. **Transparency as civic infrastructure.** We organize public information (IRS 990 data) in a way communities can use to make informed decisions. No algorithms, no ratings—just facts.

2. **Local visibility.** Our peer-group benchmarking ensures a $200K community food bank is never compared to Kaiser. Local organizations are treated with dignity and shown in their true context.

3. **Donor agency.** We don't tell people where to give. We help them discover organizations and make their own decisions. This respects community intelligence.

---

## The Behavioral Insight: Trust Through Transparency, Not Algorithms

Most giving platforms try to *influence* donors (rankings, recommendations, social proof). Daanaa does the opposite: **we remove friction and trust donors to decide.**

Research shows:
- Donors *want* to support their communities
- They *don't* trust algorithmic rankings
- They *do* trust transparent, factual information
- They *do* want to see peer context (financial stability, operational health)

**Our approach:** Show facts. Provide context. Let donors choose.

Result: Higher-quality giving decisions. Stronger communities. Trust in institutions.

## What We Are NOT

- Not a ratings agency (we don't rank)
- Not a donor surveillance platform (we don't track giving)
- Not a marketplace for donations (we don't process money)
- Not a nonprofit evaluator (we present data, not judgment)

**We are a discovery + context layer.** A utility for informed giving.

## The Business Model: Sustainable, Community-First

We remain free for donors and nonprofits forever. Funded by:

1. **Grants** (like Knight) — Core transparency infrastructure
2. **Partnership revenue** — Community partners offer tools at discounts; we earn referrals
3. **Services** — Group purchasing for nonprofits (reducing costs, freeing resources for mission)

**We will never:** Charge donors, sell nonprofit data, accept paid placement, or compromise independence.

## The Stewardship Model (Why This Matters to Knight)

Daanaa operates under a **Founding Stewardship Commitment** (11 principles):

- **No paid placement** — organizations don't pay to be ranked

**Why Knight cares:** Daanaa won't be corrupted by growth pressure or funding needs to compromise transparency. We're structured for long-term integrity.

# Daanaa Impact Network — Network Partner Program
## Onboarding and Operating Guide

**Operated by EcoMargins Consulting LLC · partners@daanaa.org**

---

## What this guide covers

This guide is for businesses that have completed a Collective Action Framework (CAF) agreement with EcoMargins Consulting LLC and are entering the Daanaa Impact Network as a **Network Partner**. It explains how your listing works, how nonprofit members find and access your benefit, what you need to do to stay in good standing, and how Daanaa supports you.

If you are a **Community Partner** (no formal contract), a shorter onboarding email covers your setup. This guide applies to Network Partners only.

---

## The one-sentence version

You offer nonprofits a real, meaningful discount. They discover you through Daanaa. Daanaa never touches the transaction. Everyone wins.

---

## Part 1 — How the network works

### 1.1 The Impact Network is a trust relationship

Nonprofits find Daanaa because it is independent. They trust it because we have made an unconditional commitment — in our Founding Stewardship Commitment and in your CAF agreement — that vendor participation never influences how any organization is ranked, scored, or displayed. This is non-negotiable.

Your benefit listing exists alongside other partners. Daanaa does not sort, weight, or favor partners based on fee size, category, or any commercial relationship.

### 1.2 Who is eligible to access your benefit

**Impact Network Members** are nonprofit organizations that have a verified claimed page on Daanaa. A claimed organization has provided their name, title, phone number, and EIN, and we have verified by phone that the contact represents the organization.

When a nonprofit accesses your benefit, they will identify themselves as a Daanaa Impact Network Member. The only information Daanaa may provide to you for verification is the organization's name, EIN, and claimed status. We do not share donor data, financial scores, wallet contents, giving history, or any user-level behavioral data with you, ever.

### 1.3 What the benefit listing shows

Your listing at `daanaa.org/guild/[your-slug]` displays:
- Your benefit name and description (from Exhibit A of your CAF)
- How a nonprofit accesses the benefit (code, link, or form)
- Exclusions and expiration, if any
- Contact information for questions

Nonprofits reach your listing from:
- The **Member Benefits** directory (`daanaa.org/member/benefits`)
- Your dedicated guild referral page (`daanaa.org/guild/[your-slug]`)
- Links shared by Daanaa in newsletters, onboarding sequences, or platform announcements

## Part 2 — Your obligations

### 2.1 Keep your listing current

If your benefit changes — pricing, access code, expiration, eligible products, or contact — notify Daanaa within 5 business days at partners@daanaa.org. Listings that become inaccurate without notice are grounds for removal under your CAF agreement.

### 2.2 Honor the benefit without friction

When a verified nonprofit presents their EIN and claimed status, honor the CAF Discount described in Exhibit B without additional barriers, hidden fees, or conditions not disclosed in Exhibit A.

Do not require nonprofits to:
- Sign up for a mailing list as a condition of accessing the benefit
- Provide more personal information than necessary to verify eligibility and fulfill the service
- Complete a sales call before receiving the discount

### 2.3 Data use — hard limits

You may use an Impact Network Member's name and EIN for two purposes only:
1. Verifying eligibility
2. Delivering the service the nonprofit has initiated

You may not:
- Enroll members in marketing, retargeting, or sales sequences without separate, affirmative consent
- Build commercial prospect databases from member participation
- Resell or transfer member information to any third party
- Use EIN-level data to infer donor pool size, financial capacity, or giving patterns

These restrictions survive termination of your CAF agreement.

### 2.4 Quarterly Spend Reports

Submit a Quarterly Spend Report within 30 days of each calendar quarter end. The report should include:
- Number of Impact Network Members served during the quarter
- Aggregate revenue from those members
- Average effective discount applied

Send reports to partners@daanaa.org. Reports are confidential. Daanaa will not disclose your financial data without your written consent.

### 2.5 Milestone tiers

Your CAF Exhibit B defines milestone tiers. As cumulative member adoption grows, the minimum CAF Discount in Exhibit B applies. Daanaa will notify you when a milestone is reached based on Quarterly Spend Reports. Tier upgrades take effect at the start of the following quarter.

You may improve your discount above the milestone floor at any time. You may not reduce below the applicable floor without a written amendment.

## Part 3 — What Daanaa does for you

### 3.1 Your listing on the platform

Daanaa maintains your listing at `daanaa.org/guild/[your-slug]` for the term of your CAF agreement. Your listing is accessible from the Member Benefits directory and from any inbound link you share.

### 3.2 Member introductions

As nonprofits join and claim their pages, they receive onboarding communications pointing them to the Member Benefits directory. You benefit from the network effect as Daanaa's member base grows.

### 3.3 Milestone notifications

Daanaa tracks cumulative member adoption from your Quarterly Spend Reports and notifies you when you cross a milestone tier.

### 3.4 Removal with notice

If Daanaa removes your listing for any reason other than fraud or harm to members, we give you 14 days' prior written notice and an opportunity to cure. Immediate removal is reserved for material misrepresentation, data misuse, or harm.

## Part 4 — How you may represent the relationship

### 4.1 What you may say

During the term of your CAF agreement, you may identify your business as a **"Daanaa Impact Network Partner"** in your own marketing materials. This includes your website, pitch decks, email signatures, and sales materials.

### 4.2 What you may not say

You may not:
- State or imply that Daanaa endorses your products or services
- Suggest that Daanaa has evaluated, vetted, recommended, or certified your offering beyond listing it
- Use "Daanaa" or our logo in a way that could mislead nonprofits into thinking Daanaa is a party to the transaction or has guaranteed your benefit

If you are unsure about specific language, email partners@daanaa.org before publishing.

### 4.3 After termination

Upon expiration or termination of your CAF agreement, remove all "Daanaa Impact Network Partner" references from your materials within 30 days.

## Part 5 — Getting listed

### Step 1 — Exhibit A: complete your benefit description

Fill in the Benefit Description table in Exhibit A of your CAF agreement. The fields are:

| Field | What to provide |
|---|---|
| Benefit name | Short, clear title (e.g., "15% off all annual plans for Daanaa members") |
| Eligible products/services | Specific products or tiers this applies to |
| Exclusions | What is not included |
| Access method | Code / unique link / email / form submission |
| Benefit code or link | The actual code or URL |
| Expiration | Date or "No expiration" |
| Eligibility verification | How a nonprofit confirms they qualify (e.g., "submit EIN at partner.com/daanaa") |
| Contact for questions | Email or phone for nonprofits to reach your team |

### Step 2 — Exhibit B: confirm your milestone tier schedule

Review the milestone tier table in Exhibit B. Confirm the minimum CAF Discount at each tier and the effective date logic. If you want to offer a better rate at launch, note it.

### Step 3 — Send your exhibits to Daanaa

Email your completed Exhibits A and B to partners@daanaa.org. We will review for completeness and accuracy before publishing.

### Step 4 — Review and go live

Daanaa will draft your guild page at `daanaa.org/guild/[your-slug]` and share a preview for your approval. Once you confirm, the page goes live and appears in the Member Benefits directory.

**Timeline:** Allow 5–10 business days from signed CAF + complete exhibits to go-live.

## Part 6 — Keeping it working

### Renewals

Your CAF renews automatically for successive 12-month periods. Either party may decline renewal with 60 days' written notice before the end of any term.

### Updating your benefit

Email partners@daanaa.org with the updated Exhibit A fields. We will update your listing within 5 business days. Material changes that reduce the CAF Discount require a written amendment.

### Questions from nonprofits

Nonprofits may contact your team directly using the contact information in Exhibit A. Daanaa does not mediate disputes between partners and nonprofits — those are directly between the parties.

### Partner contacts at Daanaa

- Partnership inquiries and listing updates: partners@daanaa.org
- Billing and reporting: partners@daanaa.org
- Legal and policy questions: legal@daanaa.org

## Part 7 — The independence guarantee

This is worth repeating explicitly because it is the foundation of the network's credibility.

Your participation in the Impact Network — including any fees you pay to Daanaa — will never affect:

- How any nonprofit appears in Daanaa search results
- Any organization's financial context score
- Any ranking, badge, tier, or trust signal on the platform
- Any editorial or methodology decision

No vendor may pay for placement, boost an organization's score, or suppress a competitor's visibility. This principle is non-waivable and derives directly from Principle 7 of the Daanaa Founding Stewardship Commitment.

Nonprofits trust Daanaa because they know this. Your listing works because they trust Daanaa. The independence guarantee protects both of us.

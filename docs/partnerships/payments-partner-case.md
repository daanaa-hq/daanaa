# Partnership Case: Online Giving for the Invisible 97%

Prepared by Daanaa (daanaa.org) · 2026-06-12 · Contact: partners@daanaa.org

---

## Who we are

Daanaa is a free public directory of every U.S. 501(c)(3), built from IRS and
ProPublica records. 2,064,613 organizations are indexed, each with a page,
a peer financial context score, and a verified claim process: the organization's
leader proves identity by phone and signs federal attestations before taking
control of their page. We are a civic project. We never handle donations, never
charge nonprofits, and never sell placement.

## The problem, in your terms

The nonprofit long tail is unreachable for you economically. Of the 2.06 million
organizations in our registry:

- **1,391 (0.07%)** have a known online giving link
- **116,824 (5.7%)** have any website at all
- Roughly **1.9 million** organizations cannot accept an online donation today

These orgs will never be reached by a sales team. Their individual volume is
small; collectively they are the largest unserved merchant segment in the
country, and the one with the highest trust requirements and the most
expensive onboarding (charity verification, KYC, fraud screening).

## What Daanaa brings

1. **The map.** We know, EIN by EIN, which organizations lack giving
   infrastructure. No one else has this list at full coverage.
2. **The moment.** Organizations meet us when their leader is claiming their
   page: identity verified by phone, role attested under 18 U.S.C. § 1001,
   IRS status confirmed against the registry. When that leader says "yes,
   help me set up online giving," you receive a pre-verified, intent-qualified
   nonprofit. Your charity-verification and fraud costs drop to near zero.
3. **The channel cost.** Our referral costs you nothing. No ad spend, no sales
   motion. We surface your offer inside the claim flow at the exact moment of
   need.

## What we ask

1. **A $0 (or nearest-to-zero) tier for organizations we refer.** Waived
   platform and monthly fees at minimum; covered or donor-covered processing
   where your model allows it. The smallest organizations cannot lose 3% of a
   $40 donation and we will not send them to terms that take it.
2. **A streamlined onboarding path** for Daanaa-verified organizations. We have
   already done identity and IRS-status verification; honor it with a shorter
   application.
3. **Growth support instead of money.** Tell your existing nonprofit customers
   they can claim their Daanaa page. Introduce us to the community foundations
   and nonprofit networks you already work with. We are not asking for a check
   and we do not want one.

## How placement works (read this twice)

Our processor shortlist is an honest comparison, written in plain language,
sorted by what a small organization actually pays. **Placement cannot be
bought. It is earned by terms.** If your offer is the best deal for a small
nonprofit, you top the list, and we will say exactly why. If a competitor
beats your terms, they top the list. This is non-negotiable, and it is also
the reason a partnership with us is worth having: the org's trust in the
recommendation is what makes the referral convert.

## What we will never do

- Handle, hold, or route donor funds (we are a discovery and hand-off layer)
- Sell, rent, or transfer our registry or contact data
- Let any commercial relationship influence scores, rankings, or visibility
- Recommend terms to a nonprofit that we would not accept for our own money

## Proposed pilot (90 days)

- We enable the "set up online giving" step for claimed organizations in one
  metro region.
- You provide the $0/nearest-zero tier and a fast-track onboarding link.
- Target: first 100 referred organizations.
- We jointly measure: completed setups, time-to-first-donation, support load,
  fraud/verification incidents (we expect near zero).
- If it works, we expand region by region, and your brand is part of the story
  we tell community foundations in every one of them.

## The Problem (Omidyar Angle)

### AI Governance in Philanthropy

Artificial intelligence is reshaping institutional decision-making. Philanthropy is next.

**The risk:** As AI enters nonprofit evaluation, discovery, and funding decisions, we're replicating bias at scale without oversight.

**The gap:** Most nonprofit "rating" platforms use proprietary AI models:
- Black-box scoring systems (nonprofits don't understand why they rank where they do)
- Optimization for funder priorities (not nonprofit quality or impact)
- Extractive models (nonprofits have no control over their data)
- Unaccountable algorithms (no way to appeal or correct)

**Where Daanaa fits:** We demonstrate that trustworthy AI in civic infrastructure is possible—and necessary.

## Why Trustworthy AI Matters Here

**The contrast:** Most nonprofit platforms are "AI-driven" black boxes.

**Daanaa:** Transparent, local, auditable, respecting nonprofit autonomy.

### How We Use AI (Responsibly)

**1. Mission Generation (Qwen2.5-32B)**
- Generates 1–2 sentence descriptions from IRS data
- **Reviewed before surfacing** (not auto-published)
- **Fails openly** (shows "description unavailable" if uncertain)
- **Not used for ranking** (only for context)

**2. Financial Benchmarking (Deterministic)**
- Scores from IRS data (revenue, expenses, efficiency ratios)
- **Published methodology** (anyone can reproduce)
- **No black-box ML** (clear peer groups, clear formulas)
- **Auditable** (show your work)

**3. Semantic Search (Embeddings, Local)**
- Helps donors find causes via natural language
- **Local compute** (not cloud API dependent)
- **Transparent ranking** (relevance only, not quality judgment)
- **User controls the query** (they decide what to search)

### What We Don't Do

- ❌ Use proprietary scoring models
- ❌ Optimize AI for funder influence
- ❌ Create black-box recommendations
- ❌ Extract nonprofit data for AI training
- ❌ Make autonomous decisions affecting nonprofits

## The Governance Framework

**Daanaa operates under a Founding Stewardship Commitment** (11 principles, documented in code):

1. **Mission first** — Technology serves transparency, not growth
2. **Privacy by design** — Donor data never exposed, never tracked
3. **Evidence-based trust signals** — Only public data, transparent methodology
4. **Independence protected** — No funder influence on outcomes
5. **AI as tool** — Humans make decisions; AI handles data
6. **Mistakes corrected** — Public Mistake Registry, transparent fixes
7. **Principles gate all decisions** — Before shipping, principles audit

**This is how trustworthy AI governance should work.**

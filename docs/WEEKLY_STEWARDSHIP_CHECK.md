## How to Use This

1. **Copy the template above** into a new file: `docs/STEWARDSHIP_WEEKLY/WEEK_[DATE].md`

2. **Fill it out Friday EOD** (30 min max)
   - Answer each principle check honestly
   - Note any violations or near-misses
   - Plan fixes for next week

3. **Share with partners** (if you have founding partners)
   - Monthly: send summary to partners
   - Let them audit your principles commitment

4. **Update STEWARDSHIP.md** if principles need revision
   - Never dilute principles for convenience
   - Only strengthen them
   - Document changes in the revision log

---

## Red Flags to Watch For (Temptations)

🚩 "Just this once" (sliding away from a principle)  
🚩 "It's for growth" (rationalizing a shortcut)  
🚩 "The partner wants..." (external pressure overriding principles)  
🚩 "No one will notice" (hiding something from transparency)  
🚩 "It's not technically a violation..." (rules lawyering)  
🚩 "Nonprofits asked for it" (confusing user requests with principle checks)  

If you notice yourself thinking these, **stop and discuss with a partner or advisor.**

---

## Archive

After you complete a week's check, save it in `docs/STEWARDSHIP_WEEKLY/WEEK_[DATE].md`. This creates an auditable record of when/how principles held.

Examples:
- `docs/STEWARDSHIP_WEEKLY/WEEK_2026_06_18.md`
- `docs/STEWARDSHIP_WEEKLY/WEEK_2026_06_25.md`
- etc.

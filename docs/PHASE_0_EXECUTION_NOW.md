## How to Make the Calls

### Before Each Call

1. **Read the script** (15 min)
   - FUNDER_CALL_OMIDYAR_SCRIPT_DETAILED.md
   - Know the 3 key points you want to land
   - Have DECISIONS.md + STEWARDSHIP.md handy for Q&A

2. **Check your setup** (5 min)
   - Quiet room, no interruptions
   - Have PARTNER_TRACKER.md open (fill notes live or after)
   - Test audio (if Zoom/Slack)

3. **Send calendar invite** (24h before)
   - "Daanaa Founding Partnership Call with [Name]"
   - 30 minutes
   - Include Zoom link or ask for their preference

### During Call

**Opening (2 min):**
- Thank them for time
- "I wanted to share Daanaa and see if there's a fit for Founding Stewardship partnership"

**Core pitch (10 min):**
- Follow script, but be conversational
- Watch for questions/hesitation (they'll signal what matters to them)
- **Key story:** "97% of nonprofits are invisible. We're building the discovery layer."

**Exploration (12 min):**
- Listen more than talk
- Ask: "What questions do you have?" "Where do you see fit?" "What would partnership look like?"
- Understand their constraints (timeline, budget, governance)

**Close (3 min):**
- "Would you be interested in exploring Founding Partner role?"
- If yes: "Great. I'll send materials today and follow up in 3 days."
- If no: "Understood. Is there someone else at [Foundation] I should talk to?"
- Get commitment for second call (Day 14)

### After Call

**Immediately (same day):**
1. Fill PARTNER_TRACKER.md while fresh:
   - Date, contact name, duration
   - Tone (engaged/curious/skeptical/not now)
   - Key concerns or questions they raised
   - Next steps agreed

2. Send follow-up email (template in PARTNER_TRACKER.md):
   - Thank them

   - Attach: STEWARDSHIP.md + PARTNER_AGREEMENT_SUMMARY.md + BUILD_PRIORITY_ROADMAP.md

## If a Call Goes Sideways

**They say "not interested":**
- Don't push. Thank them. Ask for referral.
- Move on. You need 3 committed, not 10 maybes.

**They ask hard questions:**
- Be honest. If you don't know: "Great question. Let me research that and get back to you."
- Don't BS. Trust > bluster.

**They want equity / control:**
- Use PARTNER_AGREEMENT_SUMMARY.md to explain governance
- Stewardship is advisory, not control (11 principles gate decisions)
- If they want operational control: this isn't the right fit

**Technical questions (how do you score orgs?):**
- Simple answer: "IRS + ProPublica data, peer-relative benchmarking, no AI conclusions."
- If deeper interest: "I have detailed methodology docs. Want to review before our next call?"

Data Scope (Design Decisions)
================================================================================

Years:           2019–2024 only (5-year window, current + 4 years back)
Organizations:  ACTIVE deductibility_status only (excludes dissolved/revoked)
Filtering:       Happens at ingestion (skipped records not stored in DB)

Why These Choices:
- 5-year window provides meaningful trends without stale/obsolete data
- Active-only prevents scoring on closed organizations
- Cleaner database (lower noise for scoring pipeline)

Our Questions for NCCS

1. Why is small-org coverage only 8.2%?
Are organizations under $50K using Form 990-N (simplified filing)? Does 990-N include Part X (balance sheet)? What % of sub-$50K filers use the simplified form?
2. Why do faith communities lag at 15.1%?
Is this a lower Form 990 filing rate, different accounting practices, or something else in NCCS extraction?
3. Where is the liabilities data?
Only 24% of orgs have liabilities despite 24.5% having net assets. Is this a parsing issue or a filing rate issue?

What We're Asking

We need your perspective on whether these gaps are:

  • Fixable: Through outreach, data extraction improvements, or technical corrections
  • Structural: Certain org types or categories simply don't report these fields
  • Partnerships: Joint research on Form 990 filing patterns or targeted outreach to underserved categories

Alternatively: if you know of better data sources for these missing metrics, we can integrate them into our pipeline.

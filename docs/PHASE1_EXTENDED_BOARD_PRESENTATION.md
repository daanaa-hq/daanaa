## THE SOLUTION (Proposed UX/SEO Strategy)

### Step 1: Remove AI Messaging (Clarity)
**From:** "AI-powered nonprofit discovery with credibility signals"  
**To:** "Find nonprofits you trust. See the evidence."

**Why:** Signals ARE the product. AI is how we computed them (internal). Users don't need to know the plumbing.

### Step 2: Optimize for Organic Search (Discovery)
- Every org page ranks for: "{org name} + nonprofit + giving"
- Every cause page ranks for: "{cause} + nonprofits"
- Every location page ranks for: "{city} nonprofits"
- 2.26M org pages = 2.26M search opportunities

**Why:** Users search for nonprofits. Make Daanaa findable. No paid ads = no vendor pressure.

### Step 3: Let Users Migrate Naturally (Growth)
- Google crawls → Daanaa appears in search results
- Users discover via search (not ads, not brand awareness)
- Users compare to GiveWell/Charity Navigator → choose Daanaa (signals + small org fairness)
- Word of mouth: "Found this on Daanaa" (small orgs share)

**Why:** Organic growth is sustainable, cheap, and pure (no manipulation).

## BOARD Q&A PREP

### Legal
**Q: Does search ranking count as "solicitation"?**  
A: No. Daanaa is a discovery directory. Orgs solicit via their own donate links. We're the middle layer (neutral).

**Q: Any regulatory exposure?**  
A: None identified. We rank orgs algorithmically (like Google). No liability for org ratings. No state-by-state solicitation registration needed (we're infrastructure, not the solicitor).

### IRS/Tax
**Q: Does organic growth threaten tax status?**  
A: No. We don't take donations, hold funds, or solicit. Discovery layer status unchanged whether traffic is 1K/mo or 100K/mo.

**Q: Is indexing 2.26M orgs "preferential treatment" of nonprofits?**  
A: No. All orgs indexed equally. No paid preference. Algorithmic ranking only.

### Accounting
**Q: Any revenue model implications?**  
A: No. Organic search is free (zero revenue, zero cost beyond server bandwidth). No new revenue streams. No pricing changes.

### Data Science
**Q: Are signals robust for public indexing?**  
A: Yes. Each signal shows confidence (0-100%) and evidence. Example: "IRS Status: Verified (100%)" is self-explanatory. Confidence prevents misreading.

### IT/Security
**Q: Is indexing 2.26M pages a security risk?**  
A: No. Pages are already public. Indexing doesn't expose new data. Sensitive paths (wallets, donations) excluded. Load capacity tested.

### Education/Donors/DAF/Researchers
**Q: Does this change anything for [your role]?**  
A: No. Discovery improved. Everything else unchanged:
- Donors: Wallet still device-first, giving unchanged
- DAF managers: Can link to Daanaa orgs, no integration needed
- Researchers: Data access unchanged, query capability unchanged
- Education: Research access unchanged, data accuracy maintained

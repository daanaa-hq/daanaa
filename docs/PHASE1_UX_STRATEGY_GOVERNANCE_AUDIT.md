## SPECIFIC BOARD QUESTIONS ANTICIPATED

### Legal
- **Q:** Does organic search count as "solicitation" under state nonprofit solicitation registration rules?
- **A:** No. We're a directory, not soliciting donations. Orgs solicit via their own links (hand-off). Daanaa is neutral discovery layer.

- **Q:** Any regulatory exposure from ranking nonprofits?
- **A:** No. We're not rating/ranking. Signals are informational. Search ranking is algorithmic (Google's, not ours). No liability.

### IRS/Tax
- **Q:** Does organic growth threaten our nonprofit status (if we become one)?
- **A:** No. Discovery layer is neutral. We don't take donations, don't hold funds, don't solicit. Tax status unaffected.

- **Q:** Is indexing 2.26M orgs "promotion" of specific nonprofits?
- **A:** No. All orgs indexed equally. No preference, no paid placement. Algorithmic ranking only.

### Accounting
- **Q:** Any revenue implications?
- **A:** No. Organic search is free. No paid ads, no conversion fees, no partner revenue. Cost is server bandwidth only (already budgeted).

### Data Science
- **Q:** Are signals robust enough for public indexing? Risk of misinterpretation?
- **A:** Yes. Signals show confidence + evidence. Example: "IRS Status: Verified (100% confidence)" is self-explanatory. Confidence scores prevent misreading.

### IT/Security
- **Q:** Is exposing 2.26M org pages a security/privacy risk?
- **A:** No. Pages are already public (org directory). Search indexing doesn't add new exposure. Sensitive paths (wallets, donations) excluded via robots.txt + meta tags.

- **Q:** Any DDoS risk from search crawling?
- **A:** Low. Google crawls at reasonable rate. No spike expected. Load testing can verify (part of Phase 1 QA).

### Education
- **Q:** Does this support nonprofit research?
- **A:** Yes. Researchers get better discovery (2.26M indexed orgs vs 1M). Data accuracy unchanged. No interference with research access.

### Donors/Giving Council

- **A:** No. Giving Wallet unchanged (client-side, not indexed). Discovery improved (easier to find orgs). Donation flow unchanged (hand-off).

### DAF Managers

- **A:** Yes. Organic search is open to all. DAF platforms can link to Daanaa orgs. No integration required, no partnership needed.

- **Q:** Does this compete with DAF discovery tools?
- **A:** No. We're a discovery layer (find orgs). DAF platforms provide giving tools (process donations). Complementary, not competitive.

### Researchers

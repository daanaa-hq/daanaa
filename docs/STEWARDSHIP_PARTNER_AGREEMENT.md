# Founding Partner Agreement — Stewardship Signatory

**Daanaa is seeking founding partners who align with our mission to build evidence-based, privacy-respecting, nonprofit-fair giving infrastructure.**

This document outlines what it means to become a **Stewardship Signatory** and founding partner of Daanaa.

---

## What Signing the Stewardship Commitment Means

By signing Daanaa's **Founding Stewardship Commitment**, you agree to:

### **1. Mission Alignment**
- Giving infrastructure should prioritize informed decision-making over transactional incentives
- Evidence-based trust signals matter more than dark patterns, gamification, or manipulation
- Small organizations deserve fairness and equal dignity alongside large institutions
- Nonprofit independence is non-negotiable

### **2. Governance & Principles**
- Daanaa will never:
  - Sell donor data or expose giving activity publicly
  - Offer paid placement or sponsored results
  - Create systems that shame organizations or weaponize transparency
  - Handle donor funds or become a payment processor
  - Allow partners/vendors to influence nonprofit rankings or visibility

- Daanaa will always:
  - Ground trust signals in verifiable data (IRS 990s, public sources)
  - Correct errors quickly and publicly
  - Explain methodology and limitations honestly
  - Treat donors and nonprofits with respect and privacy

### **3. Your Role as a Founding Partner**

You commit to:

| Commitment | What This Means |
|------------|-----------------|
| **Funding** | $100–250K over 12–18 months to fund the launch and scaling |
| **Advisory** | Participate in quarterly strategy calls; input on key decisions |
| **Credibility** | Your public association validates Daanaa's approach (with mutual agreement on communications) |
| **Amplification** | Help spread the message to nonprofits, donors, and the broader sector |
| **Research** | Participate as a case study if desired (aggregates only; no private data) |

You do NOT:
- Control day-to-day operations or hiring decisions
- Influence individual organization scores or visibility
- Have editorial control over content or messaging
- Get access to donor or nonprofit private data

## The Stewardship Commitment (What Partners Sign)

Partners sign the **Founding Stewardship Commitment** (see STEWARDSHIP.md for full text), which includes 11 principles:

1. **Mission before growth** — Trust signals over growth hacking
2. **Privacy is structural** — No donor tracking, no exposure of giving activity
3. **Trust signals are evidence-based** — Scores from real data, methodology transparent
4. **Small orgs deserve fairness** — Benchmarked against peers, not penalized for scale
5. **Don't weaponize transparency** — Respectful communication, never shame
6. **Mistakes are corrected quickly** — Public corrections, no cover-ups
7. **Independence is protected** — No vendor influence, no paid placement
8. **We don't control donor funds** — Hand-off model, never payment processor
9. **Decisions are explainable** — Documented reasoning, auditable choices
10. **AI is a tool, not authority** — Deterministic scoring, human oversight
11. **Principles strengthen, don't weaken** — Changes documented, re-affirmed

## Decision-Making & Dispute Resolution

### **How Decisions Get Made**
- **Day-to-day operations**: Daanaa team (Akbar + team)
- **Major strategy**: Quarterly partner calls (consensus-seeking)
- **Principle violations**: Escalation process (see below)

### **If There's Disagreement**
If a partner believes Daanaa is violating the Stewardship Commitment:

1. **Raise it directly** (Akbar + team discussion)
2. **30-day discussion period** (good faith resolution)
3. **If unresolved**: Escalation to co-partners (majority opinion)
4. **Last resort**: Public statement of disagreement (with partner approval)

This protects both parties: Daanaa stays true to principles, partners aren't blindsided.

## Questions Partners Often Ask

**Q: Will signing make us responsible for Daanaa's actions?**
A: No. Signing the Stewardship Commitment means you endorse the principles, not that you control operations or are liable for outcomes. Daanaa operates as EcoMargins LLC (with legal oversight). You're endorsing a set of principles, not managing the company.

**Q: Can we publicize the partnership?**
A: Yes, with mutual approval. Daanaa will include you in launch announcements and quarterly impact reports. You can also mention the partnership in your grantmaking and communications.

**Q: What if priorities shift?**
A: This commitment lasts 18 months. If circumstances change (leadership, strategy, funding), we discuss and either adapt together or amicably part ways.

**Q: Do we get access to individual nonprofit data?**
A: No. All data sharing is aggregate (trends, patterns, anonymized insights). Individual nonprofit and donor data is confidential and never shared.

**Q: What happens if Daanaa gets acquired or changes direction?**
A: Partners are notified immediately. Major changes require discussion with co-partners. If Daanaa violates the Stewardship Commitment, partners can publicly disassociate.

## Stewardship Principles Review

**Daanaa operates under 11 founding Stewardship Principles** (see STEWARDSHIP.md):

1. **Mission before growth** — Nonprofit mission is primary; growth/partnerships don't override purpose
2. **Privacy is core** — Donor privacy protected; minimize data collection/retention
3. **Trust signals evidence-based** — Scores/badges from real data only, no unverified outputs
4. **Small orgs deserve fairness** — Systems don't disadvantage smaller nonprofits
5. **Don't weaponize transparency** — Communicate respectfully, no shame language
6. **Mistakes corrected quickly** — Errors fixed promptly and openly
7. **Independence protected** — No partner influence on rankings/scores
8. **Never handle funds** — Daanaa is discovery layer only; money goes direct to orgs
9. **Decisions explainable later** — Document decision rationale, not just outcomes
10. **AI is tool, not replacement** — Human accountability remains; AI outputs reviewable
11. **Principles strengthened, not weakened** — Changes documented, explicit re-sign-off when principles shift

### ⚠️ DECISION 10: Revenue Model (HIGHEST RISK)

**Principles at Risk:** P1 (mission before growth), P7 (independence protected)

| Principle | Risk | Decision | Mitigation | Amendment Needed? |
|-----------|------|----------|-----------|---|
| **P1** | Revenue focus might override mission (especially B2B) | Hybrid (separate B2B arm) | B2B keeps nonprofits as primary; B2B is separate | ⚠️ Clarify |
| **P7** | Corporate B2B customers might influence platform independence | Hybrid (formal firewall between arms) | Structural firewall: separate LLC, no cross-influence | ✅ **YES** |

**Key Risk:** If Daanaa takes B2B ESG revenue from corporates, those corporates might implicitly expect platform influence (e.g., "boost visibility for our matched nonprofits" or "hide nonprofits that compete with us"). This directly violates P7 (independence protected).

**Stewardship Amendment Proposal (if hybrid B2B ESG approved):**

```markdown
### Stewardship Principle 7.1 — B2B Revenue Firewall (NEW)

Corporate revenue streams (B2B ESG, employer-giving platforms, 
corporate foundation partnerships) are structurally separated 
from the platform's nonprofit discovery and ranking system.

**The firewall:**
- B2B revenue arm is legally separate (distinct LLC)
- B2B customers NEVER influence Daanaa platform rankings, 
  visibility, or nonprofit scoring
- Board has explicit veto power over any B2B partnership 
  that might create platform influence
- Annual audit confirms firewall integrity (no customer 
  influence leaked into platform)

**What this means:**
- A corporate partner can use Daanaa for employee giving
- But cannot get their matched nonprofits preferential 
  ranking or visibility on Daanaa platform
- Small nonprofits compete fairly with large nonprofits, 
  regardless of corporate relationships
```

**Amendment Needed?** ✅ **YES, if hybrid B2B ESG approved**

## Amendment Process

**1. Board votes on 15 decisions (primary meeting)**

**2. For each approved decision with amendment:**
   - Draft amendment language (legal team + board chair)
   - Present amendment to board (special meeting or next regular meeting)
   - Board votes on amendment (simple majority, or 2/3 if you define "material change")
   - If approved: update STEWARDSHIP.md + sign-off required from all contributors

**3. Publish updated Stewardship Commitment:**
   - Update STEWARDSHIP.md with new amendments
   - Add to Revision Log (date, author, change)
   - Re-sign-off by all board members + AI agents

**4. Timeline:**
   - Immediately after primary board meeting: draft amendments (5 days)
   - Within 2 weeks: board votes on amendments
   - Within 1 month: implementation begins with amended principles in place

## Principle Integrity Test

**Before approving any decision, ask:**

> Does this decision strengthen, weaken, or maintain Stewardship alignment?

- **Strengthen:** Decision improves trust, fairness, or independence (e.g., formal COI policy)
- **Maintain:** Decision is neutral to principles (e.g., employee communications)
- **Weaken:** Decision violates principles and needs amendment OR rejection (rare; should not happen)

**Board's role:** Ensure no decision weakens principles. If tension exists, either:
1. Modify decision to align better, OR
2. Amend principle to reflect board's evolution in values

**This meeting is an opportunity to test and strengthen Stewardship.**

---

**Final thought:** Stewardship isn't static doctrine. It evolves with the organization. These amendments codify Daanaa's actual values as the platform grows. That's exactly what P11 (principles strengthen, not weaken) demands.

# Public Momentum Strategy: Attract Support Through What We Publish

**Core shift:** Stop thinking "prove ourselves then pitch for money." Start thinking "do work so good funders come to us."

**Why this works:** Funders don't fund pitches. They fund movements. You build a movement by doing great work publicly and letting the sector see it.

---

## The Strategy in One Sentence

**Publish research monthly → attract visibility → organic funder interest → support flows to people doing great work**

Instead of:
- Build → Apply → Hope we get funded

We do:
- Build → Publish → Funders come to us

## Why This Attracts Support Better

### **1. You're Not Asking for Money; You're Showing Results**

**Old:** "We have a plan. Fund us to execute it."
**New:** "Here's what we did. Here's what the sector learned. Want to support this?"

Funders respond to the second one.

### **2. You Filter for Mission-Aligned Support**

When you publish openly:
- Funders who believe in the mission find you
- Funders who want control or influence see you're not for sale
- Right partners self-select

### **3. You Build Credibility Organically**

- Nonprofit media writes about your research
- Regional platforms adopt your model (credibility!)
- Academic institutions validate you (credibility!)
- Nonprofits claim profiles (credibility!)
- Board joins publicly (credibility!)

Credibility comes from **the work being real**, not from a pitch deck.

### **4. You Create Demand-Pull vs. Supply-Push**

**Supply-push:** "Here's our ask" (foundation gets 100 of these per week)
**Demand-pull:** "Here's what we published" → "We want to support this" (foundation gets maybe 1 per year)

Demand-pull gets funded faster and better.

## Why This Is More Mission-Aligned

**Stewardship Principle #1: Mission before growth**

The old approach: Prove ourselves → Get money → Do work (in order)

**This approach:** Do mission → Publish mission → Attract support for mission (work comes first)

You're not trying to get rich. You're doing important work and showing it. Support flows naturally.

**This is how movements get funded:**
- Open source: Linus publishes Linux → companies adopt it → fund development
- Wikipedia: Volunteer editors publish knowledge → readers use it → donations flow
- Mozilla: Developers publish Firefox → users love it → organizations fund it
- Daanaa: You publish research → sector uses it → funders support it

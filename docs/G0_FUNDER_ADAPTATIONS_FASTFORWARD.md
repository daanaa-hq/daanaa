## The Problem (Fast Forward Angle)

### The Nonprofit Tech Adoption Gap

Nonprofits know they need better tools. But discovery is broken.

**The gap:** Most nonprofit tech solutions are built FOR nonprofits (accounting software, CRM, email marketing). But **discovery of which tools exist, which are trustworthy, which match your budget—that's a nonprofit's own problem.**

Meanwhile, nonprofits spend $50B annually on software, much of it overpaying because they don't know better options exist.

**Where Daanaa fits:** We're not a tool. We're the discovery layer that helps nonprofits find organizations doing similar work, understand their financial health, and make faster decisions about where to invest limited resources.

But here's the insight: **when nonprofits understand financial context, they make better tech decisions.** A nonprofit that knows it's in the top 25% for financial health is confident enough to invest in better tools. A nonprofit that sees peer group comparisons realizes "we're overspending on payment processing."

**Daanaa solves an upstream problem: helping nonprofits understand their peer group so they make smarter operational decisions.**

## The Behavioral Insight: Removing Friction to Better Decisions

Most nonprofit tech adoption fails not because tools are bad, but because:

1. **Discovery is hard** — Nonprofits don't know what tools exist or if they're trusted
2. **Trust is uncertain** — Without peer context, they can't tell if a vendor is legit
3. **Isolation** — They don't know if peers face the same problems (payment processing costs, insurance challenges, etc.)

Daanaa removes that friction. By showing peer context + financial health, we help nonprofits:
- Understand their operational position ("We're in the top 25% financially")
- See common problems ("Other food banks report 40% of revenue goes to payment processing")
- Make confident tech decisions ("If [Org] is stable + healthy, their vendor recommendations matter")

**Result:** Nonprofits adopt better tools faster. Investments are smarter.

## The Business Model: Sustainable, Nonprofit-First

We remain free for nonprofits and donors. Funded by:

1. **Grants** (like Fast Forward) — Core platform development
2. **Partnership revenue** — Community partners offer tools at discounts; we earn referrals
3. **Services** — Group purchasing for nonprofits (payment processing, insurance, software)

**We will never:** Charge nonprofits for discovery, sell nonprofit data, or accept paid placement.

## Pro Tips

1. **Submit early, not at deadline.** Avoid submission portal crashes + give staff time to read.

2. **Weekly tracker updates.** Don't let submissions go forgotten; log receipt confirmations immediately.

3. **Ask program officers.** When in doubt, email the program officer to clarify deadline, format, or fit.

4. **Build relationships pre-submission.** If possible, get Akbar an intro call with program officers before formal application.

5. **Prepare for questions.** Funders often ask for clarifications. Have materials ready (budget detail, timeline, team bios).

6. **Celebrate early wins.** First approval momentum makes subsequent funders more interested ("already funded by Fast Forward").

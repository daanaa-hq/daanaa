## Your Key Decisions

### Decision 10: Long-Term Revenue Model ⚠️ **VOTE NEEDED**

**Question:** How does Daanaa sustain itself? Free forever, freemium, B2B ESG, or hybrid?

**Your angle:** Revenue projections, cost structure, cash flow, and runway.

| Model | Year 1 Revenue | Year 1 Cost | Runway | Sustainability |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| **Forever Free** | $0 | $200–400K (ops) | 0–6 months | Fundraising-dependent; risky |
| **Freemium** | $50–150K | $200–400K (ops) | 3–9 months | Better but still grant-heavy |
| **B2B ESG** | $200–500K | $200–400K (ops) | 12+ months | Self-sustaining if 5+ enterprise customers |
| **Hybrid** | $100–300K | $200–400K (ops) | 6–12 months | Balanced; multiple revenue streams |

**Cost Assumptions:**
- **Fixed ops:** 2–3 FTE (founder, engineer, operations), ~$200K annual
- **Infrastructure:** Server, email, analytics, LLM inference, ~$50K annual
- **Legal/compliance:** Tax filing, privacy audit, insurance, ~$30–50K annual
- **Marketing/sales:** Varies by model; B2B ESG model needs sales FTE (~$100K)

**Finance Flags to Raise:**
- Forever free = pure fundraising; we compete with thousands of nonprofits for grants. High risk.
- Freemium: need 1000+ active school/nonprofit users at $50–100/month to hit break-even. Realistic?
- B2B ESG: 5+ enterprise contracts at $40–100K/year reaches sustainability. Feasible but requires sales team.
- Hybrid: diversifies risk. First 6 months tight; break-even Year 2 if B2B ramps.

**Recommendation:** Hybrid (freemium + B2B ESG). Reduces single-revenue-stream risk, reaches sustainability by Year 2.

### Decision 11: Volunteer Liability Structure ⚠️ **VOTE NEEDED**

**Question:** Who is liable if a volunteer causes damage? Nonprofit, Daanaa, or shared?

**Your angle:** Insurance costs, liability exposure, and nonprofit operational burden.

| Option | Nonprofit Liability | Daanaa Liability | Insurance Cost | Risk to Daanaa |
|--------|---|---|---|---|
| **Nonprofit bears all** | ✅ Yes | ❌ No | $0 | Medium (negligent placement claim possible) |
| **Shared liability** | ✅ Primary | ❌ Secondary (via insurance) | $20–30K/yr | Low (insured) |
| **Daanaa assumes** | ❌ No | ✅ Yes | $50–200K/yr | Very High (all claims hit us) |

**Insurance Quotes (estimates):**
- General liability only: $2–5K/year
- General + Professional: $10–15K/year
- Full suite (GL + Pro + Cyber + D&O): $30–50K/year

**Finance Flags to Raise:**
- Option A (nonprofit bears all): cheapest upfront, but risky if volunteer causes significant harm and nonprofit sues us for negligent placement.
- Option B (shared): mid-cost, reasonable risk split. Insurance premium is ~$20–30K/year but manageable.
- Option C (Daanaa assumes): most expensive (~$50–200K/year depending on volunteer volume). Only viable if B2B ESG revenue is strong.

**Recommendation:** Option B (Shared liability). Requires $20–30K insurance, but fair to nonprofits and protects Daanaa. Budget this into Year 1.

### Decision 15: Insurance & Risk Management ⚠️ **VOTE NEEDED**

**Question:** What insurance should we carry? Bare minimum, moderate, comprehensive, or progressive?

**Your angle:** Annual insurance costs and coverage gaps.

| Option | Coverage | Annual Cost | Budget Impact |
|--------|---|---|---|
| **Bare Minimum** | General liability ($1M) | $2–5K | Minimal |
| **Moderate** | General + Professional ($1M each) | $10–15K | Small |
| **Comprehensive** | GL + Pro + Cyber + D&O ($2M + $1M) | $30–50K | Medium |
| **Progressive** | Moderate Y1, Comprehensive Y2 | $10–15K Y1, $30–50K Y2 | Phased |

**Finance Flags to Raise:**
- General liability alone covers accidents; professional liability (errors & omissions) covers claims about our ranking/advice causing harm.
- Cyber/data breach coverage is ~$15–20K/year additional; critical if we hold volunteer + donor data.
- Directors & Officers (D&O) coverage protects board members from personal liability; ~$10K/year.
- Progressive scaling makes sense: start moderate, add comprehensive Y2 once revenue is stable.

**Recommendation:** Option D (Progressive). Year 1: $12–15K (general + professional). Year 2+: add cyber + D&O ($30–35K total).

**Question:** Prioritize student-centric, financial-centric, or hybrid messaging?

**Your angle:** Marketing and communications budget implications.

| Model | Marketing Focus | Budget Impact | Revenue Implication |
|--------|---|---|---|
| **Student-centric** | "Help students volunteer" | Low (organic via schools) | Depends on school adoption |
| **Financial-centric** | "Improve nonprofit financial health" | Medium (needs nonprofit sales) | Higher per nonprofit |

### Decision 9: Donor Account Structure (Adjacent) ⚠️ **ADVISORY**

**Question:** Single profile, Head of Household + co-givers, or household entity account?

**Your angle:** Household giving aggregation, DAF integration, tax receipt efficiency.

| Model | Engineering Cost | Household Coordination | DAF Revenue Impact |
|--------|---|---|---|
| **Single** | $0 | Poor (fragmented) | Lower (separate receipts) |
| **Head of Household + Co-Givers** | $10–20K | Good (aggregated) | Higher (consolidated households) |
| **Household Entity** | $20–30K | Best (unified) | Highest (entity-level) |

**Finance insight:** Head of Household model reduces receipt volume by ~50% (one HoH receipt vs. individual receipts). Household wallet encourages larger giving (family coordinates budget together). 50% fewer receipts = operational savings. Worth the engineering cost ($10–20K).

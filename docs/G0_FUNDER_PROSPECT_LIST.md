## Structural Notes (for attorney @ G1)

**Eligibility pattern:** Most funders accept fiscal sponsorship immediately. Some require nonprofit incorporation within 12–24 months. **Recommend:**

1. **Months 0–6:** Apply through fiscal sponsor (Fast Forward, TECHSoup-class sponsor, or donor-advised fund)
2. **Months 6–12:** Incorporate own 501(c)(3) if you want post-award autonomy OR stay under sponsor long-term (simpler)
3. **Months 12+:** All awards transition to your own EIN if incorporated

**Question for attorney at G1:** Does a fiscal sponsor + LLC structure hurt or help early grant applications? Ask about **qualified fiscal sponsorship agreements** that let you maintain board control.

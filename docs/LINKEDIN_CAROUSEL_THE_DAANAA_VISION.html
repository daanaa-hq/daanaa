PRINCIPLE 1
Make Giving Easy
Giving should be frictionless. No gates, no accounts, no tracking. Just discovery, decision, and action.
When people find an organization worth supporting, they should be able to give in seconds—not minutes.

PRINCIPLE 5
Context, not verdict
Show donors what an organization's finances look like compared to peers in their sector and size.
Don't rank them. Don't shame them. Don't pretend one number tells the whole story.
Context
enables better decisions. Verdicts enable gaming.

PRINCIPLE 7
Independence is protected
No paid placement. No sponsored results. No partner influence over rankings.
Daanaa stays operationally independent from the money. Donors give directly to nonprofits, not through us.
Never
will an organization's score change because they paid a fee

KEY INSIGHT
Transparency is a tool
When you show how something works, people can trust it. When you hide how, they can't.
Our methodology is published. Our scoring is versioned. Our mistakes are corrected publicly.
That's not a liability. That's the whole point.

## The Adaptation Pattern

### 1. **Open with Funder's Language**

**Master narrative says:** "97% of nonprofits are invisible to donors"

**Fast Forward version:** "Nonprofits struggle to make informed tech decisions without peer context"

**Knight version:** "Local organizations lack visibility despite critical work"

**Mozilla version:** "Nonprofit data is fragmented; proprietary AI platforms are filling the gap"

**Your work:** Translate the problem into funder's vocabulary.

---

### 2. **Reframe Our Solution for Their Goal**

**Master narrative says:** "Daanaa is a discovery platform"

**Fast Forward version:** "Daanaa provides financial context so nonprofits make smarter operational decisions"

**Knight version:** "Daanaa is information infrastructure for informed local giving"

**Mozilla version:** "Daanaa demonstrates trustworthy AI + open infrastructure for civic tech"

**Your work:** Show how Daanaa solves THEIR problem, not just the general problem.

---

### 3. **Emphasize the Funder's Values**

**Fast Forward values:** Nonprofit efficiency + tech adoption
→ Emphasize: "Better peer context → better tool decisions → faster adoption"

**Knight values:** Informed communities + local engagement
→ Emphasize: "Transparency + local visibility + human agency"

**Mozilla values:** Open internet + trustworthy AI
→ Emphasize: "Local compute + public methodology + no black boxes"

**Your work:** Research what each funder actually funds (not the marketing).

---

### 4. **Use Specific Examples**

**Master narrative:** Generic ("help donors discover organizations")

**Fast Forward:** "Payment processing fees consume 40% of revenue for food banks. Better peer context helps them negotiate better processors."

**Knight:** "$460B flows annually to 1.9M nonprofits, but most donors only know household names."

**Mozilla:** "Proprietary nonprofit rating platforms use black-box algorithms. Daanaa uses transparent, local, open-source approaches."

**Your work:** Add one concrete example per funder showing how your solution solves THEIR specific problem.

## The Playbook (Step-by-Step)

For each new funder:

1. **Read their current open RFP** — What language do they use? What problems do they highlight?

2. **Identify their funder persona** — Civic tech? Tech-for-good? Nonprofit infrastructure? AI ethics?

3. **Rewrite the problem** — Use their language. Show how Daanaa solves THEIR specific problem.

4. **Emphasize the value** — What does this funder care about? (efficiency, transparency, independence, equity, innovation, etc.)

5. **Add one concrete example** — Make it specific to their sector.

6. **Adapt budget** — Show how funds support their priority (e.g., "AI ethics review" for Schmidt).

7. **Emphasize stewardship principles** — Show how our principles align with their values.

8. **Customize appendices** — What evidence do they care about?
   - Fast Forward: Partner integrations, nonprofit testimonials
   - Knight: Local coverage data, donor diversity
   - Mozilla: Trustworthy AI methodology, local compute specs
   - Omidyar: Ethics framework, governance structure
   - Echoing Green: Founder background, systems change vision

## What NOT to Adapt

**Same across all funders (never change):**
- Core facts (1.97M orgs, 537K complete data, live platform)
- Stewardship commitment (11 principles)
- Revenue model (grants + partnerships + services, never data sales)
- Team + execution (founder + AI agents, lean)

**What TO adapt:**
- Problem framing (their language)
- Solution emphasis (their priorities)
- Examples (their sector)
- Tone (their culture)
- Appendices (their evidence needs)

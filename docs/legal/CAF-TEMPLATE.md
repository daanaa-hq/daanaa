## Recitals

Daanaa operates an independent nonprofit discovery platform serving 1.8 million+ U.S. 501(c)(3) organizations and the donors and community members who support them. Daanaa's mission is to make it easier for people to discover and support mission-driven organizations, particularly those with limited public visibility.

Partner is a business that provides [PRODUCT/SERVICE CATEGORY] and wishes to offer members of the Daanaa Impact Network access to its services at preferential rates, in alignment with Daanaa's mission of supporting the nonprofit sector.

The Parties wish to establish a structured, values-aligned commercial relationship under the terms below.

## 1. Definitions

**"Impact Network Members"** means nonprofit organizations that have a verified claimed page on the Daanaa platform at the time a benefit is accessed.

**"Benefit"** means the discount, service access, pricing tier, or other offering Partner makes available to Impact Network Members under this Agreement, as described in Exhibit A.

**"Milestone Tier"** means the pricing level applicable to a given period, determined by the cumulative member adoption volume set forth in Exhibit B.

**"CAF Discount"** means the preferential pricing Partner commits to offer Impact Network Members, which must be at least [X]% below Partner's standard published pricing for equivalent services.

**"Daanaa Stewardship Commitment"** means the founding principles document published at daanaa.org/stewardship, as updated from time to time.

## 2. Partner Commitments

### 2.1 Benefit Offering
Partner agrees to offer Impact Network Members the Benefit described in Exhibit A throughout the Term of this Agreement. The Benefit must be:

(a) Real and currently available to Impact Network Members upon verification of their claimed status;
(b) Honored without additional barriers, fees, or conditions not disclosed in Exhibit A;
(c) At least as favorable as the CAF Discount specified in Exhibit B;
(d) Updated in Exhibit A within 5 business days if material terms change.

### 2.2 Honest Representation
Partner will not misrepresent the nature, availability, or value of the Benefit. Inflated reference prices, fictitious original pricing, or bait-and-switch practices are grounds for immediate termination under Section 8.2.

### 2.3 Milestone Commitments
Partner commits to maintaining the CAF Discount through each Milestone Tier defined in Exhibit B. As member adoption increases, Partner agrees to either:

(a) Maintain the CAF Discount at the same percentage, or
(b) Improve the CAF Discount in accordance with the milestone schedule in Exhibit B.

Partner may not reduce the CAF Discount below the Milestone Tier floor applicable to the current adoption level.

### 2.4 Reporting
Partner will submit a Quarterly Spend Report to Daanaa within 30 days of each calendar quarter end. Reports must include: number of Impact Network Members served, aggregate revenue generated from those Members, and the average effective discount applied. Daanaa will keep all reporting data confidential and will not disclose vendor-level financials without Partner's written consent.

### 2.5 Data Restrictions
Partner may use Impact Network Member information (organization name and EIN) only to verify eligibility and deliver the Benefit. Partner may not:

(a) Enroll Members in marketing lists without separate, affirmative consent from the Member;
(b) Use Member data to build commercial prospect databases or enrich third-party lists;
(c) Resell or transfer Member information to any third party;
(d) Infer giving capacity, donor pool size, or financial position from a Member's participation.

These obligations survive termination of this Agreement.

### 2.6 Values Alignment
Partner acknowledges the Daanaa Stewardship Commitment and agrees that its participation in the Impact Network will not materially conflict with those principles. Partner will notify Daanaa promptly if it becomes aware of any practice or circumstance that may create such a conflict.

### 2.7 No Charitable Solicitation
Partner is not authorized to solicit charitable contributions on Daanaa's behalf, in Daanaa's name, or in connection with the Impact Network. Partner is not a fundraising agent for Daanaa or for any Impact Network Member.

## 3. Daanaa Commitments

### 3.1 Network Access
Daanaa will make Partner's Benefit visible to verified Impact Network Members through the Daanaa platform during the Term.

### 3.2 Referral Page
Daanaa will provide Partner with a dedicated referral page at daanaa.org/guild/[PARTNER-SLUG] linking to the Benefit and describing Partner's offering to Impact Network Members.

### 3.3 Independence — Absolute Obligation
Daanaa represents and warrants that Partner's participation in the Impact Network, including the payment of any fees or other consideration, does not and will not affect:

(a) How any organization appears in Daanaa search results;
(b) Any organization's financial context score or visibility tier;
(c) Any ranking, badge, or trust signal on the Daanaa platform;
(d) Editorial, scoring, or methodology decisions.

This obligation is non-waivable and is incorporated by reference to Principle 7 of the Daanaa Stewardship Commitment.

### 3.4 Disclosure
Daanaa will disclose to Impact Network Members that vendor relationships contribute to platform sustainability. Daanaa will not represent Partner's participation as an endorsement of any individual organization.

### 3.5 Reasonable Notice
If Daanaa removes Partner's listing under Section 8.1 for reasons other than fraud or harm to Members, Daanaa will provide Partner with 14 days' prior written notice and an opportunity to cure.

## 5. Intellectual Property

### 5.1 License to Partner Name and Marks
Partner grants Daanaa a limited, non-exclusive license to display Partner's name, logo, and Benefit description on the Daanaa platform solely for the purpose of operating the Impact Network during the Term.

### 5.2 License to Daanaa Name and Marks
Daanaa grants Partner a limited, non-exclusive license to identify itself as a "Daanaa Impact Network Partner" in its own marketing materials during the Term. Partner may not use the Daanaa name in any way that implies endorsement of Partner's products or that misrepresents the nature of the relationship.

### 5.3 No Transfer

## 6. Limitation of Liability

### 6.1 Disclaimer
Daanaa provides the Impact Network platform "as is." Daanaa makes no warranty that the platform will be continuously available, error-free, or that any specific number of Members will access Partner's Benefit.

### 6.2 Cap
To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, each Party's total liability to the other under this Agreement is limited to the greater of: (a) fees paid or payable by Partner in the 12 months preceding the claim, or (b) $5,000. For Benefit-Only arrangements with no monetary consideration, Daanaa's liability to Partner is zero.

### 6.3 Exclusion
Neither Party is liable to the other for indirect, incidental, consequential, punitive, or special damages, even if advised of their possibility.

### 6.4 Independent Transactions
Daanaa is not a party to any transaction between Partner and an Impact Network Member. Daanaa is not liable for Partner's service quality, fulfillment, pricing disputes, or any harm arising from those transactions.

## 7. Indemnification

Partner agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Daanaa, its members, managers, officers, employees, and agents from any claim, liability, loss, damage, or expense (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from: (a) Partner's misrepresentation of the Benefit; (b) Partner's failure to fulfill a listed Benefit; (c) Partner's use of Impact Network Member data in violation of Section 2.5; (d) Partner's violation of any applicable law; or (e) any claim by an Impact Network Member arising from a transaction with Partner.

---

## 8. Term and Termination

### 8.1 Term
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for [12/24] months ("Initial Term"), unless earlier terminated. It renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either Party gives 60 days' written notice before the end of any Term period.

### 8.2 Termination for Cause
Daanaa may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if Partner:
(a) Misrepresents the Benefit in any material respect;
(b) Fails to honor the Benefit for verified Impact Network Members;
(c) Uses Impact Network Member data in violation of Section 2.5;
(d) Violates applicable law in connection with its participation;
(e) Acts in material conflict with the Daanaa Stewardship Commitment.

### 8.3 Termination for Convenience
Either Party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon 60 days' written notice to the other Party.

### 8.4 Effect of Termination
Upon termination: (a) Daanaa will remove Partner's listing from the Impact Network; (b) Partner will cease representing itself as a Daanaa Impact Network Partner; (c) obligations in Sections 2.5, 5, 6, and 7 survive termination; (d) fees accrued through the termination date remain due.

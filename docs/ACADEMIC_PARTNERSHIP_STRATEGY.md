# 🎓 Academic Partnership Strategy

**Objective:** Establish peer-reviewed academic credibility for Daanaa governance framework to ensure institutional stewardship and global legitimacy beyond our platform.

**Timeline:** August 2026 – December 2027

---

## Why Academic Partnership Matters for Legacy

❌ **Without academia:** Framework survives on founder heroics. If Daanaa disappears, framework credibility fades.

✅ **With academia:** Framework becomes peer-reviewed, teachable, citeable. It lives in university curricula, research papers, policy documents. Survives founder departure.

## Academic Paper Roadmap

### Paper 1: "Governance as Commons" (PRIMARY)
**Timeline:** August 2026 – April 2027 (9 months)

**What it covers:**
1. Problem statement (AI governance in civic space)
2. Literature review (AI governance, nonprofit autonomy, institutional design)
3. Framework design (11 principles, autonomy matrix, automated gates)
4. Implementation (24h to 8w adoption paths)
5. Effectiveness metrics (how do we measure if governance works?)
6. Replicability data (50+ teams, 5 continents)
7. Implications (governance as commons)

**Structure:**
- Part 1: Framework design & principles
- Part 2: Implementation & adoption
- Part 3: Empirical results (effectiveness, barriers, success factors)
- Part 4: Implications for civic tech & AI governance

**Target venue:** *Nonprofit and Voluntary Sector Quarterly* (reach nonprofit researchers) + ACM FAccT (reach AI governance researchers)

**Estimated length:** 8,000-12,000 words

**Writing timeline:**
- Weeks 1-2: Outline + literature review (Claude + Codex)
- Weeks 3-6: Draft sections 1-4 (design + implementation)
- Weeks 7-9: Add empirical results (effectiveness data)
- Weeks 10-12: Review + revision cycle
- Months 2-3: Peer review + revisions
- Month 4: Publish

---

### Paper 2: "Autonomous Accountability" (AI SAFETY)
**Timeline:** September 2026 – May 2027 (9 months)

**What it covers:**
1. AI autonomy in civic systems (novel problem)
2. Daanaa's approach (distributed autonomy, founder gates)
3. Adversarial testing results (what breaks autonomous governance?)
4. Failure modes (when does autonomy fail?)
5. Recovery procedures (how do we restore governance on failure?)
6. Implications for AI governance

**Target venue:** ACM FAccT, ICWSM, *AI & Society*

**Estimated length:** 7,000-10,000 words

---

### Paper 3: "Trust Signals & Governance" (EMPIRICAL)
**Timeline:** October 2026 – June 2027 (9 months)

**What it covers:**
1. Literature on nonprofit trust signals
2. Hypothesis: Transparent governance increases trust
3. Empirical study: Teams with DECISIONS.md vs. without
4. Donor behavior: Does governance transparency increase donations?
5. Fairness: Do teams using framework make fairer decisions?
6. Implications for nonprofit transparency

**Target venue:** *Journal of Nonprofit Management*, *Nonprofit and Voluntary Sector Quarterly*

**Estimated length:** 6,000-9,000 words

# Volunteer Hours: User Guide

## For Nonprofits: How to Submit Volunteer Hours

### Overview
Daanaa lets you record volunteer hours contributed to your organization. Volunteers can claim their hours, and you can approve or reject the submissions.

### Step-by-step

#### 1. Sign into Your Nonprofit Account
- Go to daanaa.org/nonprofit/my-orgs
- Sign in with your Google account
- Click the organization you want to manage

#### 2. Navigate to Your Dashboard
- On the organization card, click **Dashboard**
- You'll see your organization management page

#### 3. Submit Volunteer Hours
- Look for the **Submit Volunteer Hours** section (coming in Phase 2)
- Fill in:
  - **Volunteer Name** — The person's full name
  - **Volunteer Email** — Their email address (they'll use this to claim)
  - **Hours** — Number of hours volunteered (e.g., 5.5)
  - **Service Date** — When they volunteered (YYYY-MM-DD)
  - **Activity Description** — What they helped with (e.g., "Event setup and registration")
- Click **Submit**

#### 4. Share the Claim Code
- After submission, you'll see a **claim code** (e.g., `VOL-ABC123DEF456`)
- Share this code with the volunteer by email or message
- They'll use it to claim their hours

#### 5. Review & Approve
- Submitted hours appear in your **Pending Approvals** section
- Review volunteer name, hours, and date
- Click **Approve** to confirm, or **Reject** if something is wrong
- Volunteer will get an email confirmation

## For Volunteers: How to Claim Your Hours

### Overview
If your nonprofit submitted your volunteer hours, you can claim them here.

### Step-by-step

#### 1. You'll Receive a Claim Code
- Your nonprofit will email or text you a claim code
- It looks like: `VOL-ABC123DEF456`

#### 2. Go to the Claim Form
- Visit: **daanaa.org/volunteer/submit**
- Or click the link in the email from your nonprofit

#### 3. Enter Your Email
- Paste or type the **claim code** from your nonprofit
- Enter your **email address** (must match what the nonprofit submitted)
- Click **Claim Hours**

#### 4. You're Done!
- You'll see a confirmation message
- Your nonprofit will review and approve your hours
- You'll get an email when they take action

## Troubleshooting

### "No claim code provided"
**Problem:** You tried to visit /volunteer/submit without a code.  
**Solution:** Ask your nonprofit for the claim code. It should look like `VOL-ABC123DEF456`.

### "Invalid claim code"
**Problem:** The code you entered doesn't exist or has already been claimed.  
**Solution:** Double-check the code your nonprofit sent you. Ask them to resubmit if needed.

### "Email does not match"
**Problem:** The email you entered doesn't match what the nonprofit submitted.  
**Solution:** Use the same email address the nonprofit has on file. Contact them if you're unsure.

### "Hours claimed, but I don't see it"
**Problem:** You claimed your hours, but the nonprofit doesn't see it.  
**Solution:** Check your email for a confirmation. Your nonprofit may need to refresh their dashboard. Contact them if it's still missing after 5 minutes.

### "I submitted hours but the volunteer says they didn't receive the code"
**Problem:** Volunteer didn't get the claim code.  
**Solution:** Make sure you shared the exact code shown after submission. The code is only shown once — if they lost it, you'll need to resubmit.

## FAQ

### How long do claimed hours stay in my dashboard?
Pending hours stay visible until you approve or reject them. Approved hours are kept in your history for your records.

### Can I edit hours after submitting?
Not yet — if you need to correct a submission, reject it and resubmit with the correct information.

### Is there a limit to how many hours I can submit at once?
No. You can submit one volunteer or a batch of volunteers in one session.

### What if a volunteer claims hours but I don't recognize them?
You can reject the submission with a note. They'll get an email explaining why. Reach out to them directly if needed.

### Are there tax implications?
Volunteer hours are not donations and don't create tax deductions. Check with your accountant if you have questions about how to report volunteer time.

## Tips & Best Practices

✅ **Do:**
- Keep volunteer email addresses accurate (they'll use this to claim)
- Submit hours within a day or two while details are fresh
- Use clear activity descriptions (helps for your records)
- Approve legitimate submissions promptly (volunteers appreciate quick feedback)

❌ **Don't:**
- Share claim codes publicly (anyone with a code can claim those hours)
- Submit fake volunteer hours (keep your records honest)
- Forget to share the code (volunteers can't claim without it)

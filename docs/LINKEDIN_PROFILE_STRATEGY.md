### 9. Messaging / Voice Guidelines
**Tone:** Investigative journalist (Johnny Harris) + Steve Jobs simplicity

**Rules:**
- ✅ Data-first (show numbers, sources, peer context)
- ✅ Human-centered (story about real orgs, real impact)
- ✅ No shade or shame (Stewardship P5: don't weaponize transparency)
- ✅ Inviting (discovery, exploration, agency)
- ❌ No preaching (avoid moral language)
- ❌ No hype (no "best" or "top" rankings)
- ❌ No jargon (explain terms or avoid them)

**Example good:** "1 out of every 3 nonprofits doing incredible work? You've probably never heard of them. Here's why."  
**Example avoid:** "Most nonprofits are inefficient. Here are the winners."

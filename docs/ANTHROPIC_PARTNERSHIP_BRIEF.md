# Anthropic Partnership Brief

**Why Anthropic is a perfect founding partner (if you have an intro)**

## The Opportunity

Daanaa is literally being built with Claude (the Anthropic AI). This isn't just a platform that uses AI — it's a **case study in responsible AI in production**.

Anthropic's mission is "AI that's safe, steerable, and beneficial." Daanaa demonstrates all three:

- **Safe:** Deterministic scoring (not a black-box neural network); explainable methodology
- **Steerable:** Humans make all decisions; AI is a tool, not authority (Stewardship Principle 10)
- **Beneficial:** Public good infrastructure that serves nonprofits and donors equitably

This is the kind of work Anthropic wants to support and learn from.

---

## Why They'd Partner

### **1. Validates Their Mission**
Anthropic's core thesis: "AI should benefit humanity." Daanaa is evidence that this works in practice.

### **2. Real-World Research**
Daanaa is a production system serving 1.87M organizations. Data on how responsible AI scales = valuable.

### **3. Public Credibility**
Partnership with Daanaa + Stewardship Commitment = "Anthropic funds responsible civic infrastructure."

### **4. Thought Leadership**
Joint papers, case studies, conference talks on "Building Trustworthy AI in Public Goods."

### **5. Unique Positioning**
Anthropic gets to say: "We don't just talk about beneficial AI. We fund it."
